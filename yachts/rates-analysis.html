Catastrophe Aggregation & Reserve Considerations
FactorUnderwriting / Reserving Implication
Hurricane aggregation — CaribbeanLloyd's yacht syndicates must model Caribbean fleet aggregation by marina/anchorage location. Single Cat 5 storm can trigger 200+ claims simultaneously — XL nat cat reinsurance essential
Agreed value inflationPost-COVID rebuild cost escalation means agreed values agreed in 2019-2021 may be materially below current replacement cost. Underwriter faces shortfall on total loss payment; annual AV review is critical
Superyacht single loss sizeA single megayacht total loss (60m+, agreed value £50–200m) can consume several years of underwriting profit for a small syndicate. Co-insurance structure and realistic disaster scenario (RDS) monitoring essential
Sanctions / abandonment tailRussian superyacht seizures create unresolved liability for ongoing maintenance, port dues, and potential wreck removal for detained vessels. IBNR for outstanding sanctions-related exposures remains uncertain
Osmosis / latent defect developmentGRP vessel osmosis is excluded but may be the proximate cause of flood damage claims — causation disputes can extend claims development 18–36 months while surveys are contested
