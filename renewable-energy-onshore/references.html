Known Data Gaps

Sub-class premium volumes GAP

Lloyd's does not consistently disclose premium at this sub-class level; market-size figures are estimates.

Private placement terms GAP

Programme structures, ceding commissions and rates by cedant are confidential; benchmarks here are indicative.

Loss-ratio & margin benchmarks GAP

Loss ratios and margins vary by year, territory and cedant and are rarely published; figures marked EST are directional.

BESS attritional losses by cause GAP

Balance-of-plant, installation and commissioning losses are rarely reported individually; public loss lists are weighted towards large fire events.

2026 softening by sub-class GAP

The published summary of the WTW 2026 review does not split premium movements between wind, solar and BESS or between R2 and R4.
