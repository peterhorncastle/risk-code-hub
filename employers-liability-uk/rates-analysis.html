IBNR & Long-Tail Development Considerations
FactorImpact on EL Reserves
Asbestos / mesothelioma latency20–50 year latency means policies from 1960s–1990s still generating claims; long-tail IBNR is a permanent feature of any W3 portfolio with legacy construction/shipbuilding exposure
Occupational disease IBNRHAVS, respiratory disease, chemical exposure disease claims develop slowly; COSHH breaches may not manifest in claims for 10–20 years; disease IBNR typically 40–60% of total EL reserves
Ogden discount rate uncertainty+0.25% rate is subject to 5-year review — any reduction increases lump sum multipliers significantly; underwriters should sensitivity test reserves at 0% and −0.25% for high-severity long-duration claims
COVID-19 latent claimsLong COVID occupational disease claims (healthcare workers, care home staff) are still developing; causation evidence building; class actions anticipated 2024–2026. Prudent underwriters apply IBNR loading for 2020–2022 years
Mental health claims tailStress/PTSD claims typically notified 2–4 years after the stressful period — workload intensity during COVID means 2022–2025 notifications of 2020–2021 causation are expected. Claim duration also longer for psychiatric injuries
Claims inflationEarnings-based claims (lost wages, care costs) are inflation-sensitive — UK wage inflation 2022–2024 significantly increases long-run income loss projections in serious injury cases
Diffuse Mesothelioma Payment Scheme (DMPS): The DMPS (Mesothelioma Act 2014) provides lump sum payments to mesothelioma victims who cannot trace their employer's liability insurer. Funded by a levy on EL insurers proportional to market share. The scheme paid over £46m in 2022/23. Lloyd's syndicates writing W3 contribute to the levy. The scheme is a direct financial consequence of historic under-documentation of EL policies and underlines the importance of ELTO data submission for all current policies. [I7]
