FactorImpact on Reserves
Long discovery delayACFE median 18-month detection delay means claims may be notified 1–3 years after policy year; IBNR loading essential
Large single-loss tailBEC claims with $10m+ severity develop slowly as recovery efforts, criminal proceedings and coverage disputes extend claims lifecycle to 3–5 years
AI/deepfake uncertaintyNo actuarial history for AI-enabled fraud; prudent underwriters apply additional IBNR for 2022+ policy years pending development data
Subrogation upsideSuccessful BEC recovery through FBI, FinCEN or civil litigation can materially reduce net loss — reserve gross, recognise subrogation separately
