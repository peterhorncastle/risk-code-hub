IBNR & Reserve Development — W4 Specific Considerations
FactorImpact on Reserves
DBA Occupational Disease (burn pit, radiation, chemical)Long latency occupational disease claims (cancer, respiratory, neurological) can emerge 10–25 years post-exposure. Iraq/Afghanistan burn pit IBNR is the most significant undeveloped W4 liability. Actuarial uncertainty is very high.
WHCA Reimbursement TimingUS government WHCA reimbursements for war hazard deaths can take 12–36 months to process through DOL/OWCP. Reserves must be held gross until reimbursement received; subrogation asset recognised separately.
TCN Benefit DisputesDBA benefit calculations for TCN families in low-wage countries can generate protracted disputes with OWCP over wage base, dependency determinations and suitable wage comparators. Claims remain open for 3–7 years while disputes are resolved.
PTSD / Psychological TraumaMental health and PTSD claims from conflict zone workers have long development tails — symptoms emerge months or years post-deployment. W4 policies increasingly face late-notified psychological injury claims from Iraq/Afghanistan-era contractors.
Medical Cost Inflation (medevac)Air ambulance and medical evacuation cost inflation is running at 8–12% per annum est. IBNR must account for medical cost trend; older reserves may be significantly inadequate at current medical costs.
