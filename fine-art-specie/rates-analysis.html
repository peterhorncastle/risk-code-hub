IBNR & Development Considerations
FactorImpact on Reserves
Long recovery process on theft claimsStolen art takes months to years to confirm as unrecoverable; IBNR held pending ALR search and law enforcement investigation. Premature settlement at agreed value risks early closure of recovery efforts.
Conservation cost uncertaintyWhere works are damaged rather than destroyed, the cost of professional conservation can be difficult to estimate at FNOL — conservators' assessments evolve as treatment progresses. Reserve at worst case then reduce as treatment nears completion.
Provenance/title dispute tailTitle disputes (Nazi looting restitution, UNESCO convention claims) can arise years after policy incept. Policies should consider whether coverage responds if a work is restituted out of an insured collection.
Art market valuation volatilityA rapid fall in art market values (as in 1990–1991) can make agreed values appear excessive at renewal; a rapid rise creates underinsurance risk. IBNR for open claims can be affected by market movement during the claims lifecycle.
Catastrophe accumulation (single location)Art fair, museum loan concentration or freeport storage loss could generate multiple simultaneous total losses. Cat XL reinsurance is essential; PML scenarios for single-location concentrations should be modelled annually.
