ILS — Cat Bond & Sidecar Rater

ILS / Alternative Capital — Rater

Two of the core instruments of capital-markets reinsurance. Panel A prices a catastrophe bond from its expected loss and spread; Panel B works through the economics of a collateralised sidecar (a collateralised quota share of a cat book). Inputs update live — indicative for training.

Overview History Timeline Loss Database Underwriting Rates & ROE Risk Mitigation Global Programme References Rater
How it prices A cat bond's investor coupon is the money-market reference rate plus a risk spread; the multiple is the spread divided by the expected loss — the reward per unit of modelled risk. A collateralised sidecar returns the underwriting result on the ceded book plus investment income on the posted collateral. Both are fully collateralised, so the limit equals the capital at risk.
