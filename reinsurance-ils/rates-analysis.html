Module 05 — Spreads & Returns

ILS — Spreads & Returns

ILS returns are driven by the spread over expected loss and the yield on collateral. The multiple is how the market prices risk through the cycle — widening after losses, compressing as capital returns.

The ILS Cycle in Words

Post-loss (2023)

Spreads & multiples widen

A big loss year plus trapped capital leaves investors demanding more spread per unit of expected loss — the best entry point for new ILS capital.

Yield tailwind

Collateral earns more

Higher money-market yields lift the reference return on collateral, so total ILS returns rise even before the risk spread — a structural boost in a high-rate world.

Capital returns (2025–26)

Spreads compress

Record issuance and inflows narrow spreads and multiples from the peak — until the next major event resets them.

Investor Return — What Drives It

Total ILS return is the collateral yield plus the risk spread, less realised losses — a diversifying, cat-driven risk premium. ILLUS
