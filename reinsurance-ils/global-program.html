Module 07 — In the Capital Structure

ILS — In the Capital Structure

ILS is not a separate tower but a source of capital that plugs in throughout the risk-transfer chain — behind reinsurers, alongside them, and directly for cedants.

OverviewHistoryTimelineLoss DatabaseUnderwritingRates & ROERisk MitigationGlobal ProgrammeReferencesRater

Alternative capital does not sit in one place in the programme — it flows in wherever it is efficient. It backs reinsurers through retro and sidecars, competes with them through collateralised reinsurance, and reaches cedants and even governments directly through cat bonds. This is the convergence that ties the whole Reinsurance group together. VER

ILS vs Traditional Reinsurance

Collateral vs balance sheet

ILS is fully collateralised (no credit risk, but capital is tied up); traditional reinsurers offer rated paper backed by a diversified balance sheet.

Multi-year vs annual

Cat bonds lock in multi-year capacity and price; traditional treaties renew annually with the cycle.

Complement, not replacement

Most large programmes blend both — ILS for peak, multi-year and diversifying capacity; traditional reinsurers for flexibility, reinstatement and relationship.

The Placement Workflow

1

Define the risk & trigger

The sponsor sets the layer and chooses an indemnity, industry, parametric or modelled trigger.

2

Model & structure

A modelling firm sets the expected loss; an SPV and collateral trust are established.

3

Market to investors

Arrangers place the notes with ILS funds, pension funds and hedge funds at a spread.

4

Issue & collateralise

Notes are issued; proceeds are held in trust as collateral for the term.

5

Reset & mature

Annual resets hold the risk constant; principal returns at maturity if untriggered.

Case Study — A Sponsor Blending ILS and Traditional Cover

A large primary insurer wants durable, diversified US-wind protection at an efficient price.

The problem

Buying all its peak US-wind cover in the annual traditional market exposes it to price swings and single-year capacity crunches.

The structure

A multi-year indemnity cat bond takes the top, diversifying peak layer; traditional reinsurance and collateralised capacity fill the working layers and provide reinstatement.

The terms

An indemnity trigger to avoid basis risk, annual resets, and a blend of rated and collateralised capacity below the bond.

The result

The insurer locks in multi-year peak capacity at a competitive spread, diversifies its panel, and completes the picture this Reinsurance group has built layer by layer.
