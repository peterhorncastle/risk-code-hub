Facultative — Single-Risk Rater

Facultative reinsures one risk, priced individually. Two calculators: Panel A shares a percentage of a single risk (facultative proportional); Panel B prices a layer on a single risk from its exposure (facultative excess). Inputs update live — indicative for training.

Overview History Timeline Loss Database Underwriting Rates & ROE Risk Mitigation Global Programme References Rater
How it prices Facultative proportional cedes a share of one risk — ceded premium = original premium × cession, net of ceding commission and brokerage. Facultative excess prices a layer on one risk — expected losses to the layer = original premium × expected loss ratio × exposure factor (the share of the risk's expected loss in the layer, read off an exposure curve), then loaded and grossed up for expenses and brokerage.
