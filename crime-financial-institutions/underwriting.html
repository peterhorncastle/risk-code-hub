BB Premium Formula

Base Rate × Limit × Institution Factor × Controls Score × Loss History Loading
+ Social Engineering Sub-Premium (if full-limit SE endorsed)
+ Computer Crime Extension Premium (if SWIFT/crypto scope added)
− Deductible Credit (higher deductible → rate discount)
= Total BB Annual Premium

EST Base rates vary significantly by institution type, asset size, and control quality. Indicative 2024 ranges: retail bank (mid-tier) 0.008–0.025% of insured limit; investment manager 0.012–0.035%; hedge fund 0.020–0.060%; payment processor / fintech 0.025–0.080%. All figures market-sensitive and subject to wide variation.

Key Rating Factors

🏦 Financial Strength & Credit Rating

  • Credit rating from Moody's, S&P, Fitch — proxy for governance quality
  • Capital adequacy (CET1, Tier 1 ratios for banks) — well-capitalised = lower crime risk EST
  • Regulatory standing: PRA/FCA, OCC, FDIC — active regulatory engagement is positive
  • Recent enforcement actions, regulatory fines, consent orders: significant loading
  • Auditor: Big 4 engagement vs. smaller firm — proxy for oversight quality

🔐 Internal Controls Architecture

  • Segregation of duties: front/middle/back office separation (critical for rogue trading risk)
  • Dual authorisation for payments above threshold (mandatory for large transactions)
  • Independent reconciliation of positions vs. third-party confirmations
  • Position limit framework: hard stops, real-time P&L monitoring
  • Internal audit frequency and independence from trading lines
  • Whistleblower programme: anonymous reporting channel quality

💻 IT Security Posture

  • SWIFT CSP attestation status (mandatory for SWIFT member institutions) VER
  • Multi-factor authentication: SWIFT payments, wire transfers, admin access
  • Privileged Access Management (PAM) — controls on admin account access
  • SIEM (Security Information & Event Management) — real-time threat detection
  • Penetration testing frequency; third-party security assessment (SOC 2 Type II)
  • Patch management regime: critical patch SLA (key indicator of cyber hygiene)

👥 Employee Screening & Culture

  • Pre-employment screening: criminal records, credit checks, reference verification
  • Ongoing employee monitoring: unusual access patterns, excessive overtime, no-holiday flags
  • Mandatory holiday policy (classic fidelity control — fraud often discovered during absences)
  • Employee assistance programme (financial stress indicator)
  • Culture of control: tone from top; ethics training frequency
  • Clawback provisions in compensation for material fraud contributors

📧 BEC / Social Engineering Controls

  • Payment instruction change verification: callback protocol on all payment amendment requests
  • Dual authorisation for wire transfers above threshold (e.g., £10,000)
  • DMARC/DKIM/SPF email authentication — prevents domain spoofing
  • Staff BEC training frequency and phishing simulation results
  • Out-of-band verification for large/unusual payments
  • Deepfake awareness: voice/video verification procedures for CFO instructions

📋 Claims History & Loss Profile

  • 3–5 year crime and fidelity loss history (frequency and severity)
  • Nature of prior losses: employee dishonesty (loading), computer crime (neutral/loading), BEC (moderate loading)
  • Post-loss remediation: controls improvements following prior claims
  • Subrogation recovery rate: good recovery = better controls indication
  • Aggregation of smaller losses: high frequency of small employee theft = portfolio quality concern
