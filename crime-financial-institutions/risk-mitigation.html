Module 06 — Risk Mitigation

Internal controls, employee screening, cyber security, regulatory standards and insured guidance for FI crime prevention

Effective FI crime risk mitigation operates across three lines of defence: (1) front-line controls embedded in business processes; (2) independent risk management and compliance oversight; (3) internal audit. Underwriters assess all three lines when pricing BB risks. Strong mitigation frameworks directly reduce premium, improve limit availability, and support faster claims recovery. VER

Internal Controls Framework — Three Lines of Defence

🔀 Segregation of Duties

  • No single employee can initiate AND authorise a payment or trade
  • Front office (trading/origination) separate from middle office (risk) and back office (settlements)
  • IT administration separate from business operations (prevents insider system access)
  • Rogue trader prevention: independent position confirmation vs. counterparties
  • Post-Barings and post-SocGen: absolute requirement for Lloyd's BB underwriters VER

✅ Dual Authorisation / Dual Control

  • Two independent approvers required for payments above defined threshold
  • Threshold typically: £/$10,000 for FI treasury payments (varies by size)
  • No authorisation by the initiating employee — independent approval path
  • Physical dual control: vault access, cash counting, securities handling
  • Both authorisers must not be from same team or reporting line

📊 Reconciliation & Exception Reporting

  • Daily reconciliation of positions vs. prime broker/custodian records
  • Exception reports reviewed by compliance independent of trading desk
  • Hard position limits with automatic stop triggers in trading systems
  • SWIFT payment reconciliation: confirmations matched to instructions within T+1
  • Unexplained cash movements flagged to senior management same day

📅 Mandatory Holiday & Job Rotation

  • Mandatory minimum 2 consecutive weeks' holiday per year for all financial roles
  • Classic fidelity control: ongoing fraud requires daily management; absence exposes it
  • Mandatory holiday absent: loading factor in Lloyd's BB pricing
  • Job rotation for high-risk roles (trading, settlements, treasury) every 2–3 years
  • Temporary replacement during holiday must be from a different team

🔔 Internal Audit & Whistleblowing

  • Independent internal audit — minimum annual review of high-risk processes
  • Anonymous whistleblower hotline (legally required in many jurisdictions post-Dodd-Frank) VER
  • Audit findings reported directly to audit committee; not filtered by management
  • Significant audit exceptions disclosed to BB underwriters (as may be a material change)
  • External audit: Big 4 engagement is a positive underwriting indicator

💰 Compensation & Clawback

  • Bonus clawback provisions for employees involved in fraud or material misstatement
  • Deferred compensation structures reduce incentive for short-term fraud
  • Financial stress monitoring: employee assistance programme; discretionary advances
  • Background checks on promotions to senior roles with elevated access
  • Exit interviews and access revocation checklist on termination (same-day system lockout)

Technology & Cyber Security Controls

SWIFT Customer Security Programme (CSP) — Mandatory VER All SWIFT member institutions must comply with the CSP mandatory controls (16 controls across 3 security objectives: Know Your Environment, Protect Your Environment, Detect and Respond). CSP attestation is submitted annually via the KYC Security Attestation (KYC-SA). Lloyd's BB underwriters now require CSP compliance as a condition of computer crime coverage for SWIFT members. Non-attestation or failed CSP = uninsured SWIFT exposure.

🔐 Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA)

  • MFA mandatory for: all SWIFT payment initiation; wire transfer authorisation; remote access; admin accounts
  • Phishing-resistant MFA preferred (FIDO2/hardware token vs. SMS OTP)
  • MFA bypass procedures must require senior management approval and audit trail
  • CSP Mandatory Control 4: MFA specifically required for SWIFT operator access VER

🛡️ Privileged Access Management (PAM)

  • Privileged accounts (admin, root, service accounts) managed through PAM solution
  • Just-in-time access: privileged access granted only when needed, auto-revoked
  • Session recording for all privileged access — audit trail for forensic investigation
  • Credential vaulting: privileged passwords rotated automatically; not known to users
  • Separation of IT admin from business system access — no "all-powerful" accounts

📡 SIEM & Threat Detection

  • Security Information and Event Management (SIEM): centralised log collection and alert correlation
  • Anomaly detection: unusual payment times, volumes, or destinations flagged automatically
  • SWIFT messaging pattern monitoring: deviations from normal correspondent relationships
  • Insider threat detection: data exfiltration alerts, unusual access patterns
  • 24/7 Security Operations Centre (SOC) for large FIs; outsourced SOC acceptable for smaller

BEC & Social Engineering Controls

The Callback Protocol — Most Effective Single BEC Control EST The single most effective control against BEC fraud is mandatory out-of-band verification via a pre-established callback to a known, independently verified phone number for any payment instruction that involves: (1) changing payee bank details; (2) instructions from a senior executive received only by email; (3) unusual urgency or secrecy. Lloyd's BB underwriters increasingly ask whether a callback protocol exists and is applied consistently for all payment amendments.

📧 Email Authentication (DMARC/DKIM/SPF)

  • DMARC (Domain-based Message Authentication) prevents external parties from spoofing the insured's email domain
  • SPF (Sender Policy Framework): specifies which mail servers may send on behalf of the domain
  • DKIM (DomainKeys Identified Mail): cryptographic signature on outbound emails
  • DMARC policy should be set to "reject" (not just "quarantine") for maximum protection
  • Key underwriting question: "Is DMARC deployed and set to reject?" VER

🎓 Staff Training & Simulation

  • Annual BEC/phishing awareness training — mandatory for all finance and payment staff
  • Quarterly phishing simulations: click rate below 5% is positive underwriting indicator
  • Deepfake awareness training: how to verify video/audio communications for payment instructions
  • CEO/CFO impersonation scenarios: "what would you do if…" tabletop exercises
  • Post-incident near-miss reporting: learning from BEC attempts before loss

📋 Payment Change Verification

  • Supplier/vendor bank detail changes: mandatory callback to registered contact (not the number provided in the change request)
  • New payee authorisation: senior approval for any new beneficiary account
  • Payment amount thresholds: automatic escalation for single large or unusual payments
  • Real-time fraud screening: sanctions, PEP, and fraud database checks on all new payees
  • Confirmation of payee (CoP) service — UK Faster Payments scheme VER

Guidance for Insureds

Before a Claim — Prevention Best Practice

  • Conduct an annual fidelity risk assessment: map all processes where a single employee could cause loss; implement dual controls or compensating controls for each
  • Test your callback protocol: run a BEC simulation — have a member of staff attempt a social engineering attack on your own finance team to test whether the protocol is actually followed
  • Verify SWIFT CSP compliance annually: even if you completed last year's attestation, confirm mandatory controls are still in place after system changes or staff changes
  • Review policy coverage for new risks: if your firm has moved into crypto, launched a new payment product, or adopted AI tools, check whether your BB policy wording covers the new risk profile
  • Maintain an accurate loss log: record all attempted and successful fraud events, including near-misses. This log will be requested at renewal and demonstrates risk management maturity

When a Fraud is Discovered

  • Notify underwriters immediately: BB policies are discovery-basis. Prompt notification is a condition of cover. Delay in notification can prejudice recovery. Notify your broker the same day the fraud is discovered
  • Preserve evidence: do not alter, delete, or reset any system involved in the fraud before forensic imaging. Email servers, SWIFT logs, trading system records — all must be preserved
  • Engage specialist forensic investigators: BB policies typically cover the cost of forensic investigation as a claim expense. Use an approved panel investigator if your policy requires it
  • Report to regulators if required: most FIs are required to report material fraud to their regulator (FCA, OCC, FDIC, MAS) within specified timeframes. Failure to report can trigger enforcement action independent of the insurance claim
  • Initiate recovery action: insurers will assist with subrogation — legal action against the dishonest employee or third-party fraudsters. Cooperate fully and do not independently settle without insurer consent
  • Sue & Labour: take reasonable steps to minimise the loss once discovered — reverse payments where possible, freeze accounts, alert correspondent banks. These costs are claimable as Sue & Labour expenses
