Module 05 — Rates & ROE Analysis

Market cycle, rate index 2010–2024, loss ratio trends and return-on-equity scenarios

The FI crime and fidelity market has experienced significant rate cycles since 2010, driven primarily by major loss events (rogue traders, SWIFT heists, BEC surge) rather than capacity constraints. The market hardened substantially 2019–2022 in response to escalating BEC losses, COVID-era fraud, and social engineering coverage expansion. From 2023, selective softening has been observed as new capacity entered and some sub-classes (employee dishonesty) showed improved loss ratios. Market Cycle — Four Eras EST

2010–2014: Soft Market

Post-GFC: low FI crime frequency, abundant capacity. Social engineering not yet a major driver. Rates flat to modest increases. Loss ratios 55–70%. Competition from US domestic market kept Lloyd's BB rates competitive.

2015–2018: Gradual Hardening

BEC identified 2014; SWIFT heists from 2016. Underwriters begin tightening social engineering terms. Rate increases 5–15% pa. Loss ratios drifting higher 65–85% as BEC losses accumulate. Social engineering sublimits introduced widely.

2019–2022: Hard Market

BEC losses surge; COVID fraud increase; FTX-era crypto crime. Significant rate hardening: +30–80% across FI crime segments over this period. Social engineering sublimits tightened further. New entrants (cyber-adjacent carriers) partly offset withdrawal of some traditional capacity.

2023–2024: Selective Softening

New capacity enters; employee dishonesty frequency moderates. Rates flat to −10% for well-controlled, clean-history FIs. However, social engineering/BEC sublimits remain contested; payment processor and crypto segments still hard. Deepfake fraud creating new pricing uncertainty.

ROE Scenarios — Lloyd's BB FI Crime Book ILLUS

🟢 Bull Case (Favourable)

+14–22%

Loss ratio 50–60%. Low rogue trader activity; BEC controls improving across FIs; no major SWIFT heist or Ponzi collapse. Hardened rates maintained for complex risks. Expense ratio 28–32%. ROE 14–22% on allocated capital.

🔵 Base Case (Expected)

+6–12%

Loss ratio 65–78%. Moderate BEC frequency; one or two significant rogue trader events in global market. Social engineering losses at current pace. Expense ratio 28–32%. ROE 6–12% on allocated capital.

🔴 Bear Case (Adverse)

−15–30%

Loss ratio 110–135%+. Major rogue trader event (≥$2bn), significant SWIFT-style heist, deepfake BEC wave across multiple FIs simultaneously, or major Ponzi collapse triggering mass claims. Rate softening too aggressive prior to event.
