Retrocession — Rates & ROE

Retro is the most cyclical corner of reinsurance: a small, capital-scarce market where a single heavy cat year can double rates — and where ILS capital sets the floor.

OverviewHistoryTimelineLoss DatabaseUnderwritingRates & ROERisk MitigationGlobal ProgrammeReferencesRater

Because retro is a thin, peak-cat market, its rate-on-line swings hard with capital: it spikes after big loss years (2006, 2018–19, 2023) and eases as ILS capital rebuilds. The Retro Cycle in Words

Post-loss (2023)

Rates spike

A heavy cat year plus trapped collateral drains the thin retro market; rates-on-line jump and capacity is scarce — the hardest corner of the whole market.

ILS floor

Capital sets the price

Cat-bond and collateralised capacity provide a growing, price-disciplined floor; the multiple investors demand sets where retro clears.

Capital returns (2025–26)

Rates ease

As ILS capital rebuilds and loss years fade, retro rates soften from the peak — until the next event.
