Retrocession — Retro Rater

Retrocession protects a reinsurer's own book. Two calculators: Panel A prices a retro catastrophe layer on the reinsurer's net accumulation; Panel B prices an industry loss warranty (ILW) — a binary cover that pays if a market-wide industry loss exceeds a trigger. Inputs update live — indicative for training.

How it prices Retro cat XoL is priced like any cat layer — from the modelled expected loss to the layer, loaded and grossed up. An ILW is a binary parametric cover: expected loss = limit × probability the industry loss exceeds the trigger; premium loads that expected loss. The multiple (rate-on-line ÷ probability) is the reward per unit of modelled risk — the number the retro and ILS markets watch.
