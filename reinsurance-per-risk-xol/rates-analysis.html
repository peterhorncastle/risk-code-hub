Per-Risk XoL — Rates & ROE

Per-risk pricing lives on two curves: rate-on-line falls as you go up the tower (higher layers are hit less often), and the exposure curve that tells you how much of each risk sits in a layer.

The Rate-on-Line Ladder in Words

Working layer

High RoL, short payback

Hit most years; priced on burning cost; RoL can be 30–60% with a payback of 2–3 years. Earns a steady margin over frequent losses.

Middle layer

Falling RoL

Hit occasionally; burning cost and exposure rating both matter; RoL falls into the teens as frequency drops.

High layer

Low RoL, exposure-rated

Rarely hit; priced almost entirely on the exposure curve; low single-digit RoL and long payback for genuine tail protection.

Reinsurer Return — What Drives It

Per-risk return is the margin between the technical rate and the layer's actual loss cost, times how much capital the layer's volatility demands. ILLUS
