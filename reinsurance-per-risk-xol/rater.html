Per-Risk XoL — Layer Rater

Price a per-risk excess-of-loss layer by burning cost. Enter the layer, the subject premium and the trended historical loss cost to the layer, then the loadings; the rater returns the technical rate, layer premium, rate-on-line and payback. Inputs update live — indicative for training.

Overview History Timeline Loss Database Underwriting Rates & ROE Risk Mitigation Global Programme References Rater
How it prices Burning cost = trended historical losses to the layer ÷ subject premium. Loaded for risk, then grossed up for expenses and brokerage = the rate on subject premium. Layer premium = rate × subject premium; rate-on-line = premium ÷ limit; payback = 1 ÷ rate-on-line. Exposure rating (an exposure curve) is the cross-check when the history is thin — see the Underwriting module.
