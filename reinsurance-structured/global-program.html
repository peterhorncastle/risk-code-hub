Module 07 — Capital & Programme

Structured / Finite — Capital & Programme

Structured reinsurance is a capital and balance-sheet tool. It sits outside the cat programme — its job is to reshape the liabilities the cedant already holds, not to protect against new events.

OverviewHistoryTimelineCase DatabaseUnderwritingEconomics & ROERisk MitigationCapital & ProgrammeReferencesRater

Where cat and per-risk covers protect the underwriting account against future losses, structured reinsurance works on the balance sheet — the reserves and capital already on the books. It is chosen as part of capital management and legacy strategy, not the annual reinsurance renewal. VER

What Each Tool Achieves

ToolBalance-sheet effect
Loss portfolio transferRemoves a block of reserves (and its risk) for a cash premium — frees capital, achieves exit
Adverse development coverCaps reserve deterioration above the carried level — protects capital without removing the reserves
Structured quota shareCedes a share of ongoing business with financing features — relieves capital on live books
Spread-loss / aggregateSmooths a volatile result across years via an experience account
Part VII / legal transferAchieves legal finality — the liability leaves the cedant entirely

Where It Fits in Strategy

Capital management

Removing or capping a liability reduces required capital under Solvency II / rating models, freeing it for growth or return to shareholders.

Legacy & M&A

LPTs clean up a target's old reserves before or after a deal, and let insurers exit discontinued lines.

Earnings & volatility

ADCs and spread-loss covers protect reported earnings from reserve shocks — legitimately, when real risk transfers.

The Transaction Workflow

1

Identify the liability

A legacy book, old accident years or a volatile reserve line is selected for treatment.

2

Reserve & structure

Independent reserving sets the estimate and payout pattern; LPT vs ADC vs transfer is chosen.

3

Price & test

Discounting and margins set the premium; the risk-transfer test is documented.

4

Approve & document

Auditors, regulators and (for Part VII) the court approve; finality and collateral terms are set.

5

Run off / commute

The acquirer manages the run-off and may commute to close the deal.

Case Study — Exiting a Discontinued Casualty Book

An insurer has £100m of reserves on a discontinued long-tail casualty line and wants the capital and management attention back.

The problem

The old reserves tie up capital, carry deterioration risk and distract management from live business.

The structure

An LPT transfers the £100m of reserves to a legacy specialist for a discounted premium, optionally with a Part VII transfer for legal finality.

The terms

Independent reserving, a documented risk-transfer test, an aggregate limit and collateral through the run-off.

The result

The insurer frees capital, removes the deterioration risk and exits the line; the acquirer earns the investment income and margins for taking on the run-off.
