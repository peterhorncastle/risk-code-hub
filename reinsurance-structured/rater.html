Structured / Finite — Rater

Two of the workhorses of the legacy market. Panel A prices a loss portfolio transfer (LPT) — the present value plus margins a reinsurer charges to take over a block of reserves. Panel B prices an adverse development cover (ADC) — a layer protecting reserves above the carried level. Inputs update live — indicative for training.

Overview History Timeline Loss Database Underwriting Rates & ROE Risk Mitigation Global Programme References Rater
How it prices An LPT premium is the present value of the transferred reserves (the reinsurer keeps the investment income) plus a risk margin for reserve uncertainty and a profit margin — capped by an aggregate limit. An ADC prices the reserve deterioration in a layer above the carried level, modelling the ultimate reserve outcome as a normal distribution and integrating the layer — a teaching approximation to a skewed reserve run-off.
