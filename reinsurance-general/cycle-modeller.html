Cycle engine — rate adequacy

The combined ratio is built from rate adequacy. At equilibrium nominal rates track loss-cost inflation (flat COR); rates soften below inflation only when over-capitalised, and snap up hard once margins erode or a cat destroys capital. Each lever does one job — no double-counting. VER

Catastrophe overlay

A major cat is the classic cycle trigger. Losses develop 60% in the event year, 25% next, 15% the third. Severity: +5pp mild · +10–15pp Helene/Milton · +25–40pp Andrew / 9-11 / Katrina. VER

Required capital (SCR)

Solvency II sets the SCR at the 99.5% one-year VaR (a 1-in-200 year). Coverage = equity ÷ SCR; supervisors act below ~130%. Premium-risk basis scales with premium; exposure/cat basis is held fixed. VER

Coverage where discipline holds (feeds the engine)

Reserving prudence

A constant stance adds pp to booked LR, building a cushion (over-reserve) or deficit that unwinds through the P&L as prior-year development. Over a cycle additions ≈ releases; the path is the management lever. VER

−5 aggressive · 0 neutral · +5 conservative

Calibration caveat

Rate change is modelled from rate adequacy, excess-capital softening and underwriting-loss hardening — a stylised feedback loop, damped and clamped, not a market simulator. Real cycles are bumpier (COVID-style shocks, capital-market swings). Investment yield is held constant. Required capital is a simplified factor/exposure proxy for the SCR. Anchor presets are ~5-year reported averages. For real capital planning use the firm's internal model.
