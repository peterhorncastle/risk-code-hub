Landmark Events Anchoring This Hub

  • VER
    1906 San Francisco earthquake
    First modern global catastrophe test; established the value of diversification and London's claims-paying ethos.
  • VER
    1992 Hurricane Andrew
    Launched catastrophe modelling and the Bermuda "Class of 1993"; the defining modern reinsurance event.
  • VER
    2001 September 11 attacks
    The archetypal multi-line clash loss; drove terror pools and the "Class of 2001".
  • VER
    2005 Hurricane Katrina
    Birthed the sidecar and accelerated the ILS market.
  • VER
    2022 Hurricane Ian → 2023 hard market
    Triggered the sharpest property-cat repricing in a generation.
  • VER
    2025–26 record capital & softening
    $648bn capital, $61.3bn cat bonds, and a ~12% property-cat RoL decline at January 2026.

Known Data Gaps

Exact broker index values GAP

Proprietary Rate-on-Line index levels sit with the brokers; the chart reconstructs the published shape, not exact values.

Precise ILS / traditional capital split GAP

The alternative-capital figure is derived and varies by source definition of "dedicated" capital.

Segment-level premium volumes GAP

Public reinsurance premium totals differ by methodology; line-of-business splits are not consistently disclosed.

Private-treaty terms GAP

Ceding commissions, attachment points and reinstatement terms are confidential; benchmarks here are indicative.

Live counterparty ratings GAP

Reinsurer security ratings change; always check the current rating before relying on any figure here.
