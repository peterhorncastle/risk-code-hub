Reinsurance — Layer Pricing Rater

A bespoke reinsurance pricing tool. Unlike the primary-line raters (rate-on-value), this uses layer-pricing models: proportional cession economics for quota share, burning-cost / rate-on-line pricing for excess-of-loss, and expected-loss / multiple pricing for catastrophe bonds. Inputs update the results live.

Three models, one tool The Proportional panel prices a quota share by cession economics; the Excess of Loss panel prices a catastrophe or per-risk layer by expected loss, risk load and rate-on-line; the Catastrophe Bond panel prices ILS by expected loss and multiple. All figures are indicative for training.
How a catastrophe bond prices An investor buys the bond, posts collateral (which earns a money-market reference rate), and receives a coupon of reference + spread. If a covered peril event breaches the trigger, principal is written down to pay the sponsor. The investor's return is therefore coupon − loss. The key ILS pricing metric is the multiple = spread ÷ expected loss; low-expected-loss (remote) bonds trade at higher multiples. Triggers can be indemnity (pays the sponsor's actual loss), industry-loss index (pays on a market index such as PCS/PERILS), or parametric (pays on measured physical parameters) — the last two introduce basis risk. Defaults below mirror a real deal (Beazley PoleStar Re 2024-3: ~1.2% attachment probability, ~0.93% expected loss, 10.5% spread).
