Citation Confidence Framework

All content in this hub uses a four-tier confidence badge system applied at source level. This page provides the full confidence audit, source categories, and identified data gaps.

VER  Verified — sourced from court records, regulatory filings, government data, or widely reported public settlements with consistent figures across multiple sources.
EST  Estimated — industry estimates, broker market surveys, or analyst reports that represent the author's reasonable best estimate but are not independently verifiable.
ILLUS  Illustrative — composite examples or worked examples constructed to demonstrate principles; not drawn from a specific real case.
GAP  Data gap — the relevant information does not exist publicly or is commercially sensitive; the gap is flagged rather than estimated.

Landmark Cases & Settlements

Six events that shaped US GL coverage, loss reserves, and underwriting practice. VER

1944
Escola v. Coca-Cola Bottling Co. — California Supreme Court

Waitress injured by exploding bottle. Justice Traynor's concurrence established the rationale for strict products liability — that manufacturers should bear the cost of injuries from defective products regardless of negligence. Became the intellectual foundation for the 1963 Greenman v. Yuba Power Products decision that formally adopted strict liability in California, later spreading nationally.

Insurance Significance: Created the products-completed operations hazard (PCOH) as a distinct and separately priced GL coverage element. All modern CGL forms include PCOH as Coverage A.
1982
Johns-Manville Bankruptcy — Asbestos Trust Established

Johns-Manville, the largest US asbestos manufacturer, filed Chapter 11 in 1982 to deal with approximately 16,500 pending asbestos claims. The Manville Personal Injury Settlement Trust, established in 1988, became the template for mass tort bankruptcy resolution and set the precedent for using Chapter 11 to manage mass tort liability — the same mechanism used by BSA (2020), J&J (2021), and Purdue Pharma (2021) decades later.

Insurance Significance: Triggered near-collapse of Lloyd's of London via asbestos/pollution/health (APH) spiral in late 1980s–early 1990s. Led to absolute asbestos exclusions, occurrence-basis reform, and Lloyd's Equitas restructuring.
1998
Master Settlement Agreement (MSA) — Tobacco Industry

46 US states settled with the four major tobacco companies (Philip Morris, RJ Reynolds, Brown & Williamson, Lorillard) for $246bn payable over 25 years. The MSA was the largest civil settlement in US history at the time. It followed decades of tobacco companies successfully defending individual lawsuits by arguing plaintiffs assumed the risk of smoking.

Insurance Significance: Demonstrated that mass product liability can overcome decades of individual case defences through coordinated state AG action. Template for opioid MDL 2804 strategy. Absolute tobacco exclusion became universal in all GL policies.
2018
Johnson v. Monsanto — First Roundup Nuclear Verdict

Dewayne "Lee" Johnson, a school groundskeeper, won $289m against Monsanto for Non-Hodgkin's lymphoma allegedly caused by Roundup/glyphosate. While reduced to $78m on appeal, the verdict opened floodgates for approximately 125,000 similar claims, eventually settled for $10.9bn in 2020. Internal Monsanto "Monsanto Papers" showing attempts to suppress scientific research were central to the punitive award and made available to all plaintiff counsel in subsequent litigation.

Insurance Significance: Demonstrated nuclear verdict model for agricultural chemicals. Showed that internal corporate communications on product safety become the plaintiff bar's most powerful tool in punitive damages arguments. Triggered review of all chemical manufacturer GL products provisions.
2021
Boy Scouts of America — $2.46bn SAM Settlement

Approximately 82,000 survivors of sexual abuse by BSA troop leaders filed claims in BSA's Chapter 11 proceeding, making it the largest sex abuse bankruptcy in US history. The settlement, approved in 2023, required complex insurance archaeology across 80+ years of occurrence policies from dozens of carriers including Hartford, Century Indemnity, AIG, Chubb, and London Market. Coverage disputes over intentional acts, expected/intended exclusions, and policy language were litigated across multiple jurisdictions simultaneously.

Insurance Significance: Confirmed that occurrence-basis GL policies from 1940s–1990s remain active, live exposures. Lookback window legislation in 20+ states reopened historical SAM claims. SAM now effectively requires standalone coverage or absolute exclusion in all new GL placements.
2023
3M AFFF PFAS Settlement — $10.3bn

3M settled claims from US public water suppliers for contamination of drinking water with PFAS from AFFF (aqueous film-forming foam) used at airports and military installations. The settlement covered approximately 300 public water systems. 3M separately agreed to stop manufacturing PFAS products by end of 2025. DuPont/Chemours settled separately for $1.185bn. Total industry PFAS liability estimated to exceed $40bn across all manufacturers and users.

Insurance Significance: Largest single environmental/GL settlement in US history. Triggered mandatory PFAS exclusion across entire Lloyd's GL market from 2021. EPA 2024 PFAS drinking water rule creates ongoing litigation pipeline. PFAS now treated as an excluded uninsurable risk equivalent to asbestos in GL policies.

Identified Data Gaps

The following information would materially improve the accuracy of this hub but is not publicly available or commercially sensitive. GAP

Lloyd's US GL GWP by Sub-CodeThe split of Lloyd's GWP between UA (direct primary), UC (umbrella/excess), UR (reinsurance), and US (surplus lines) is not disclosed in Lloyd's public reporting. Total US casualty is reported but sub-code disaggregation is proprietary.
Nuclear Verdict Frequency & Quantum by State (Insured)While plaintiff verdict databases (VerdictSearch, Westlaw) record verdicts, the proportion of nuclear verdicts that survive appeal, settle pre-trial, or are actually paid within policy limits is not systematically tracked. True insured loss from nuclear verdicts is difficult to isolate.
Social Inflation Attribution by CauseThe relative contribution of litigation funding vs. plaintiff bar strategy vs. juror attitudes vs. attorney anchoring to observed social inflation is not empirically established. All published estimates are model-based.
PFAS Historical GL Policy Exposure (Lloyd's)The total aggregate PFAS exposure embedded in Lloyd's historical occurrence-basis US GL policies (pre-2021 exclusion) has not been publicly disclosed. The reserve development risk from historical PFAS occurrence policies is a material IBNR uncertainty.
SAM Lookback Window Total Exposure (Lloyd's Historical)The aggregate exposure of historical Lloyd's occurrence-basis GL policies to SAM lookback window claims across all active state lookback windows is not publicly quantified. This represents a potentially significant IBNR tail.
