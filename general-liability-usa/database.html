US GL Major Loss Database

⚠ Social Inflation Warning: US GL loss costs have escalated materially since 2017 driven by nuclear verdicts (jury awards >$10m), litigation funding, and plaintiff bar strategies. Many "moderate" losses pre-2015 would be reclassified as major under today's tort environment. All loss figures are at reported/paid amounts and may understate economic impact due to defence costs and IBNR development.

1985–2002 Asbestos — Manville Trust & Industry-Wide Reserve PROD CAT >$100bn (industry) UA / US VER
CauseMesothelioma and asbestosis claims against asbestos manufacturers and installers. Johns-Manville filed for bankruptcy 1982; Manville Personal Injury Settlement Trust established.
Coverage TriggerProducts-completed operations hazard (PCOH), Coverage A — BI. Long-tail occupational disease development over 40+ years.
Market ImpactLargest single insurance loss in history at time of settlement. Triggered Lloyd's near-collapse in 1988–1992 spiral. Led to absolute asbestos exclusions in all new GL policies from c.1986.
Underwriting LessonOccurrence-basis long-tail accumulation; invisible at policy inception. Demand for absolute exclusions and prior knowledge conditions now standard.
1998 Tobacco — Master Settlement Agreement (MSA) PROD CAT $246bn (25-year payout) UA / US VER
CauseState AGs brought suit against tobacco manufacturers for Medicaid reimbursement of smoking-related illness costs. 46-state MSA signed November 1998.
Coverage TriggerProducts liability, Coverage A — BI (disease). Most tobacco company GL policies excluded or litigated extensively over coverage.
Market ImpactLargest civil litigation settlement in US history. Demonstrated the scale of product liability exposure for consumer goods with addictive or latent harm properties.
Underwriting LessonAbsolute tobacco exclusion now standard. Reinforced need for mass tort exposure screening in PCOH rating.
2023 3M — AFFF PFAS Water Supply Settlement PFAS CAT $10.3bn UA / US VER
CausePFAS contamination of public water supplies from AFFF (aqueous film-forming foam) used at airports and military bases. 3M settled with US public water suppliers.
Coverage TriggerProducts liability / property damage (Coverage A & B). Environmental contamination — many policies disputed due to pollution exclusions.
Market ImpactLargest PFAS settlement to date. Industry-wide PFAS exclusions now applied across GL market. Major IBNR reserve development for US E&S carriers.
Underwriting LessonPFAS/forever chemicals now standalone exclusion in most Lloyd's GL slips. Exposure screening requires supply chain and manufacturing process review.
2023 DuPont / Chemours — PFAS Personal Injury Settlement PFAS CAT $1.185bn UA / US VER
CausePFAS/PFOA contamination near Washington Works, WV plant. Personal injury claims for kidney cancer, thyroid disease, and other illnesses linked to C8/PFOA exposure.
Coverage TriggerProducts/pollution — BI. Extensive coverage litigation; many insurers relied on pollution exclusions.
Market ImpactConfirmed plaintiff ability to connect PFAS to specific disease causation. Opened litigation template for further PFAS BI claims against other manufacturers.
Underwriting LessonChemical manufacturers with PFAS in supply chain require specialist environmental GL endorsement or separate placement.
2021 Opioids — National Prescription Opiate Litigation (MDL 2804) PROD CAT >$50bn (multi-defendant) UA / US VER
CauseMulti-state litigation against pharma manufacturers, distributors and pharmacies for the opioid epidemic. Johnson & Johnson ($5bn), McKesson/Cardinal/AmerisourceBergen ($21bn), Walgreens ($5.7bn).
Coverage TriggerProducts liability, Coverage A — BI (addiction/death). Significant GL coverage litigation on occurrence vs. claims-made trigger and "expected or intended" exclusions.
Market ImpactCreated new category of mass tort exposure for pharma distributors. Drove pharmacy GL rates up 200–400%. Many insurers withdrew from pharma distribution GL entirely.
Underwriting LessonOpioid/controlled substances exclusions now standard for US GL. Due diligence required on pharma clients' supply chain role.
2020 Bayer/Monsanto — Roundup Glyphosate Litigation PROD CAT $10.9bn (settlement fund) UA / US VER
CauseNon-Hodgkin's lymphoma claims against Monsanto/Bayer alleging glyphosate herbicide causes cancer. Bayer settled approximately 100,000 claims for $10.9bn in 2020.
Coverage TriggerProducts liability — BI. Coverage disputed on "known loss" and "expected or intended" grounds. IARC Group 2A carcinogen classification central to plaintiff case.
Market ImpactDemonstrated litigation funding power; single case verdict $289m (reduced on appeal). Triggered broad re-evaluation of agricultural chemical GL pricing.
Underwriting LessonHerbicide/pesticide manufacturer GL now high-hazard classification. Prior cancer studies and IARC status reviewed at underwriting.
2019–2023 Boy Scouts of America — Sexual Abuse Claims SAM CAT $2.46bn UA / US VER
CauseApproximately 82,000 sexual abuse claims filed in BSA Chapter 11 bankruptcy proceeding. Historical abuse by troop leaders dating back to 1940s–1990s.
Coverage TriggerGL — personal injury/BI (SAM). Complex coverage litigation across dozens of policy years and insurers including Hartford, Century, AIG, Chubb.
Market ImpactLargest SAM settlement in history. Triggered statutory lookback window proliferation across US states. SAM GL now typically excluded or written as separate policy.
Underwriting LessonHistorical SAM occurrence policies remain active long-tail exposure. Exclusions alone insufficient for youth-serving organisations without separate stand-alone SAM policy.
2023 Catholic Diocese Nationwide — Historic Abuse Settlements SAM CAT >$4bn (cumulative) UA / US EST
CauseClergy sexual abuse claims against Catholic dioceses. State lookback windows (NY CVAA 2019, NJ 2019, CA AB-218 2019) reopened historically time-barred claims.
Coverage TriggerOccurrence GL policies from 1950s–1990s. Insurers including London Market historically wrote long-tail GL for religious institutions.
Market ImpactMultiple dioceses filed for bankruptcy including Los Angeles, Baltimore, San Francisco. Religious institution GL one of most difficult classes to place on London Market.
Underwriting LessonLookback windows a permanent feature of US tort landscape. Religious, educational, and youth-serving organisations require comprehensive SAM coverage review.
2022 Astroworld Festival — Crowd Crush (Travis Scott / Live Nation) BI MAJ ~$750m (est. settlement fund) UA / UC EST
Cause10 deaths and hundreds of injuries at Houston music festival, November 2021. Crowd crush during Travis Scott performance. Approximately 4,800 plaintiffs filed claims.
Coverage TriggerOperations GL — BI (Coverage A). Event organiser, venue operator, and artist all named. Live Nation/Scoremore settled majority of claims by 2023.
Market ImpactLarge-scale event GL re-priced significantly. Crowd management and security standards now key underwriting considerations for events >10,000 attendees.
Underwriting LessonCrowd dynamics and emergency egress plans required for major event GL. Contractual indemnities between organiser/venue/promoter critical to allocation.
2020 3M — Combat Arms Earplugs — Military Hearing Loss MDL PROD MAJ $6bn (settlement) UA / US VER
CauseDual-sided earplugs supplied to US military 2003–2015 alleged to be defective — could loosen without user awareness, causing hearing loss and tinnitus. Largest MDL in US history (230,000+ claimants).
Coverage TriggerProducts liability — BI. 3M settled for $6bn in August 2023 avoiding bankruptcy filing. Government contractor defence partially successful for some claims.
Market ImpactRecord MDL volume. Demonstrated plaintiff bar ability to manage massive multi-claimant product liability litigation at scale.
Underwriting LessonMilitary/government contractor products require specialist GL with government contract exclusion carve-back analysis.
2019 Johnson & Johnson — Baby Powder / Talc Litigation PROD MAJ $8.9bn (proposed settlement) UA / US VER
CauseClaims that asbestos-contaminated talc in J&J Baby Powder caused ovarian cancer and mesothelioma. Approximately 60,000 claimants. J&J used divisive merger (Texas two-step) to create LTL Management LLC for bankruptcy resolution.
Coverage TriggerProducts — BI. Coverage disputed across decades of occurrence policies. J&J Texas two-step bankruptcy strategy ultimately rejected by courts.
Market ImpactLargest consumer products liability case since tobacco. Established limits on "Texas two-step" divisive merger strategy as coverage/mass tort bypass tool.
Underwriting LessonTalc/mineral-derived products require enhanced due diligence on asbestos contamination testing history.
2023 Nuclear Verdict — Georgia Truck Accident (Winn-Dixie Driver) BI MAJ $1.7bn (punitive verdict) UA / UC VER
CauseFatal truck accident. Jury awarded $1.7bn including $1.5bn in punitive damages against Winn-Dixie/Southeastern Grocers. One of the largest single-plaintiff verdicts in US history.
Coverage TriggerCommercial auto/GL — BI. Punitive damages may not be covered in all states. Umbrella/excess layers triggered well beyond primary limits.
Market ImpactEmblematic of nuclear verdict trend. Trucking/fleet GL rates increased 50–150% in Georgia, Florida, Texas, California over 2019–2023.
Underwriting LessonPunitive exposure screening for fleet operators. State venue/jurisdiction analysis critical — Georgia, Florida, Texas highest verdict states.
2021 Nuclear Verdict — Florida Nursing Home Negligence BI MAJ $256m verdict UA / UC VER
CauseNursing home negligence / elder abuse claim in Florida. Jury awarded $256m reflecting punitive component for institutional recklessness.
Coverage TriggerOperations GL — BI. Long-term care/senior living GL one of hardest classes to place post-2018 in Florida due to nuclear verdict history.
Market ImpactFlorida nursing home GL effectively withdrawn by most admitted carriers. Predominantly E&S/surplus lines market with very high retentions.
Underwriting LessonSenior living GL requires state-specific analysis. Florida, Georgia, Alabama: extreme caution. Consider mandatory minimum $2m SIR.
2018 Roundup — Dewayne Johnson (Groundskeeper) First Verdict PROD MAJ $289m (reduced to $78m on appeal) UA / US VER
CauseFirst Roundup/glyphosate trial verdict — groundskeeper Dewayne Johnson won $289m against Monsanto. Opened floodgates for subsequent cases that eventually settled for $10.9bn.
Coverage TriggerProducts — BI. Internal Monsanto emails on scientific manipulation were key to punitive damages award.
Market ImpactPrecedent-setting verdict for mass tort litigation against agricultural chemical companies. Triggered litigation funding investment in glyphosate plaintiff portfolio.
Underwriting LessonInternal communications/corporate governance records material to punitive damage exposure. Underwriters should request product safety committee status for chemical manufacturers.
2020 Defamation / False Arrest — Social Media Platform GL PI MAJ ~$50m (illustrative composite) UA / UC ILLUS
CauseComposite representing PI/defamation claims under Coverage B (personal and advertising injury) arising from viral social media misidentification, false arrest allegations, and malicious prosecution claims.
Coverage TriggerCoverage B — personal and advertising injury (offences b, d, e of ISO CG 00 01). Coverage B aggregate limits often inadequate relative to BI. IP exclusion potential conflict.
Market ImpactCoverage B exposure growing as defamation/PI claims increase in social media era. Many writers sub-limiting Coverage B to $1m regardless of occurrence limit.
Underwriting LessonReview Coverage B sub-limit vs. total limit. Consider knowledge of publication exclusion endorsement for media-adjacent risks.
2022 E-cigarette / Vape — Youth Marketing Products Liability MDL PROD MAJ $462m (JUUL settlement) UA / US VER
CauseJUUL Labs settled with 33 states and DC for $462m over marketing e-cigarettes to minors. Separate personal injury claims ongoing for nicotine addiction and lung injury.
Coverage TriggerProducts — BI/PI. Coverage disputed on intentional act grounds re: youth marketing. Altria (35% stake in JUUL) named in many suits.
Market ImpactE-cigarette/vape product GL effectively uninsurable in standard market. E&S specialty only at very high rates with addiction exclusions.
Underwriting LessonAny product with inhalation risk + youth access warrants heightened screening. Regulatory status with FDA critical underwriting data point.
2022 Tyco / Johnson Controls — AFFF PFAS Municipal Claims PFAS MAJ $750m+ (partial) UA / US EST
CauseMunicipal water system claims against AFFF manufacturers including Tyco/JCI. Groundwater and drinking water contamination from firefighting foam at industrial and military sites.
Coverage TriggerProducts / pollution GL. Most policies post-1986 contain total pollution exclusions (TPE) that were heavily litigated in PFAS context.
Market ImpactLed to PFAS absolute exclusion becoming market-standard in Lloyd's GL slips from 2021. Dispute over whether PFAS is "pollution" under TPE continues in state courts.
Underwriting LessonPFAS exclusion required for all manufacturing, chemical, and industrial GL. Water utility GL separately exposed as claimant class.
2019 Construction Scaffold Collapse — New York Labour Law §240 BI MAJ ~$35m (illustrative) UA / UC ILLUS
CauseIllustrative composite of New York Labour Law §240 ("Scaffold Law") absolute liability claims. NY §240 imposes strict/absolute liability on owners and general contractors for gravity-related construction accidents. Plaintiff contributory negligence is no defence.
Coverage TriggerOperations GL — BI. NY §240 is unique nationally and drives NY construction GL to among highest rates in the US market.
Market ImpactNew York construction GL rates 3–5× national average. Many carriers exclude NY §240 exposure entirely. Separate endorsement required.
Underwriting LessonNY construction GL requires separate §240 analysis. Sublimit or exclusion standard. Any risk with NY operations and construction activity flagged for referral.
2022 Biometric Data — Illinois BIPA Class Actions (Tech Sector) CYBER MAJ $650m (BNSF/Facebook prior settlements) UA / UC VER
CauseIllinois Biometric Information Privacy Act (BIPA) class actions for unlawful collection of fingerprints/facial recognition data. $1,000–$5,000 per violation, no proof of harm needed. Facebook settled for $650m (2021), BNSF for $228m.
Coverage TriggerGL Coverage A (BI — privacy injury) or Coverage B (PI — publication of private information). Disputed whether BIPA claims are "bodily injury" under GL. Also triggers cyber coverage.
Market ImpactBIPA creates aggregated statutory damages without actual harm. Illinois GL policies for technology users carry substantial BIPA sub-limit considerations.
Underwriting LessonIllinois operations with biometric data collection require BIPA sublimit or exclusion analysis. Cyber and GL co-ordination essential to avoid gaps.
2023 USA Gymnastics — Larry Nassar Abuse Settlement (USOC) SAM MAJ $380m UA / US VER
CauseSexual abuse of gymnasts by team physician Larry Nassar. USA Gymnastics and USOC (now USOPC) settled with approximately 500 survivors. Nassar sentenced to 175 years.
Coverage TriggerGL — SAM/PI. Coverage disputed on intentional act exclusion grounds. Multiple insurers including Markel and Great American involved in coverage litigation.
Market ImpactConfirmed that institutional SAM exposure exists even in non-religious youth organisations. Sports governing body GL now specialty class.
Underwriting LessonAny youth-serving sports organisation requires standalone SAM coverage. Institutional knowledge and reporting protocols are key underwriting criteria.
2021 Amazon — Third-Party Marketplace Products Liability BI MOD ~$40m (est. aggregate) UA / US EST
CauseProducts sold through Amazon Marketplace found to injure users — defective children's products, fire-risk electronics. Pennsylvania Superior Court ruled Amazon liable as product seller (2021).
Coverage TriggerProducts — BI. Amazon required third-party sellers to name Amazon as additional insured. Amazon's own GL programme covers marketplace operator role.
Market ImpactE-commerce platform product liability a growing category. Platform operator GL for e-commerce one of the fastest-growing specialty GL segments.
Underwriting LessonMarketplace/platform operator GL distinct from retailer GL. Requires analysis of seller vetting, product safety oversight, and indemnity flow-through from sellers.
2020 Hand Sanitiser / PPE — COVID-19 Product Defect Claims PROD MOD ~$30m (est. composite) UA / US ILLUS
CauseComposite of product defect claims related to COVID-era products: methanol-contaminated hand sanitiser (HAND LLC — methanol poisoning deaths), ineffective KN95 masks, defective ventilators.
Coverage TriggerProducts — BI. PREP Act provided some immunity for COVID countermeasures. Coverage A applicable to BI from defective product.
Market ImpactCOVID-era product rush created new product liability categories. Emergency manufacturing without standard QC created insurable events.
Underwriting LessonNew product manufacturers require full quality control documentation review. Emergency production or product line changes require mid-term notice.
2018 Camp Lejeune Water Contamination — Property & Health Claims PD MAJ $21bn+ (2022 PACT Act authorisation) UA / US VER
CauseTrichloroethylene (TCE) and perchloroethylene (PCE) contamination of Camp Lejeune water supply 1953–1987. PACT Act 2022 opened two-year window for veteran and family member claims. 200,000+ claims filed.
Coverage TriggerGovernment/military liability — not standard CGL. Private contractor GL potentially implicated for remediation contractors. Highlights long-tail environmental contamination exposure.
Market ImpactGovernment contractor operations and environmental liabilities often intermingled. Industrial solvent use in operations requires careful GL coverage mapping.
Underwriting LessonAny contractor with historic government site operations should be reviewed for historical contamination exposure.
2023 Premises Liability — Chicago Rooftop Collapse (Restaurant) BI MOD ~$15m (est.) UA EST
CauseRestaurant rooftop deck structural failure during private event — multiple injuries. Premises liability (operations GL) against property owner, restaurant operator, and event organiser.
Coverage TriggerCoverage A — BI. Multiple insured defendants. Allocation between property owner GL and tenant/operator GL. Additional insured status contested.
Market ImpactIllustrative of premises liability claims against food/beverage/hospitality sector. Structural integrity assessments now key for high-occupancy entertainment venues.
Underwriting LessonHospitality GL should require evidence of structural inspections for elevated or temporary structures. Additional insured chain must be clear.
2022 PFAS in Food Packaging — Fast Food Chains (Class Action) PFAS MOD ~$20m (est. composite) UA / US ILLUS
CauseClass actions against major fast food chains (McDonald's, Burger King, Wendy's) over PFAS/PFOA in food packaging materials. Allegations of product contamination and consumer deception.
Coverage TriggerProducts GL — BI/PI. Coverage disputed — PFAS exclusions applied from 2021. Earlier policies potentially exposed under products coverage.
Market ImpactFood packaging supply chain implicated in PFAS exposure. Food service GL now screened for packaging material sourcing.
Underwriting LessonFood and beverage GL requires PFAS supply chain warranty. Packaging sourced from non-compliant suppliers creates uninsured product liability.
2021 GL Coverage B — Website Accessibility ADA Class Actions CYBER MOD ~$10m (composite) UA ILLUS
CauseADA Title III class actions against businesses for inaccessible websites (missing alt text, keyboard navigation, screen reader compatibility). Surge in serial plaintiff filings post-2018 (Winn-Dixie case).
Coverage TriggerCoverage B — personal and advertising injury (discrimination offence). GL coverage for ADA digital accessibility claims disputed — some courts have found coverage, others have not.
Market ImpactADA website accessibility a high-frequency, low-severity category that strains aggregate limits. Defence costs often exceed settlement value.
Underwriting LessonReview Coverage B aggregate relative to ADA claim frequency in insured's sector. Retail and hospitality particularly exposed. WCAG 2.1 compliance reduces risk.
2021 Electric Scooter Injuries — Shared Mobility Products GL PROD MOD ~$25m (est. composite) UA / US EST
CauseHead injuries and fatalities from shared electric scooter use (Bird, Lime, Spin). Product design defects (speed limiter failure, brake failure) and premises liability where sidewalk deployment injured pedestrians.
Coverage TriggerProducts GL — BI. Complex split between operator GL (operations/premises) and product manufacturer GL (defective hardware). Waiver of subrogation between manufacturers and operators.
Market ImpactShared mobility GL a new specialty category. City permit conditions often require minimum GL limits of $5m per occurrence for e-scooter operators.
Underwriting LessonShared mobility operators require combination of products + operations GL. Municipal permit requirements drive minimum limit specifications.
2020 Cannabis Dispensary — Premises BI & Dram Shop (Colorado) BI MOD ~$12m (illustrative) UA ILLUS
CauseIllustrative composite: cannabis dispensary sold edibles to customer who subsequently caused a motor accident. Colorado dram shop analogue liability — dispensary liability for post-consumption third-party harm.
Coverage TriggerOperations GL — BI. Many standard carriers exclude cannabis operations. E&S specialty market. Dram shop liability for cannabis analogous to alcohol liability.
Market ImpactCannabis GL one of fastest-growing E&S specialty categories. All 38 recreational/medical states have distinct regulatory regimes affecting GL exposure.
Underwriting LessonCannabis GL requires state-by-state analysis. Operations (dispensary vs. cultivation vs. processing) have very different GL profiles.
2022 Construction Defect — Condominium HOA (Florida) BI MOD ~$20m (est.) UA / UC EST
CauseSurfside Champlain Towers South collapse, June 2021 — 98 deaths. While directly a structural failure, triggered wave of Florida condominium GL and construction defect claims and reserve increases across the state.
Coverage TriggerOperations/completed operations GL — BI and PD. Developer, contractor, structural engineer all potentially liable. HOA GL separate from developer/contractor GL.
Market ImpactFlorida property/condo GL repriced dramatically post-Surfside. Legislative changes (SB 4-D) mandated structural inspections. GL rates for Florida condo construction +80–150%.
Underwriting LessonFlorida condominium GL requires Surfside-era structural inspection certificates. Completed operations tail for condo developments typically 10+ years.
2023 Data Destruction — IT Contractor Ransomware (Operations PD) PD MOD ~$18m (est.) UA / US ILLUS
CauseIllustrative composite: managed IT service provider suffered ransomware; client data and systems destroyed. Client sued IT contractor under GL for property damage (loss of data and systems).
Coverage TriggerCoverage A — PD (electronic data). ISO CG 00 01 2001 amendment excluded "electronic data" from PD definition. Many IT contractor GL policies now add electronic data back in. Complex GL/cyber co-ordination required.
Market ImpactIT/technology contractor GL and cyber increasingly interlinked. Standalone cyber insufficient if GL policy excludes electronic data PD.
Underwriting LessonTechnology E&O/GL combined policy recommended for IT service providers. ISO CG 00 01 electronic data exclusion must be evaluated.
2023 Slip & Fall — Retail Grocery (High-Frequency Attritional) BI MIN $50k–$500k (per claim) UA EST
CauseRepresentative of high-frequency premises liability — slip and fall on wet floors, spills, poor lighting in retail environments. Primary driver of attritional GL loss for retail, hospitality, and food service operations.
Coverage TriggerCoverage A — BI. Premises operations. High frequency/low severity, but aggregate impact material for large retail chains. Defence costs often exceed settlement amount for minor injuries.
Market ImpactAttritional GL for retail is actuarially predictable but influenced by jurisdiction — California, Florida, New York materially above national average.
Underwriting LessonSlip and fall frequency driven by incident reporting procedures, cleaning logs, CCTV. Risk quality driven by loss control practices. Key driver of primary GL rate for retail risks.
2022 Food Contamination — E. coli Outbreak (Small Restaurant Chain) PROD MIN $2m–$5m (illustrative) UA ILLUS
CauseIllustrative of food contamination claim against regional restaurant chain — multiple E. coli illness cases, CDC traceback to contaminated lettuce. Reputational damage, loss of business, and personal injury claims.
Coverage TriggerProducts GL — BI. Potentially also products recall (separate cover). Restaurant GL typically includes products coverage on standard CGL form.
Market ImpactFood service GL attritional category. Chipotle precedent (multiple E. coli outbreaks 2015–2018, $25m DOJ settlement) shows reputational multiplier for national chains.
Underwriting LessonRestaurant/food service GL loss experience is the primary rating driver. HACCP certification and food safety audit records should be requested for large accounts.
2021 Contractor Injury on Third-Party Premises (Additional Insured Claim) BI MIN $500k–$2m (illustrative) UA ILLUS
CauseIllustrative of additional insured (AI) GL claim: subcontractor employee injured on general contractor's site; GC named as AI on subcontractor's GL policy. AI tender triggers coverage dispute over scope and limits.
Coverage TriggerCoverage A — BI. Additional insured endorsements (CG 20 10 / CG 20 37) define scope. Ongoing vs. completed operations AI distinction frequently litigated.
Market ImpactAI claims represent a significant proportion of construction GL claims. AI endorsement wording and limits critical to coverage outcome.
Underwriting LessonReview AI endorsement language — blanket AI (CG 20 33) vs. specific (CG 20 10/37). Subcontractor limit adequacy relative to GC project value critical.
2022 Copyright / Advertising Injury — Social Media Content Claim PI MIN $200k–$1m (illustrative) UA ILLUS
CauseIllustrative: business uses copyrighted music in social media advertising without licence; copyright holder claims infringement under Coverage B (advertising injury — "use of another's copyrighted work" — offence g of ISO CG 00 01).
Coverage TriggerCoverage B — advertising injury. IP exclusion (exclusion i) may apply if infringement was intentional. Defence costs often $100k–$500k for IP copyright disputes.
Market ImpactSocial media advertising injury a growing sub-category. IP infringement in digital marketing now a standard Coverage B loss type.
Underwriting LessonCoverage B IP exclusion must be understood by insured. Digital marketing agencies require careful Coverage B analysis — consider separate IP/media liability endorsement.
2023 Nuclear Verdict — Drunk Driving Accident / Dram Shop (Texas) BI MOD $300m (verdict, under appeal) UA / UC VER
CauseTexas dram shop case — restaurant/bar served visibly intoxicated patron who subsequently caused a fatal accident. Jury awarded $300m including $250m punitive against the establishment.
Coverage TriggerOperations GL — BI. Texas dram shop liability codified in Tex. Alco. Bev. Code §2.02. Punitive damages not covered under most GL policies in Texas.
Market ImpactTexas hospitality/liquor liability rates among highest in US. Nuclear verdict risk drives many Texas food/beverage operators to E&S market.
Underwriting LessonLiquor liability endorsement essential for any F&B operation serving alcohol. Texas, Florida, Louisiana — highest dram shop verdict states. Punitive multiplier must be assessed for umbrella adequacy.
