US GL Underwriting Framework

US General Liability is technically demanding, jurisdiction-sensitive, and subject to systemic inflation pressures that make purely actuarial pricing unreliable without qualitative overlay. The ISO CG 00 01 form provides the standard structure, but Lloyd's E&S underwriters must understand deviation from admitted-market norms, E&S form differences, and the unique exposure characteristics of the risks that reach the London market — which are, by definition, those the admitted market has declined or restricted. EST

⚠ Social Inflation Overlay (2017–present): US GL loss costs have escalated at 8–15% per annum above underlying economic inflation since 2017. This reflects nuclear verdict proliferation, third-party litigation funding, plaintiff bar expertise, and expanding theories of liability (PFAS, SAM lookback windows, BIPA). Any rating approach that relies on pre-2017 loss trend data without a social inflation load is materially under-reserved.

US-Specific Hazard Considerations

Four hazards dominate US GL underwriting in the Lloyd's E&S market and require explicit assessment at submission stage. EST

Nuclear Verdicts

Jury awards >$10m — driven by anchoring tactics, reptile theory, litigation funding. Highest frequency: Georgia, Florida, Texas, California, Illinois. Umbrella/excess adequacy is the critical question for any high-hazard risk in these states.

PFAS / Toxic Tort

Per- and polyfluoroalkyl substances (PFAS/PFOA/PFOS) now absolute exclusion in Lloyd's GL. Any manufacturer using fluorosurfactants, stain/water repellents, or AFFF products must be declined or placed on specialist environmental form.

SAM Exposure

Sexual abuse and molestation (SAM) lookback windows active in 20+ states. Youth-serving organisations (schools, sports, religious, camps) must carry standalone SAM cover. Standard CGL intentional acts exclusion likely to respond — but SAM may be disputed.

Social Engineering / Cyber GL

BIPA (Illinois), biometric data, website accessibility ADA claims, and data destruction PD claims increasingly implicate GL Coverage A and B. GL/cyber co-ordination essential. ISO CG 00 01 electronic data PD exclusion must be assessed for technology risks.

NY Labour Law §240

Scaffold Law absolute liability — no contributory negligence defence. New York construction GL rates 3–5× national average. §240 sublimit or exclusion standard for non-specialist writers. Require explicit coverage decision at quotation.

Cannabis / Emerging Industries

Cannabis operations (cultivation, processing, dispensary), psychedelic therapy, and shared mobility represent the fastest-growing E&S GL categories. All require specialist form, state-by-state regulatory analysis, and non-admitted placement in most states.

Premium Rating — UA Primary CGL

The standard Lloyd's rating approach for US primary CGL (UA) combines a base rate per unit of exposure with multiplicative adjustment factors. EST

UA Primary CGL Premium Formula Premium = Exposure Base × Base Rate × Industry Hazard Factor × Jurisdiction Factor × Claims History Loading × Limit Factor × Deductible Credit × Social Inflation Load

Key Rating Factor Cards

Industry Hazard Factor

Reflects the inherent claims frequency and severity of the insured's primary business activity. Based on ISO class codes, historical loss statistics, and NCCI data.

Ranges: 0.6× (low hazard office) → 3.0× (high hazard manufacturing / nuclear verdict sector)

Jurisdiction / Venue Factor

The single most critical factor in US GL. Adjusts for the litigation climate, nuclear verdict frequency, and plaintiff-friendly tort rules in the risk's primary operating states.

NY/FL/GA/TX/CA: +50–120% load vs. national base. Midwest / Mountain states: −10–20% credit.

Claims History Loading

3–5 year loss history with current carrier(s). Experience modification approach — actual vs. expected losses on a developed basis. Social inflation development factor applied to older losses.

Clean 5yr: 0.80× credit. 1 major loss: 1.5–2.0× load. 3yr LR >80%: referral mandatory.

Limit / Layer Factor

ISO increased limits factors (ILFs) adjusted for nuclear verdict severity distribution. Standard base limit $100k/$300k. Most E&S risks: $1m/$2m occurrence/aggregate.

ILF $1m/$2m vs. $100k/$300k base: approximately 2.2–2.8× depending on class.

Deductible / SIR Credit

Self-insured retention or per-occurrence deductible credit. Large risks may carry $50k–$250k SIR, reducing primary layer expected losses materially.

$10k ded: −8%. $25k ded: −15%. $100k SIR: −30–40% depending on class frequency.

Social Inflation Load (2024)

Explicit load above actuarial trend to reflect nuclear verdict growth, litigation funding, and plaintiff bar strategy evolution. Applied on top of standard loss trend.

Nuclear verdict exposure states: +15–30% additional load. High-hazard class + nuclear state: +25–40%.

Products-Completed Operations (PCOH)

Separate PCOH premium for manufacturers, distributors, and contractors. PCOH tail can extend 10+ years post-operation for construction and products risks.

PCOH premium typically 30–60% of operations GL premium for manufacturing class.

Additional Insured Exposure

Blanket AI endorsements (CG 20 33) extend coverage to unlimited AIs and increase claim frequency. Specific AI (CG 20 10/37) preferred — limits exposure to named relationships.

Blanket AI surcharge: +10–20% where used. Construction GC blanket AI: +15–25%.

Automatic Referral Triggers

The following conditions require senior underwriter or syndicate authority approval before quotation. EST

Nuclear Verdict Jurisdiction — High HazardHigh-hazard class (construction, trucking, hospitality) with >50% operations in GA, FL, TX, CA, IL. Combination of venue + hazard requires senior sign-off on limit adequacy.
3yr Loss Ratio >80%Any risk with developed 3-year loss ratio exceeding 80% on current programme. Root cause analysis required — systemic vs. attritional.
PFAS / Opioid / Tobacco ExposureAny manufacturing, distribution, or processing risk with known or suspected PFAS, opioid, or tobacco/nicotine supply chain involvement. Absolute exclusion minimum requirement.
SAM / Youth-Serving Org.Any religious institution, school district, youth sports organisation, camp, or scouting body. SAM lookback window analysis required. Standalone SAM endorsement or exclusion mandatory.
Occurrence Limit >$10mPrimary occurrence limit in excess of $10m for any UA risk. Large limit underwriting requires actuarial support for ILF above standard tables.
NY Construction / Scaffold LawAny construction risk with >25% of payroll in New York. §240 analysis mandatory. Cannot bind without explicit §240 coverage decision.
Cannabis / Emerging SubstanceAny client with cannabis cultivation, processing, dispensary, or CBD-infused products operations. US regulatory patchwork makes standard GL form potentially void in multiple states.
Mass Tort Industry InvolvementPharma distribution, earplugs/PPE manufacturing, talc/mineral products, or agricultural chemicals. Mass tort exposure requires coverage counsel review before quotation.
