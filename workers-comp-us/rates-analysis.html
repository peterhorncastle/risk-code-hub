IBNR & Long-Tail Development Considerations
Development FactorImpact on WC Reserving
Long-tail indemnity benefitsPermanent total disability claims can extend 30–40 years; actuarial tail for PTD claims requires long discount rate assumptions and life expectancy modelling
Medical cost trend uncertaintyWC medical is open-ended (no dollar cap on Part One); medical inflation uncertainty is the primary WC reserving risk for accident years 2021+
Opioid reserve reopeningPre-2018 accident years with opioid-dependent claimants may see claim reopening as tolerance, addiction management and overdose events generate ongoing medical costs
COVID long-tailLong-COVID disability and respiratory impairment claims from 2020–2022 accident years developing slowly; NCCI guidance suggests 2–5 year development tail for COVID WC claims
Black lung latencyCoal mining WC black lung claims may not manifest for 20–30 years post-exposure; reserve adequacy for active and former coal mine operators requires specialist actuarial review
Catastrophic injury annuityCatastrophic injury cases (TBI, spinal cord) are often settled via structured settlements or annuities; present-value calculations exposed to longevity and discount rate risk
