Primary Sources — Clauses, Conventions & Official Publications

📜 Institute War & Strikes Clauses

  • ⚓
    Institute War and Strikes Clauses Hulls—Time (CL.280, 1983)
    Joint Cargo Committee / LMAA. The principal wording governing hull war risk cover in the Lloyd's market. Clauses 1.1–1.6 define covered perils; Clause 3 sets out exclusions. Available via Lloyd's Market Association (LMA). VER
  • ⚓
    Institute War and Strikes Clauses Hulls—Voyage (CL.281, 1983)
    Companion wording for single-voyage war risk coverage. Same peril structure as CL.280 but on a per-voyage basis. VER
  • ✈️
    AVN52C — Aviation War, Hi-Jacking and Other Perils Exclusion Clause
    Lloyd's Aviation Underwriters Association. Excludes war perils from aviation H&M policies; the aviation equivalent of the marine F.C.&S. clause. AW-coded business covers these excluded perils. VER
  • 📄
    F.C.&S. (Free of Capture and Seizure) Warranty
    Standard exclusion clause in all H&M policies carving out war perils. Historical antecedent of CL.280. Creates the structural need for a separate war risk market. VER

🛡️ JWC & Market Bodies

  • 🛡️
    Joint War Committee (JWC) — Listed Areas
    Lloyd's/IUA Joint War Committee. Publishes the official JWC Hull War, Strikes, Terrorism and Related Perils Listed Areas. Updated periodically; listed areas trigger mandatory AWRP. JWC is joint Lloyd's/company market body. VER
  • 📊
    Lloyd's Market Association (LMA) — War Risk Clauses Repository
    lma.com. Publishes all Institute clauses, market bulletins, and war risk guidance for Lloyd's practitioners. VER
  • 🏛️
    International Group of P&I Clubs (IG Group) — War Rules
    igpandi.org. P&I Clubs publish war rules governing third-party war liability cover. War pooling threshold applies. VER
  • 🚢
    BIMCO — Voywar 2013, Conwartime 2013
    Baltic and International Maritime Council. Standard charterparty war risk liberty/payment clauses. Sets out rights and obligations of shipowners and charterers when vessels transit war zones. VER

🌊 IMO & Industry Standards

  • 🌊
    Best Management Practices for Protection against Somalia Based Piracy — BMP6 (2023)
    IMO/BIMCO/ICS/INTERCARGO/INTERTANKO et al. Updated BMP6 extends beyond Somali piracy to cover Red Sea/Houthi threats, drone/missile countermeasures, and Gulf of Guinea piracy. Key quasi-warranty for hull war underwriters. VER
  • 📡
    UKMTO — United Kingdom Maritime Trade Operations
    Royal Navy/UK MoD. Operates UKMTO Voluntary Reporting Scheme in Indian Ocean/Gulf region. Registration is quasi-warranty in many Lloyd's hull war endorsements; UKMTO provides voyage tracking. VER
  • 🔒
    ISPS Code — International Ship and Port Facility Security Code (2002)
    IMO. Adopted post-9/11; requires ship security plans, security officers, and port facility security. ISPS compliance is a condition of cover for security-related war/terrorism losses in many Lloyd's wordings. VER
  • ⚙️
    ISM Code — International Safety Management Code
    IMO Resolution A.741(18). Requires Safety Management Certificates (SMC) for all ocean-going vessels. Underwriters typically require valid ISM certification; absence may void war cover. VER

📰 Statistics & Market Data

  • 📈
    Lloyd's List — Shipping Intelligence & War Risk Reporting
    lloydslist.com. Primary trade publication for shipping market; publishes war risk rate movements, JWC decisions, fleet routing analysis. Subscription required. VER
  • 🗂️
    IMB Piracy Report — Annual
    ICC International Maritime Bureau (IMB). Annual piracy and armed robbery report. 2024 edition covers Red Sea incidents, Gulf of Guinea trends, Strait of Malacca. Key loss data source for PIR-type events. VER
  • 📋
    IUMI — International Union of Marine Insurance Ocean Hull Statistics
    iumi.com. Annual hull insurance statistics: premium income, loss ratios, market trends. War risk premium data partially included in ocean hull statistics. VER
  • 📊
    Marsh — War Risk Briefings & Fleet Advisory
    marsh.com. Broker advisory on war risk rates, AWRP movements, Red Sea/Black Sea situation. Market-facing rate estimates and routing analysis. VER

🏛️ Sanctions & Regulatory

  • ⚖️
    OFAC — Office of Foreign Assets Control (US Treasury)
    ofac.treas.gov. SDN list; OFAC sanctions applicable to US-connected insurance transactions. Marine war policies covering sanctioned vessels or ports are void. VER
  • 🏦
    OFSI — Office of Financial Sanctions Implementation (UK HM Treasury)
    gov.uk/ofsi. UK financial sanctions; equivalent to OFAC for UK-regulated insurers and Lloyd's. VER
  • 🇪🇺
    EU Council Regulations — Russia Sanctions
    Council Regulation (EU) 2022/263 et seq. EU sanctions on Russia post-Ukraine invasion; prohibit EU insurers from covering Russian vessels and related interests. VER
  • 🌍
    ILO MLC 2006 — Maritime Labour Convention
    ilo.org. Requires shipowners to provide crew insurance (death, disability, repatriation). Article 4.2 and Standard A4.2 specify minimum financial security. KM-code PA policies must comply. VER

  • 🛳️
    CMF (Combined Maritime Forces) — Maritime Security Advisories
    combinedmaritimeforces.com. US-led multinational naval coalition; Operation Prosperity Guardian (Red Sea). Issues maritime security advisories referenced by underwriters. VER
  • 🇬🇧
    MSCHOA — Maritime Security Centre Horn of Africa
    mschoa.org. EU Naval Force Somalia (EUNAVFOR). Provides vessel reporting and threat assessment for Gulf of Aden/Indian Ocean. Referenced in BMP compliance. VER

Landmark Cases & Market-Shaping Incidents

Kawasaki Kisen Kaisha v Tsurushima Maru (1921) — Constructive Total Loss: War Seizure

Facts: Japanese vessel seized by German forces in WWI and not returned after war. Shipowner claimed CTL under war policy.

Ruling: Court confirmed CTL where vessel's return was commercially improbable. Established foundation for CTL valuation in war seizure cases. Principle still applied in modern CL.280 CTL claims. VER

Stena Impero — Iran IRGC Seizure (2019)

Facts: UK-flagged Stena Impero seized by Iranian Revolutionary Guard Corps in Strait of Hormuz, held for 65 days. Owners claimed under hull war policy (CL.280 Clause 1.2 — capture, seizure, arrest).

Ruling: Coverage confirmed — seizure by government forces of an opposing state falls within CL.280 peril. Settlement reached; Stena recovered vessel. Underwriters paid detention/CTL-threat expenses. Reinforced live exposure of the market. EST

Merck & Co v Starr Indemnity / Zurich (NotPetya — Cyber/War Attribution) (2023)

Facts: NotPetya malware (attributed to Russian GRU) caused ~$1.4bn damage to Merck. Insurer denied claim under "hostile/warlike action" exclusion in property/liability policy. Dispute over whether state-sponsored cyber attack = "war".

Ruling (NJ Superior Court 2023): Court found for Merck. "Warlike action" exclusion requires kinetic/physical conflict; cyber attacks by state actors on civilian targets not automatically "war" under policy language. Significant for marine war underwriters assessing cyber-war boundary.
Marine War Implication: CL.280 war perils do not expressly include cyber acts. LMA5403 cyber exclusion interacts with war clauses. Attribution remains the key legal battleground for cyber/war boundary in hull war policies. EST

Russia 2022 — Leased Aircraft Confiscation (>$10bn AW Loss Event)

Facts: Following Russia's invasion of Ukraine, Western-leased aircraft (~520 aircraft, book value ~$10bn) were stranded when Russia closed airspace and refused to return aircraft to lessors. Aviation war (AW) underwriters faced mass CTL claims under "government seizure/confiscation" perils.

Dispute: Whether confiscation by Russia constitutes "war" or "government act" under aviation war wordings; whether all-risks or war policy responded. As of early 2026, litigation ongoing in multiple jurisdictions; estimated settlements in progress but final numbers not confirmed. Major market-defining event for AW and reinsurance capacity. VER

MV Sirius Star Ransom Payment (2008)

Facts: Saudi VLCC Sirius Star hijacked by Somali pirates; $3m ransom paid. Hull war policy responded under piracy peril (CL.280 Cl.1.5). P&I Club separately covered crew welfare costs.

Outcome: Ransom covered as "piracy" expense under war policy — established ransom payments as insurable losses under Lloyd's piracy clauses, accelerating take-up of K&R/war cover in the tanker market. VER

Known Data Gaps GAP

  • Lloyd's W/WB/AW/WX GWP — Standalone War Class Data Lloyd's does not publicly separate war risk premium from combined hull GWP. Estimated global marine war market at ~$600m–$800m GWP (2024) but no authoritative breakdown by Lloyd's code is publicly available. A Lloyd's Insights subscription or syndicates' Solvency II disclosures would be required.
  • JWC AWRP Rate Schedules JWC publishes listed areas only; premium rates are negotiated between brokers and lead underwriters and are commercially confidential. The AWRP ranges shown throughout this hub are derived from broker/market commentary, not JWC publications. Actual rates in 2024/25 may vary substantially between fleets.
  • Black Sea / Russia–Ukraine — Aggregate Insured War Loss Multiple vessel detentions, seizures, and damage claims arising from the Russia–Ukraine conflict are either in litigation or under confidential settlement. Definitive aggregate insured loss figure for the Lloyd's war market is not confirmed in the public domain as at April 2026. Estimates range from $500m to >$2bn depending on Black Sea and aviation confiscation included.
  • Red Sea Houthi Campaign — Running Insured Loss Total Campaign began November 2023; loss assessment ongoing through 2024. No authoritative market-wide insured total yet published. Individual vessel loss amounts confirmed (e.g., Marlin Luanda); aggregate remains an estimate. IUMI 2025 statistics expected to provide first consolidated view.
  • Aviation War (AW) — Russia Confiscation Final Settlement Litigation in Ireland, UK, and Russia involving AerCap, Air Lease, Avolon and their insurers/reinsurers. As at April 2026, settlements in progress; final aggregate AW loss figure for underwriters remains uncertain. Published estimates: $8–12bn across hull/war/BI policies combined; final insured allocation unconfirmed.
