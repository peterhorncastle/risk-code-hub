Key Jurisdiction Profiles
United Kingdom

UK — Pool Re + Lloyd's Standalone

Pool Re reinsures UK commercial property damage from terrorism up to the full sum insured (no SI cap since 2019 reform). Pool Re does not cover BI, active assailant casualty, or political violence (riots/civil commotion). A standalone terrorism policy (TO code) fills these gaps. NBCR added to Pool Re in 2017 — though with sublimits on BI. Access denial BI (inability to access undamaged premises) is available from Lloyd's. VER

United States

USA — TRIA Backstop + Standalone (TU)

TRIA requires US commercial insurers to make terrorism coverage available (make available mandate). TRIA certification requires: act committed by foreign individual(s), $5m+ aggregate loss, Secretary of Treasury/AG certification. TRIA does not cover: domestic terrorism (Oklahoma City would not qualify), events below $5m threshold. Standalone terrorism (TU) policy still essential for BI, active assailant and cyberterrorism beyond TRIA scope. VER

France

France — GAREAT + Terrorism Endorsement for BI

GAREAT covers property damage for French-sited risks above €6m SI (smaller risks covered through mandatory insurance mechanism). GAREAT does not cover BI — a terrorism endorsement to the property policy (or standalone terrorism BI policy) is required. GAREAT's CCR backing provides effectively unlimited government reinsurance for major events. Paris Bataclan (2015) was GAREAT's largest single event. EST

Germany

Germany — Extremus AG for Large Risks

Extremus covers property damage for German-sited risks with SI above €25m. Smaller risks have limited terrorism coverage options — many rely on standard property policies with specific terrorism clauses. Government backstop up to €10bn per event. Extremus does not cover NBCR. Standalone terrorism policy (TO) needed for BI and risks below the Extremus SI threshold. EST

Middle East

Middle East — No Government Backstop; Full Lloyd's Programme

No government terrorism reinsurance pool exists across the GCC or wider Middle East. Multinationals operating in Saudi Arabia, UAE, Kuwait, Bahrain, Qatar, Jordan and Lebanon require full Lloyd's terrorism and political violence programme (TO/RW/RS). Rates are Zone C–D depending on country. Political violence (RS/RW) coverage is particularly important given regional geopolitical instability and the frequency of civil unrest events. EST

Ukraine / Active Conflict

Ukraine — War on Land (WL); Active Lloyd's Market

Following Russia's 2022 invasion, Ukraine-sited commercial property requires war on land (WL) or political violence (RW) cover. No government pool exists — Lloyd's is the only market. Premiums are Zone E (0.15–0.50%+ of SI depending on location proximity to front line). Claims are complex — physical assessment of damaged property in active conflict zones is extremely difficult. Lloyd's WL underwriting is highly selective with stringent risk information requirements. VER

NBCR Capacity Note — Global Programmes: NBCR terrorism coverage is the most restricted capacity in the global terrorism market. For the case study above, NBCR would be covered under Pool Re for the London location (post-2017) and potentially under TRIA for the New York location (if a TRIA-certified NBCR event). For Riyadh and other non-pool jurisdictions, NBCR coverage would require a specialist Lloyd's NBCR endorsement — and capacity is severely limited (typically $25–50m sublimit maximum from the handful of NBCR-writing syndicates). The Riyadh location would likely have no NBCR cover, or a very low sublimit. Insureds in Zone D countries should have an explicit NBCR conversation with their broker and the Lloyd's market. VER
