Lloyd's Risk Code Coverage

TO — Terrorism Other (Rest of World)

Standalone terrorism insurance for risks outside the USA. Covers physical damage and business interruption arising from an act of terrorism — defined broadly to include politically, ideologically or religiously motivated violence intended to influence a government or intimidate the public. Most Lloyd's terrorism premium by count of risks sits under TO. No government backstop applies — pure market capacity.

TU — Terrorism USA (TRIA Backstop Applies)

US-sited terrorism risk where the Terrorism Risk Insurance Act (TRIA) 2002 backstop applies. Insurers writing TU risks must offer terrorism cover (make available) and, where a TRIA-certified event occurs, the US federal government shares losses above the mandatory retention. TRIA reauthorised to 2027. Active assailant and NBCR sub-covers are typically written alongside TU.

TW — Terrorism Worldwide (Global Programme)

Terrorism on a worldwide basis — typically written for multinational corporations seeking a single global master policy. TW policies combine TO and TU exposure and must navigate multiple government backstops and local admitted requirements. Premium is aggregated from country-by-country exposure mapping. Often written as a difference-in-conditions layer above local admitted placements.

RS — Riot & Strike (Civil Commotion)

Covers physical damage and business interruption arising from riot, civil commotion, strike and malicious damage — perils typically excluded from standard property policies. RS sits at the lower end of the political violence spectrum, below insurrection or revolution. Key markets: South Africa (SASRIA), Middle East, Latin America. The 2021 South Africa KZN riots ($1.7bn insured) were the largest RS event on record.

RW — Revolution & War (Political Violence)

Broader political violence cover including insurrection, revolution, rebellion, mutiny, coup d'etat and civil war — as well as the more limited RS perils. RW represents the full political violence spectrum short of declared major-power war. Critical for clients operating in politically unstable emerging markets: Middle East, sub-Saharan Africa, Central Asia. PV policies often bundle RS+RW in a single form.

WL — War on Land (Physical Damage)

Physical damage to property caused by land-based armed conflict — applicable to conventional war, whether declared or undeclared. WL policies were largely theoretical capacity until Russia's invasion of Ukraine in 2022, when they became live and heavily claims-relevant. Lloyd's is the primary market for WL capacity globally. Closely interfaces with political risk and trade credit insurance.

Coverage Taxonomy

Physical Damage

Property damage directly caused by an act of terrorism, political violence or war on land. The foundational peril — covers structural damage, contents loss, debris removal and reinstatement. Triggers standalone terrorism policy because standard property policies contain a blanket terrorism exclusion. VER

Business Interruption

Loss of revenue and additional expenses following terrorism-caused physical damage. BI indemnity periods of 12–36 months are standard; longer for high-dependency landmark sites. Access denial BI (inability to access premises following a nearby terrorist incident) is a critical extension for urban commercial properties. VER

Active Assailant / Workplace Violence

Emerging standalone product covering losses from mass casualty events — mass shootings, knife attacks, vehicle ramming. Covers BI, crisis management costs, medical expenses and third-party liability. Las Vegas 2017 ($750m+ est.) drove rapid growth in active assailant capacity at Lloyd's. Fastest-growing terrorism sub-product. VER

Political Violence

Broader than terrorism — encompasses riot, civil commotion, strike, insurrection, revolution and civil war. PV policies respond to the full spectrum of politically-motivated violence including events that do not meet the strict legal definition of "terrorism." Essential for emerging market operations. VER

War on Land

Physical damage in declared or undeclared land-based armed conflict. Traditionally a niche product, but the Ukraine conflict (2022–) brought WL into mainstream underwriting focus. Lloyd's syndicates are the primary capacity providers globally. Heavy aggregation management required. VER

NBCR

Nuclear, Biological, Chemical, Radiological terrorism — severely limited market capacity; typically excluded from standard terrorism policies or available only via specialist NBCR endorsement at Lloyd's. Very few syndicates write NBCR; sublimits are low. Government reinsurance backstops do not typically cover NBCR events. VER

The Terrorism Exclusion: Virtually all standard commercial property and liability policies contain a terrorism exclusion — typically broad in scope, excluding any act of terrorism whether or not the insured contributes to it. This exclusion emerged systematically after the IRA Baltic Exchange bombing (1992) and Pool Re's establishment (1993), and became universal globally post-9/11 (2001). The standalone terrorism policy — coded TO, TU or TW at Lloyd's — is the purpose-built product to fill this gap, restoring the terrorism cover that was removed from the property policy stack. VER
NBCR Capacity Warning: Nuclear, Biological, Chemical and Radiological (NBCR) terrorism cover is subject to severely limited market capacity. Only a handful of specialist Lloyd's syndicates will write NBCR exposure, and sublimits are typically $25m–$50m maximum. Most standard terrorism wordings contain an explicit NBCR exclusion. Government reinsurance schemes (Pool Re, GAREAT, TRIA) may provide partial backstop for NBCR events but terms vary significantly by jurisdiction. NBCR cover should be negotiated separately and confirmed explicitly in policy wording. VER
