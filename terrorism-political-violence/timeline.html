1992Major Loss

Baltic Exchange Bombing — IRA, London

IRA truck bomb at St Mary Axe, City of London. Three killed; approximately £800m in insured losses — the largest UK terrorism loss prior to 9/11. Triggered the creation of Pool Re.

Insured Loss: ~£800m est.
1993Regulatory / Government

Pool Re Established — UK Government Backstop

Reinsurance (Acts of Terrorism) Act 1993 establishes Pool Re as a government-backed terrorism reinsurer for UK commercial property. The UK government acts as reinsurer of last resort. Pool Re remains the cornerstone of UK terrorism insurance infrastructure. [L3]

1995Major Loss

Oklahoma City Bombing — Domestic Terrorism, USA

Timothy McVeigh detonates a truck bomb outside the Alfred P. Murrah Federal Building — 168 killed. Insured losses estimated at $125m+. Absorbed within standard property policies; no structural US market reform results. The first major US domestic terrorism event targeting a commercial/government building.

Insured Loss: ~$125m est.
1996Major Loss

Canary Wharf (Docklands) Bombing — IRA, London

IRA bomb at South Quay Plaza, Docklands. Two killed; £185m insured loss. First significant Pool Re payout — demonstrates pool mechanism working as intended. Lloyd's specialty terrorism capacity grows in response to confirmed demand. [L3]

Insured Loss: ~£185m est.
1993–2000Market Structural

Lloyd's Specialty Terrorism Capacity Develops

Following Pool Re's establishment, Lloyd's syndicates develop standalone terrorism capacity for UK risks (excess of Pool Re) and international terrorism risks (no government backstop available). Lloyd's becomes the global centre for terrorism insurance. TO/TU/TW classes take shape. Premium volumes remain modest but infrastructure is established.

2001Major Loss

September 11 Attacks — USA

Four coordinated al-Qaeda attacks. World Trade Center, Pentagon, United Airlines Flight 93. 2,977 killed. Insured loss estimated at $40–47bn across property, BI, aviation, liability, workers' comp and life. Lloyd's pays ~$4.8bn. Universal terrorism exclusions inserted market-wide within weeks. The event that created the modern standalone terrorism insurance market. [I1]

Insured Loss: $40–47bn VER
2002Regulatory / Government

TRIA Enacted — US Federal Terrorism Backstop

Terrorism Risk Insurance Act 2002 (Pub.L. 107-297) creates a federal backstop for TRIA-certified terrorism events. Insurers must make terrorism coverage available; government shares losses above mandatory retentions. Enables the US terrorism insurance market to function. GAREAT (France), Extremus (Germany) and other European pools also established 2001–03. [L1]

2004Major Loss

Madrid Train Bombings — Spain

Al-Qaeda coordinated bomb attacks on four commuter trains in Madrid. 193 killed. Insured losses estimated at €50m+. Consorcio de Compensación de Seguros (Spain's state insurer/reinsurer) responds. Tests European government pool structures. Demonstrates that terrorism loss is not confined to the USA or UK.

Insured Loss: ~€50m est.
2005Major Loss

London 7/7 Bombings — UK

Four coordinated suicide bombings on London Underground and a bus. 52 killed. Insured losses estimated at £50m across property damage, BI and casualty. Pool Re responds. London terrorism insurance market demonstrates resilience. Key learning: rapid emergency response and business continuity planning significantly reduces BI loss period. [L3]

Insured Loss: ~£50m est.
2008Geopolitical

Mumbai Attacks — Taj Hotel & Trident

Lashkar-e-Taiba coordinated attacks across Mumbai including the Taj Mahal Palace Hotel, Trident Hotel and CST railway station. 175 killed; 3-day siege at landmark hotels. Insured losses ~$40m est. for hotel property; broader casualty and BI losses significant. First major global terrorism event demonstrating hotel/hospitality sector vulnerability. [I3]

Insured Loss: ~$40m est. (hotels only)
2010–2011Product Innovation

Arab Spring — Political Violence Claims Drive RS/RW Growth

Uprisings across Tunisia, Egypt, Libya, Syria, Bahrain and Yemen produce significant political violence insurance claims for multinationals and project finance lenders. RS (riot & strike) and RW (revolution & war) codes see substantial growth in premium and claims. Political violence becomes a recognised mainstream product — previously a niche line. Capacity builds rapidly at Lloyd's. [I4]

2013Major Loss

Boston Marathon Bombing — USA

Tsarnaev brothers detonate two pressure-cooker bombs near Boston Marathon finish line. Three killed, 264 injured. Insured losses estimated at $350m across medical, liability, BI (street closures and city lockdown), event cancellation and property damage. Tests TRIA certification process — ultimately not TRIA-certified. Highlights BI loss beyond direct damage zone. [I3]

Insured Loss: ~$350m est.
2015Major Loss

Paris Bataclan & Coordinated Attacks — France

IS coordinated attacks across six locations in Paris including the Bataclan concert hall. 130 killed. Insured losses estimated at €400m+ across property, BI, casualty and event cancellation. GAREAT responds. Paris attacks are the single largest terrorism insured loss in Europe since 9/11. Event cancellation/contingency market severely affected. [I2]

Insured Loss: ~€400m est.
2015–2016Product Innovation

Active Assailant Product Emerges at Lloyd's

Following Paris, Nice, and Brussels attacks, Lloyd's syndicates develop standalone active assailant (AA) insurance covering mass casualty events at venues, hotels, retail and offices. AA cover provides BI, casualty management, crisis response and liability without requiring significant property damage trigger. Earliest AA wordings developed 2015–16; capacity grows rapidly. [I5]

2016Major Loss

Nice Bastille Day Vehicle Attack — France

Mohamed Lahouaiej-Bouhlel drives a heavy truck into crowds on the Promenade des Anglais during Bastille Day celebrations. 86 killed, 458 injured. Insured losses primarily from casualty and disruption. Nice is the archetypal "soft target" mass casualty event — no explosive device, no property damage trigger — that demonstrates the need for active assailant cover. [I5]

Primarily casualty and BI losses
2017Major Loss

Las Vegas Harvest Festival — Largest US Mass Shooting

Stephen Paddock opens fire from the Mandalay Bay Hotel on 22,000 concert-goers. 60 killed, 400+ injured. Insured losses estimated at $750m+ across active assailant, general liability, event cancellation, medical and workers' compensation. Largest mass shooting insurance loss in history. Defines active assailant as a mainstream Lloyd's product class. [I5]

Insured Loss: ~$750m+ est.
2017Major Loss

Manchester Arena Bombing — UK

Suicide bombing at Ariana Grande concert, Manchester Arena. 22 killed, 1,017 injured. Insured losses estimated at £100m across property, BI (arena closure), casualty and liability. Pool Re responds to property element. Landmark case on duty of care of venue operators to protect concert-goers from foreseeable terrorism risk. [L5]

Insured Loss: ~£100m est.
2019Regulatory / Government

TRIA Reauthorisation Act 2019 — Extended to 2027

Terrorism Risk Insurance Program Reauthorization Act of 2019 (Pub.L. 116-94) extends TRIA backstop to 31 December 2027. Provides long-term certainty for US terrorism insurance market. Certifying authority: Treasury Secretary jointly with Attorney General and Secretary of Homeland Security. Certification threshold: $5m minimum aggregate loss. [L2]

2020Legal / Coverage

COVID-19 — Man-Made Event / Terrorism Coverage Debate

Some policyholders attempt to claim COVID-19 as a biological terrorism event or government-mandated closure under terrorism/political violence wordings. Lloyd's and the market generally resist these arguments — terrorism requires intentional political violence; COVID was a natural pandemic. However, the debate clarified NBCR definitions and reinforced the need for explicit communicable disease exclusions in terrorism wordings. [L6]

2022Geopolitical

Ukraine War — War on Land (WL) Policies Activated

Russia's full-scale invasion of Ukraine on 24 February 2022 activates Lloyd's war on land (WL) policies for Ukraine-based commercial property and infrastructure. First major WL claims in the post-Cold War era. Infrastructure attacks — power stations, bridges, grain silos — generate property and BI claims. Lloyd's war on land aggregation management and PML modelling undergo fundamental review. [I6]

Insured loss: significant, ongoing (est. tens of billions)
2023Geopolitical

Israel / Hamas Conflict — Political Violence Claims

Hamas attacks on Israel (7 October 2023) and subsequent Israeli military operations in Gaza generate political violence, terrorism, and war on land insurance claims — particularly for multinationals and project insureds with Middle East exposure. Lloyd's RW and WL books face loss development. Regional political risk escalates across the Middle East, affecting TO/TW programme pricing. [I6]

Claims ongoing as of publication (May 2026)
