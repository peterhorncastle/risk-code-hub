Key ROE Risk Factor — Epidemic Correlated Losses: The defining actuarial characteristic of NB livestock insurance is the potential for catastrophically correlated losses from a single disease event. Unlike most casualty lines where losses are largely independent, a single FMD incursion in the UK or ASF introduction can simultaneously destroy the economic value of thousands of insured animals across hundreds of insured farms — all within a single policy year. This correlation means that a NB livestock portfolio can move from profitable to catastrophically unprofitable in weeks. Reinsurance structure must reflect this tail risk: aggregate excess of loss or catastrophic disease stop-loss protections are essential portfolio management tools. EST
