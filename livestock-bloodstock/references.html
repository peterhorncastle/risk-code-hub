Legal & Regulatory Sources
  • [L1]
    LEGAL Animal Health Act 1981 — UK Notifiable Disease Framework

    Animal Health Act 1981 (c. 22). The primary UK legislation governing animal disease control. Grants powers for: slaughter of diseased and contact animals; movement restrictions; disinfection requirements; compensation for compulsorily slaughtered animals. Schedule 3 lists notifiable diseases including Foot and Mouth Disease (FMD), Avian Influenza (Fowl Plague), African Swine Fever (ASF), Newcastle Disease and 30+ other pathogens. The compensation provisions define the boundary between government-funded loss recovery and the commercial livestock insurance market. Available at legislation.gov.uk.

  • [L2]
    LEGAL APHA — Contingency Plan for Exotic Notifiable Animal Disease (England)

    Animal and Plant Health Agency (APHA), Contingency Plan for Exotic Notifiable Animal Diseases in England (current version, updated 2023). Sets out the government's operational response plan for notifiable disease outbreaks including FMD, ASF and Avian Influenza. Defines Temporary Control Zones, Infected Areas, Protection Zones and Surveillance Zones — the geographic designations that directly affect which livestock farms are subject to movement restrictions and government culling orders, and therefore which farms' insurance policies are activated. Available at gov.uk/government/publications.

  • [L3]
    LEGAL Cattle Passports (England) Regulations 1999 — BCMS Traceability System

    The Cattle Passports (England) Regulations 1999 (SI 1999/1800), as amended. Require a cattle passport to be issued for every bovine animal born in England and to accompany the animal at all times. All movements must be registered with the British Cattle Movement Service (BCMS) within 3 days. Similar provisions apply in Scotland (SI 1999/1182), Wales (SI 1999/1848) and Northern Ireland. The BCMS database — holding records of 90m+ cattle movements — is the definitive traceability system underpinning livestock insurance claims verification and disease contact tracing.

  • [L4]
    LEGAL Welfare of Animals (Slaughter or Killing) Regulations 1995

    SI 1995/731. Governs methods of slaughter and killing of animals (including emergency killing). Defines the circumstances in which a veterinary surgeon may authorise humane destruction of an insured animal — a critical policy condition for NX bloodstock mortality claims. The Regulations require that animals are not caused unnecessary suffering during the slaughter process and specify approved methods for different species. Compliance with these Regulations is a condition of livestock insurance policies.

  • [L5]
    LEGAL Aquaculture and Inland Fisheries (Scotland) Act 2013 — Example State Aquaculture Legislation

    Aquaculture and Inland Fisheries (Scotland) Act 2013 (asp 4). Example of domestic aquaculture-specific legislation. Governs fish farm licensing, disease control requirements, monitoring and reporting. The Act requires fish farm operators to notify Marine Scotland of suspected notifiable aquatic diseases (including IHN, ISA, VHS). Comparable aquaculture disease legislation exists in England (under SI instruments), Norway (Aquaculture Act 2005) and Chile. Relevant to Lloyd's aquaculture NB underwriting — disease control obligations define the boundary of insurable and government-compensable losses.

  • [L6]
    LEGAL EU Animal Health Regulation 2016/429 — Single EU AHL Framework

    Regulation (EU) 2016/429 of the European Parliament and of the Council on transmissible animal diseases (Animal Health Law). Came into force 21 April 2021, replacing 40+ existing EU animal health Directives. Establishes a single harmonised framework for animal disease prevention, control and eradication across EU member states. Directly relevant for Lloyd's underwriters assessing NB livestock risks in EU member states (including Ireland, France, Germany) and for international bloodstock movement involving EU-based stallions and mares.

  • [L7]
    LEGAL Jockey Club Rules of Racing — Bloodstock Identification and Ownership

    The Jockey Club (UK) Rules of Racing (current edition), as administered by the British Horseracing Authority (BHA) since 2007. Governs horse identification (equine passport — mandatory for all horses in the UK under EU-derived legislation retained post-Brexit), ownership registration, trainer licensing and race entry procedures. The equine passport system — requiring DNA profile, microchip implant and markings diagram — underpins NX insurance policy identification of insured animals and claims verification. Relevant rules also apply to the General Stud Book (Weatherbys).

  • [L8]
    LEGAL Animal Welfare Act 2006 — Minimum Welfare Standards

    Animal Welfare Act 2006 (c. 45). Establishes the statutory duty of care for all animal keepers in England and Wales (comparable legislation in Scotland: Animal Health and Welfare (Scotland) Act 2006). The Act creates a positive duty to ensure the welfare of animals — the "five needs" framework. For livestock and bloodstock insurers, the Act's requirements establish minimum standards of care that are incorporated as policy conditions. Breach of the Act (wilful neglect, failure to seek veterinary care) may constitute grounds for claims rejection or policy avoidance.

  • [L9]
    LEGAL OIE (World Organisation for Animal Health) — Disease Reporting and Classification

    World Organisation for Animal Health (OIE, now WOAH — World Organisation for Animal Health since 2022), Terrestrial Animal Health Code (current edition); WAHIS (World Animal Health Information System) — real-time disease reporting database. OIE classifies animal diseases by their international trade and public health significance; lists notifiable diseases that member countries must report to WOAH. FMD, ASF, HPAI (avian flu), Rinderpest, Newcastle Disease and 100+ other conditions are listed. OIE disease-free status drives significant trade premiums — Australia's FMD-free status underpins its livestock export market. Available at woah.org.

Industry & Market Sources
  • [I1]
    INDUSTRY DEFRA — FMD 2001 Report and Lessons Learned (Anderson Inquiry 2002)

    Department for Environment, Food and Rural Affairs (DEFRA), Origin of the UK Foot and Mouth Disease Epidemic in 2001 (June 2002); Anderson, I., Foot and Mouth Disease 2001: Lessons to be Learned Inquiry (July 2002). The definitive official account of the FMD 2001 epidemic: 6 million animals slaughtered, £8bn total economic cost, insured losses est. £1.2bn. The Anderson Inquiry's recommendations on biosecurity, movement controls, contingency planning and compensation valuation methodology directly shaped subsequent livestock insurance market practices and Lloyd's NB policy development. Available at gov.uk archives.

  • [I2]
    INDUSTRY APHA — UK Avian Influenza Situation Reports 2021–2024

    Animal and Plant Health Agency (APHA), UK Avian Influenza Situation Reports (published fortnightly during active outbreaks 2021–2024). These reports document the unprecedented scale of HPAI H5N1 in UK domestic poultry — over 40 million birds culled by 2024, affecting every UK region and species category. The reports provide the authoritative data on premises affected, species culled, geographic spread and biosecurity findings that underpin Lloyd's NB poultry insurance claims assessment and premium adjustments. Available at gov.uk/government/publications/avian-influenza-in-england.

  • [I3]
    INDUSTRY FAO — African Swine Fever Global Impact Assessment 2020

    Food and Agriculture Organization of the United Nations (FAO), Global Assessment of African Swine Fever (ASF) Outbreak in China and Other Parts of Asia: Implications for Agricultural Markets (2020). Documents the devastating impact of ASF in China — estimated 200–300 million pigs dead, representing 50%+ of China's total pig population. Analyses knock-on effects on global pork prices (100%+ increase), feed grain markets, and pig sector reconstruction timelines. The definitive reference for Lloyd's NB pig underwriters assessing ASF global spread risk. Available at fao.org.

  • [I4]
    INDUSTRY Tattersalls — Thoroughbred Sales Statistics 2023

    Tattersalls Ltd, Annual Sales Results 2023 (Newmarket, 2024). Published annually; includes aggregate turnover, average price, median price, top lot and volume data for all Tattersalls sales including October Book 1 and Book 2 Yearling Sales, Guineas Horses in Training Sale and December Breeding Stock Sale. The top lot at October Book 1 2023 sold for 3.4m gns. Tattersalls data is the primary benchmark for NX yearling insurance agreed-value assessments in the UK and European markets. Available at tattersalls.com.

  • [I5]
    INDUSTRY BHA (British Horseracing Authority) — Racing and Breeding Statistics 2023

    British Horseracing Authority, Statistical Record 2023. Comprehensive data on UK racing: number of runners, fatalities on the racecourse (including cause), trainers, owners, jockeys and prize money data. The BHA racecourse fatality statistics are a key data source for NX mortality rate assessment for active racehorses — essential actuarial input for Lloyd's bloodstock syndicate rating models. Also includes Weatherbys General Stud Book (GSB) breeding registration data, which underpins equine passport identification. Available at britishhorseracing.com.

  • [I6]
    INDUSTRY Lloyd's — Bloodstock Market Bulletin and Capacity Guide

    Lloyd's of London, Bloodstock Insurance Market Overview (Lloyd's Market Association / LMA, periodic publication). Provides guidance on Lloyd's bloodstock market capacity, syndicate specialisms, and policy structure for the NX class. The Lloyd's bloodstock market is estimated at £200–300m GWP (est., not publicly disclosed at sub-class level). Lloyd's Market Association (LMA) Financial Lines and Agriculture working groups publish guidance on NX policy wordings, agreed-value basis, infertility cover conditions, and programme structure for international bloodstock operations. Available at lma.london.

  • [I7]
    INDUSTRY DEFRA / BCMS — Cattle Movement Statistics 2023/24

    Department for Environment, Food and Rural Affairs (DEFRA) / British Cattle Movement Service (BCMS), Cattle Tracing System Annual Statistics 2023/24. Published annually; includes total UK cattle population, movement volumes, herd sizes by region, and cattle registered under the Cattle Passport system. UK cattle population: approximately 9.5 million (2023). The BCMS statistics provide the population data underlying NB livestock insurance market sizing, claims frequency assessment and geographic accumulation analysis. Available at gov.uk.

  • [I8]
    INDUSTRY NOAH (National Office of Animal Health) — Livestock Animal Health Market Data

    National Office of Animal Health (NOAH), UK Animal Medicines Market Report (annual). Data on animal medicine expenditure by species — cattle, sheep, pigs, poultry — as a proxy indicator of veterinary care investment and disease burden. Vaccination product sales data indicates the penetration of preventive health programmes across UK livestock sectors. Relevant to NB underwriting assessment of veterinary care quality and disease prevention investment at farm level. Available at noah.co.uk.

  • [I9]
    INDUSTRY GlobalAg Risk Solutions — Livestock Insurance Market Overview 2023

    GlobalAg Risk Solutions, Global Agricultural Insurance Market Overview 2023. Provides international market data on agricultural insurance including livestock mortality, aquaculture and crop. Global livestock insurance GWP estimated at $15–20bn (dominated by government-subsidised crop/multi-peril schemes); commercial non-subsidised livestock mortality (NB/NX equivalent) estimated at $2–3bn globally. Identifies key growth markets: aquaculture (Asia-Pacific), commercial poultry (USA, Brazil, EU), bloodstock (UK/Ireland, USA). Available at globalagrisolutions.com.

  • [I10]
    INDUSTRY Keeneland Association — Annual Sales Results and Market Analysis 2023

    Keeneland Association, 2023 September Yearling Sale Results (Lexington, KY, 2023). Annual Keeneland September Yearling Sale is the world's largest thoroughbred yearling sale by volume: 3,500+ yearlings offered; aggregate turnover $350m+. Top lot 2023: son of Justify sold for $4.1m. Keeneland data is the primary benchmark for NX yearling insurance agreed-value assessments in the North American market. The Keeneland/Tattersalls price correlation underpins transatlantic bloodstock valuation comparisons used by Lloyd's underwriters. Available at keeneland.com.
