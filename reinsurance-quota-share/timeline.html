Financial / structured quota share
Capital Insurers use quota share deliberately for capital relief; the line into finite reinsurance blurs. EST
2005
KRW hurricanes reprice proportional cat exposure
Market Event limits and loss corridors tighten on proportional treaties with catastrophe-exposed books. VER
2016
Solvency II in force
Regulatory Formalises the SCR-reducing capital value of a quota share — central to insurer capital planning. VER
2018–23
MGA & fronting boom
MGA Delegated underwriting makes quota share the plumbing of fronting; ceding commissions become the key term. VER
2023
Hard market squeezes ceding commissions
Market After Ian and years of loss creep, reinsurers cut ceding commissions and added loss corridors on proportional treaties. VER
2023
Vesttoo collateral fraud
MGA Forged letters of credit backing fronted quota shares (~$1.3–4bn) expose collateral risk in delegated capacity. VER
2025–26
Softening — ceding commissions recover
Market Record capital and competition begin to lift ceding commissions again as the cycle turns. EST
