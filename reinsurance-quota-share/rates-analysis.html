Quota Share — Rates & ROE

Quota share has no rate-on-line — its cycle is the ceding commission. When commissions rise, reinsurer margins compress; when they fall, margins widen. Here is that cycle and what it means for return on the proportional book.

The Commission Cycle in Words

Soft market

Commissions rise

Reinsurers compete for premium and capacity is abundant; ceding commissions climb to 35%+ and loss corridors disappear — good for cedants, thin for reinsurers.

Hard market (2023)

Commissions cut, corridors added

After losses destroy capital, reinsurers cut ceding commissions, widen loss corridors and tighten event limits — restoring proportional margins.

Sliding scale

The aligned middle

A sliding-scale commission moves the terms with the actual loss ratio automatically, dampening the cycle for both sides.
