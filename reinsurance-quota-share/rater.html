Price a quota share treaty by cession economics. Enter the book, the loss-ratio view and the commissions; the rater returns the reinsurer's technical result, combined ratio, the profit commission and the result after it. Inputs update live — indicative for training.

How it prices Ceded premium = GNPI × cession. Result = ceded premium − ceded losses − ceding commission − brokerage. A profit commission then returns a share of the treaty profit (after a reinsurer expense allowance) to the cedant. The combined ratio is (losses + ceding commission + brokerage) ÷ ceded premium.
