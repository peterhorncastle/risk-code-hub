Quota Share — Market & Loss Database

Proportional treaties don't have "layers" that a single event pierces — instead they are stressed by loss-ratio deterioration, catastrophe accumulation and commission cycles. These are the episodes that moved proportional terms.

Reading the database Because quota share cedes the whole loss ratio, the reinsurer's protection against a bad year is not a per-event cap but the commission structure — the sliding scale, the loss corridor and the event limit. Every episode above shows those levers moving. The Contract & Wordings module explains each.
