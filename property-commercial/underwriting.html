Rating Formula — Commercial Property

Commercial property premiums are built from a base rate applied to the total insured value (TIV), then adjusted for risk-specific factors. Cat-exposed risks require a separate catastrophe loading derived from probabilistic modelling (PML/EML), not just a flat factor.

Premium = TIV × Base Rate
× Construction Factor
× Occupancy Factor
× Protection Factor
× Cat Loading (PML/EML-driven)
× Claims Experience Loading
× Deductible Credit
+ BI Premium (separate rated on MIP and gross profit)

COPE — Detailed Rating Factors

🏗️ Construction — Base Rate Multiplier

Frame (wood): 1.5–2.5× base — highest fire/wind susceptibility
Joisted Masonry: 1.0–1.5× base
Masonry Non-Combustible: 0.7–1.0× base
Modified Fire Resistive: 0.5–0.75× base
Fire Resistive: 0.3–0.6× base — RC/steel frame with sprinklers
Roof age >20 years: +10–25% loading; ACM cladding post-Grenfell: +15–40%

🏭 Occupancy — Hazard Class Loading

Low hazard (offices, retail): base rate
Medium (light manufacturing, warehousing): +20–60%
High (chemicals, plastics, food processing): +80–200%
Special hazard (pyrotechnics, explosives): declination or specialist placement
Hot work operations: +15–30%; Unoccupancy: +20–50% or warranty required

🔒 Protection — Discount/Loading

NFPA 13 sprinklers throughout: -20 to -40%
Sprinklers partial / none: flat or loading
24-hr watchman: -5 to -10%
Intruder alarm (Grade 2+): -3 to -8%
BAFE-contracted maintenance: -5%
Remote fire station (>10 miles): +15–30% on all-perils

🌊 Exposure — Cat Loading

CRESTA zone assignment: drives PML percentage applied to TIV
Flood zone: FEMA Zone AE (1-in-100 year): +30–80% or sublimit
Environment Agency Zone 3: UK flood high risk: +25–60%
WUI wildfire score: Verisk FireLine >20: significant loading or exclusion
Earthquake MMI zone: MMI VII+ triggers cat model PML calculation

📊 PML / EML Assessment

PML (Probable Maximum Loss): Maximum loss from single foreseeable event; typically 50-year or 100-year return period from RMS/AIR model
EML (Estimated Maximum Loss): Loss assuming most of the risk is damaged; less probabilistic, more physical — used on industrial risks lacking model data
ILF (Increased Limit Factor): Applied to uplift base rate for higher policy limits beyond the attachment

Critical Underwriting Checks — Before Binding

  • Underinsurance audit: Verify TIV against independent rebuild cost valuations — construction cost inflation 2020–2024 ran at 8–12% p.a. in UK. Day-One reinstatement election required for all UK risks over £1m TIV.
  • Cyber physical damage exclusion: Mandatory for all Lloyd's property risks from 1 January 2023. Confirm LMA5564 or equivalent endorsement is attached.
  • Communicable disease exclusion: Post-COVID, all BI covers must have explicit communicable disease exclusion. Verify wording.
  • PFAS exclusion: For risks with chemical/manufacturing occupancy; LMA standard PFAS exclusion being adopted across market.
  • Terrorism: Pool Re/TRIA participation confirmed for UK/US risks. Standalone terrorism cover structured separately.
  • Average / co-insurance: If sum insured is below actual rebuild cost, average clause operates — insured bears pro-rata shortfall at every claim. Day-One reinstatement clause preferred.
