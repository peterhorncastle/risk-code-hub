Primary Sources

Legislation & Regulation

1
REGTerrorism Risk Insurance Act (TRIA) 2002 — as reauthorised 2019

US Federal backstop for certified acts of terrorism. Mandatory insurer participation; 20% co-share above deductible. Governs all P5 US commercial property terrorism coverage. Public Law 107-297.

VER
2
REGNational Flood Insurance Program (NFIP) — FEMA, USA

US government flood insurance programme; FEMA Flood Insurance Rate Maps (FIRMs) define flood zones for rating purposes. Write-Your-Own programme; critical for US P5 property exposure assessment.

VER
3
REGNational Planning Policy Framework (NPPF) — UK Government 2023

UK planning policy; flood risk sequential test and exception test; Flood Risk Assessment requirements for development in Zone 2 and 3. Critical for commercial property risk location assessment.

VER
4
REGInsurance Act 2015 (UK)

Duty of fair presentation of risk; replaced Marine Insurance Act 1906 for commercial property. Knowledge requirements; proportionate remedies for non-disclosure. Governs all P2/P3/B2/B3 UK commercial risks.

VER
5
REGFinancial Services and Markets Act 2000 (FSMA) — Pool Re Reinsurance Provisions

Statutory basis for Pool Re (Pool Reinsurance Company Ltd) — UK government-backed terrorism reinsurer. All UK commercial property policies now offer Pool Re terrorism extension.

VER

Case Law

6
CASEFCA v Arch Insurance UK Ltd & Others [2021] UKSC 1

UK Supreme Court — COVID-19 business interruption test case. Ruled on disease clauses, denial of access clauses and hybrid wordings. Landmark for BI coverage interpretation; ~£1.2bn industry payout; all BI wordings rewritten.

VER
7
CASEMondelez International v Zurich American Insurance (Illinois, settled 2022)

NotPetya cyber attack property claim. Mondelez claimed $100m under all-risks property policy; Zurich invoked war exclusion. Settled confidentially 2022; drove LMA5564 cyber exclusion adoption.

VER
8
CASEOrient-Express Hotels v Assicurazioni Generali [2010] Lloyd's Rep IR 472

Hurricane Katrina BI claim; "but for" test for BI losses — hotel could not trade regardless of its own damage given area devastation. Changed BI wording approach; area damage clauses revised.

VER

Industry Standards & Guidance

9
STDNFPA 13 — Standard for the Installation of Sprinkler Systems (2022 ed.)

US/global benchmark for automatic fire suppression system design and installation. Reference for all construction and occupancy-based sprinkler requirements in commercial property underwriting.

VER
10
STDLMA5564 — Cyber Physical Damage and Cyber Business Interruption Exclusion Clause

Lloyd's Market Association standard cyber exclusion clause; mandatory for all Lloyd's property risks from 1 January 2023. Eliminates silent cyber exposure from property portfolio.

VER
11
STDISR — Industrial Special Risks Wording (Australian/Lloyd's market form)

All-risks property wording developed in Australia; widely used in Lloyd's market as benchmark for large commercial all-risks policies. Covers material damage and BI on reinstatement basis.

VER
12
STDISO 22301 — Business Continuity Management Systems (BSI, 2019)

International BCM standard. ISO 22301 certification typically attracts BI premium credit; reduces realistic maximum indemnity period; demonstrates documented recovery capability.

VER
13
STDLloyd's Realistic Disaster Scenarios (RDS) — Annual Syndicate Requirement

Lloyd's mandated scenarios: US Gulf hurricane, US earthquake, European windstorm, Japanese earthquake, pandemic, cyber. Syndicates must model and report aggregate exposures; drives cat budget management.

VER
14
STDBS EN 12845 — Fixed Firefighting Systems: Automatic Sprinkler Systems

European equivalent of NFPA 13; governs sprinkler design in UK/EU commercial buildings. Required for Lloyd's protection discount eligibility alongside BAFE SP203-1 contractor maintenance.

VER
15
INDMunich Re NatCat Service — Annual Natural Catastrophe Statistics

Munich Re publishes annual global nat cat statistics used throughout this hub (2023 $118bn insured, historical event losses). Primary industry source for cat loss data.

VER
16
INDSwiss Re sigma — World Insurance Report (Annual)

Swiss Re sigma provides global insurance GWP, combined ratio and nat cat data; key source for market size estimates, combined ratios and premium volume statistics across P/B codes.

VER
17
INDLloyd's Annual Report — Market Results (2023)

Lloyd's Annual Report publishes property combined ratios, GWP by class and cat event impact. Source for Lloyd's property GWP estimates (~£9bn P/B codes) and combined ratio narrative.

VER
18
INDVerisk (AIR Worldwide) — CLASIC/2 and Touchstone Cat Models

AIR Worldwide probabilistic cat models; used for US hurricane, earthquake, European windstorm PML calculations. One of two primary cat models used at Lloyd's alongside RMS (Moody's).

VER
19
INDRMS (Moody's) — RiskLink Platform

RMS probabilistic cat models; primary platform for many Lloyd's syndicates. 2023 v23 model suite includes US hurricane, earthquake, Japan quake, European flood and wildfire models.

VER
20
INDRICS Reinstatement Cost Assessments — Building Cost Information Service (BCIS)

UK benchmark for commercial property rebuild cost valuations. BCIS construction cost indices used for Day-One reinstatement uplift factors. RICS Red Book governs independent valuation methodology.

Known Data Gaps

  • GAP 1 — Syndicate-specific property rates: Actual rate-per-unit figures are commercially sensitive. All rates in the Underwriting and Rates modules are indicative ranges (ILLUS) derived from market publications, not proprietary syndicate data. GAP
  • GAP 2 — Lloyd's P-code breakdown by GWP: Lloyd's does not publish GWP split by individual P/B sub-codes. The ~£9bn estimate for aggregate P/B property codes is derived from Lloyd's Annual Report total property segment plus broker estimates. GAP
  • GAP 3 — Captive frequency data: No comprehensive published dataset on what proportion of Lloyd's large commercial property risks involve captive integration. Captive statistics are held by CICA (Captive Insurance Companies Association) and Willis Towers Watson captive surveys but require subscription access. GAP
  • GAP 4 — PFAS property loss experience: PFAS contamination as a property exclusion driver is very recent (2023–2024). No published loss statistics yet exist for PFAS property claims. Gap will close as 3M/DuPont litigation resolves and property claims data emerges. GAP
  • GAP 5 — EV / BESS fire loss statistics: Formal loss statistics for lithium-ion battery / BESS-related commercial property fires are not yet compiled by Lloyd's or the wider market. Data exists at incident level (NFPA, London Fire Brigade) but no commercial property claims data published at market level. GAP
