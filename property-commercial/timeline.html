2001LOSS

9/11 World Trade Center — Largest Man-Made Insured Loss

~$47bn insured loss from attacks on WTC, Pentagon and United 93. VER Property losses ~$11bn. Immediate market hardening; terrorism exclusions added across all property policies; Pool Re extended; TRIA signed into law (Nov 2002). Largest property cat in Lloyd's history to that date.

2002REG

TRIA — Terrorism Risk Insurance Act (USA)

US Federal backstop for certified acts of terrorism; insurer mandatory participation; 20% co-share above deductible; reauthorised 2005, 2007, 2015, 2019. VER Stabilised US property market; Lloyd's P5 risks now routinely include TRIA certification language.

2005LOSS

Hurricane Katrina — $36bn Insured, $85bn Total

New Orleans levee failures; 1,800 deaths; largest-ever US insured cat loss at that time. VER Flood vs wind coverage disputes; NFIP stressed to breaking point; cat model recalibration by RMS and AIR; Lloyd's Realistic Disaster Scenarios (RDS) updated for US hurricane. Triggered immediate 40%+ rate increases on US property.

2007MKT

Lloyd's Franchise Performance Directorate — RDS Mandatory

Lloyd's required all syndicates to model Realistic Disaster Scenarios and report aggregate exposures. VER Introduced US Gulf hurricane, US earthquake, European windstorm and Japanese earthquake as mandatory RDS events. Standardised aggregate reporting across the market; basis for SYNdicates' catastrophe budget management.

2010LOSS

Haiti Earthquake — $7bn Total, <5% Insured

7.0 magnitude; 220,000 deaths; $7bn+ total loss; insured losses under $300m — extreme insurance gap. VER Demonstrated the global property protection gap; led to parametric insurance development for sovereign risk in Caribbean. World Bank Caribbean Catastrophe Risk Insurance Facility (CCRIF) paid out within 14 days.

2011LOSS

Thailand Floods — Supply Chain BI Aggregation Exposed

Chao Phraya basin industrial zones submerged for 3 months; $43bn total loss, ~$12bn insured. VER Japanese manufacturers (Toyota, Honda, Western Digital) suffered far greater losses from supply chain BI than their direct property damage. Triggered fundamental reunderwriting of contingent BI, supplier extensions and maximum indemnity periods.

2011LOSS

Tōhoku Earthquake & Tsunami — Japan

9.0 magnitude; Fukushima nuclear disaster; $235bn total loss, $35bn insured. VER Largest earthquake loss in history by total economic cost. Nuclear exclusion clauses tested. Fire following earthquake losses exceeded direct quake damage in some zones. Triggered Japanese market rate increases of 50%+.

2012LOSS

Hurricane Sandy — $65bn Total, $20bn Insured

Category 1 at US landfall but storm surge unprecedented; flooded lower Manhattan and New Jersey. VER $20bn insured but $45bn uninsured — flood gap in NFIP again exposed. Business interruption from power outages (non-damage BI) litigated widely. FEMA flood map updates accelerated.

2014TECH

RMS v11/v17 US Hurricane Model — Cat Model Revolution

RMS released updated US hurricane models showing materially higher expected losses for Florida and Gulf Coast; multiple syndicates revised upwards their PML estimates by 15–30%. EST Demonstrated model risk as a systemic underwriting factor; Lloyd's began requiring multiple-model blend disclosure.

2017LOSS

Harvey / Irma / Maria — Record $43bn Insured Hurricane Season

Harvey: $30bn insured (Houston flooding); Irma: $30bn (Florida Keys/Caribbean); Maria: $30bn (Puerto Rico). VER Combined 2017 cat losses ~$135bn insured — record year. Puerto Rico suffered catastrophic infrastructure failure; BI losses enormous. Market hardened sharply into 2018/2019.

2018CLIMATE

Camp Fire (Paradise, CA) — Deadliest & Costliest Wildfire

Camp Fire destroyed Paradise, CA; 85 deaths; $12.5bn insured. VER PG&E liability (~$13.5bn settlement); multiple Californian insurers withdrew from homeowners market; WUI expansion widely recognised as systemic risk driver. Wildfire moved from secondary peril to primary market concern for California E&S property.

2019REG

NotPetya — Mondelez v Zurich War Exclusion Dispute

NotPetya ransomware (2017) caused $10bn+ global damage; Mondelez claimed $100m under its all-risks property policy; Zurich invoked war exclusion. VER Settled confidentially 2022. Market accelerated adoption of Lloyd's Market Association (LMA) cyber physical damage exclusion clauses (LMA5564 and equivalents), separating "silent cyber" from property covers.

2020–22LOSS

COVID-19 BI Test Case — FCA v Arch (UKSC 2021)

UK Supreme Court (Jan 2021) ruled in favour of policyholders on disease clause, denial of access and hybrid wordings. VER ~£1.2bn paid UK; global COVID BI settlements estimated $40–50bn. All BI wordings rewritten to expressly exclude communicable disease. Maximum indemnity periods and non-damage BI triggers fundamentally repriced.

2022REG

Lloyd's Cyber Physical Damage Exclusion — Mandatory from Jan 2023

Lloyd's mandated that all property policies must include a clear cyber physical damage exclusion from 1 January 2023. VER Eliminated silent cyber exposure from property portfolio; standalone cyber market grew in response. LMA produced standard exclusion endorsements (LMA5564, LMA5400 et al.).

2022LOSS

Hurricane Ian — $60bn Insured; Florida Market Crisis

Category 4 landfall at Fort Myers, FL; $60bn insured — second-largest ever US cat event. VER Six Florida domestic insurers became insolvent. Citizens Insurance insured exposure reached $1.3tn. Assignment of benefits (AOB) legislation reformed 2022. Lloyd's Florida property market significantly thinned; capacity withdrawn from coastal risks.

2023LOSS

Turkey–Syria Earthquake — 50,000 Deaths, $7bn Insured

7.8 magnitude Kahramanmaraş earthquake; 50,000+ deaths; $90bn total loss, $7bn insured — 8% penetration. VER DASK (Turkish Catastrophe Insurance Pool) paid approximately TRY 35bn. Exposed the massive protection gap in middle-income countries. Lloyd's P6 international property writers faced attritional losses from Turkish commercial accounts.

2023CLIMATE

Record Nat Cat Year — $118bn Insured Losses

$118bn globally insured nat cat in 2023 — third consecutive year above $100bn. VER Severe Convective Storms (SCS — hail, tornado, derecho) accounted for ~60% of losses. European floods (Slovenia, Italy), Maui wildfire ($5.5bn), Australia cyclones. SCS elevated from secondary to primary peril in North America by all major reinsurers.

2023REG

PFAS "Forever Chemical" Contamination — Emerging Property Exclusion

3M agreed $10.3bn PFAS water contamination settlement; DuPont/Chemours $1.19bn. VER Property insurers began adding PFAS exclusions to commercial policies; contaminated land and buildings represent latent property exposure. Lloyd's LMA developing standard PFAS exclusion language for property portfolio (2024).

2023–24TECH

Parametric Property Insurance — Lloyd's Parametric Facility Launch

Lloyd's launched dedicated parametric facility; Ariel Re, Descartes Underwriting, FloodFlash among active parametric property underwriters. EST Parametric triggers (earthquake intensity, flood depth, wind speed) remove claims adjustment entirely; basis risk remains key limitation. AXA XL, Zurich and Munich Re deploying parametric capacity for nat cat protection gaps.

2024CLIMATE

Market Hardening Plateau — Climate Stress Testing Begins

Lloyd's Realistic Disaster Scenarios updated to include climate-adjusted hurricane and flood scenarios for 2024 renewals. VER PRA (Prudential Regulation Authority) CBES (Climate Biennial Exploratory Scenario) results published; syndicates required to model 1.5°C, 2°C and 3.3°C warming pathways. Reinsurers began non-renewal notices on Florida coastal risks above 5% roof pitch in zones >12m from water.
