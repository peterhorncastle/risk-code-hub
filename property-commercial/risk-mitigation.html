Fire Risk Controls

🔥 Fire Suppression Systems

  • NFPA 13 / BS EN 12845 automatic sprinklers — most effective single risk improvement (reduces fire loss by 60–90%) VER
  • System design: wet pipe (standard), dry pipe (cold zones), deluge (high hazard), foam (chemicals/aviation fuels)
  • Annual BS 9251 / NFPA 25 maintenance; BAFE SP203-1 contractor required for Lloyd's discount eligibility
  • Sprinkler impairment procedures: isolations >4hrs require hot-work permit and fire watch
  • Fire detection: Addressable fire alarm (category L1) linked to central monitoring station (Grade A, BS 5839-1)

🏗️ Fire Compartmentation & Construction

  • Compartmentation: maximum 2,000m² fire compartments in warehouses without suppression
  • Fire doors: FD30S (30-min) for corridors; FD60S (60-min) on stairwells and protected routes
  • Cavity barriers in voids and roof spaces — especially critical for post-2000 commercial builds with large unchecked voids
  • ACM cladding remediation: EWS1 forms now required for mortgaged commercial buildings >11m (UK)
  • Smoke extraction systems: BS EN 12101 compliant for covered malls and large industrial spaces

🔧 Hot Works & Ignition Control

  • Hot-work permit system mandatory for welding, cutting, grinding — most common cause of large commercial fire losses
  • 30-minute fire watch after hot work completion; 1-hour for high-risk areas
  • Electrical testing: EICR (Electrical Installation Condition Report) every 5 years for commercial premises
  • PAT testing: portable appliance testing annually for higher-risk occupancies
  • Arson prevention: CCTV, perimeter security lighting, vacant-area management

⚡ EV / Battery Storage — Emerging Fire Risk

  • Lithium-ion battery thermal runaway: uncontrollable and highly toxic; sprinklers cannot suppress — only contain and cool
  • EV charging in carparks: Lloyd's market applying +50–200% loading or exclusion depending on construction and suppression
  • BESS (Battery Energy Storage Systems): dedicated fire suppression (gaseous or water mist); NFPA 855 standard
  • Insurers increasingly requiring thermal management system certificates and separation distances for BESS risks

Flood Risk Controls

🌊 Physical Flood Barriers

  • Demountable flood barriers (FloodSax, AquaFence): effective for short-term flood defence of known entry points
  • Door guards and threshold barriers: prevent surface water entry; must seal to 30cm above design flood level
  • Flood-resistant construction: suspended ground floors; waterproof render to DPC; flood-resistant insulation
  • Sump pumps: should be on separate power circuits with backup generator in flood-prone zones
  • Environment Agency/SEPA flood warning registration: FWD (Flood Warning Direct) 24hr advance notice

📋 Business Continuity — Flood Response

  • Property-level flood response plan (PFRP): pre-identified actions, contact lists, movable asset schedule
  • Stock elevation: ground-floor stock moved 600mm+ above design flood level or stored in mezzanine
  • Server/electrical panel elevation: critical IT above flood level; waterproofed electrical distribution boards
  • Salvage contractors: pre-arrangement with Polygon/ServiceMaster or equivalent; reduces BI indemnity period by weeks
  • Flood risk assessment (FRA): required for any new build or extension in EA Zone 2 or 3 (NPPF)

Nat Cat Resilience — Wind, Earthquake, Wildfire

🌀 Hurricane / Windstorm Hardening

  • Impact-resistant glazing (ASTM E1996 / Miami-Dade TAS): significant loss reduction on US coastal risks
  • Hurricane straps and tie-downs on roof structure: prevents uplift; Florida Building Code requirements
  • Roof covering rated for 150mph+ (Owens Corning Duration / GAF Timberline) for Florida E&S placement
  • Roof age <10 years: generally required for preferred terms on FL coastal risks; >20yr roof: declination or ACV basis
  • Opening protection plan: inventory of all openings; pre-storm plywood/shutter deployment procedure

🏚️ Earthquake Seismic Risk Controls

  • Seismic retrofit: soft-story buildings (California) require mandatory retrofit by 2040; significant loss reduction
  • Anchoring: water heaters, racking, plant machinery secured to prevent fall/tip damage
  • Non-structural mitigation: securing suspended ceilings, IT equipment, shelving — often exceeds structural damage
  • Post-earthquake inspection protocol: do not re-enter until structural engineer clearance
  • USGS ShakeMap: real-time shaking intensity; enables immediate triage of affected portfolio locations

🔥 Wildfire Defensible Space

  • Zone 0–5ft: ember-resistant vents; non-combustible materials immediately against structure
  • Zone 1 (0–30ft): clearance of combustible vegetation; no wood mulch within 5ft of building
  • Zone 2 (30–100ft): 10ft spacing between tree crowns; horizontal clearance under canopies
  • Ember exposure: NFPA 1144 — most structures ignited by ember accumulation, not direct flame contact
  • Metal or Class A roof covering: single most effective structural wildfire mitigation measure VER
  • Verisk FireLine score >15: triggers Lloyd's underwriter referral; >24: significant loading or declination

Business Interruption — Resilience Planning (Post-COVID Rewriting)

  • Supply chain mapping: Identify single-source suppliers and geographic concentration. Post-Thailand 2011 and COVID, tier-2 and tier-3 supplier concentration in single flood/quake zones is a material BI aggregation risk.
  • Maximum Indemnity Period (MIP): Must reflect realistic rebuild, regulatory approval and refit time — not aspirational recovery time. UK commercial builds now average 24–36 months with planning consent. MIP of 12 months almost certainly inadequate for any complex occupancy.
  • Contingent BI triggers: Post-COVID, insurers require explicit named-location contingent BI triggers. Unnamed/blanket contingent BI extensions are being removed or priced as standalone covers.
  • Non-damage BI: Access denial without physical damage; denial of access by authority — these triggers remain contentious. Explicit wording required; pandemic and communicable disease excluded universally since 2021.
  • Alternative trading: Pre-agreed alternative trading locations, remote working capability, and dual-source supply agreements reduce BI loss and shorten indemnity period. Underwriters grant credit for documented BCM (Business Continuity Management) — BS ISO 22301.

Top 10 Underwriter Actions — Property Risk Improvement

  1. Require independent reinstatement valuation: Construction cost inflation 2020–2024 has created endemic underinsurance. Day-One reinstatement clause election should be mandatory for UK commercial risks >£1m TIV.
  2. Verify NFPA 13 / BS EN 12845 sprinkler coverage: Confirm scope, test records and last impairment log. Partial coverage is rated as unsprinklered for uncovered areas — ensure policy wording reflects this accurately.
  3. Attach cyber physical damage exclusion: Lloyd's mandatory since 1 Jan 2023. Confirm LMA5564 or equivalent wording. Do not bind without it.
  4. Attach communicable disease BI exclusion: FCA test case wording post-2021. Confirm disease clause and denial of access wording excludes communicable disease / pandemic.
  5. Review BI indemnity period vs rebuild time: Obtain realistic rebuild estimate from a quantity surveyor; compare to elected MIP. Flag underinsurance risk to insured and document.
  6. Assess flood zone and request PFRP: For EA Zone 2 or 3 / FEMA AE zone risks, require Property Flood Resilience Plan and evidence of barrier maintenance. Confirm sump pumps are operational.
  7. Check hot-work permit procedures: Most common ignition source for large commercial fire losses. Require sight of permit system, evidence of BAFE contractor maintenance, and last fire alarm test certificate.
  8. Apply CRESTA zone cat model loading: Do not rate US cat-exposed risks without obtaining RMS/AIR PML outputs. Florida CRESTA zone PML should drive rating not generic loadings.
  9. Review ACM / cladding for UK commercial: Post-Grenfell, ACM cladding on buildings >11m requires EWS1 assessment. Loading of 15–40% for unresolved cladding or apply exclusion.
  10. EV / BESS fire risk assessment: Ask insureds about EV charging installations and battery energy storage. Apply loading or exclusion for BESS without dedicated gaseous suppression or NFPA 855 compliance.
