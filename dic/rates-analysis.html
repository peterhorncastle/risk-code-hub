IBNR & Development Considerations
FactorImpact on Reserves
NatCat DIC claims coordination delayDIC claims cannot be quantified until local admitted claim is settled — typical delay 6–18 months post-loss. IBNR must reflect this lag
DIC trigger disputesLocal insurer may dispute DIC trigger (arguing local policy does respond); litigation extends development to 3–5 years in some markets
Financial lines DIC long tailD&O and PI DIC claims have 5–7 year development tails; financial lines DIC IBNR typically larger than property DIC as % of premium
Currency translation at settlementDIC claims settled in local currency may translate to higher or lower master currency amounts depending on FX movements — reserve in master currency
Fronting carrier recovery riskWhere local fronting carrier fails to reinsure to master (BEPS or regulatory issue), DIC settlement costs may not be recoverable — contingency reserve appropriate
Follow-the-settlements upsideWhere local insurer settles claims quickly and DIC follows, settlement speed reduces IBNR uncertainty — programmes with strong claims cooperation clauses develop faster
