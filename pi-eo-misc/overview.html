What Is PI E&O Miscellaneous?

The PI E&O Miscellaneous hub covers the broad catch-all segment of the professional indemnity market — the professions that do not fall neatly into the more specialised Lloyd's PI classes (legal/accountants E2–E5, technology F4/F5, construction E6/E7, or financial institutions F2/F3). This is one of the largest and most heterogeneous classes in the Lloyd's market, spanning management consultants, insurance brokers, real estate agents, recruiters, HR advisers, designers, media and PR professionals, healthcare non-medical practitioners, event managers, travel agents, and dozens of other professional service categories.

All four codes operate on a claims-made basis, with the retroactive date being the single most critical policy term. Despite the heterogeneity, all sub-classes share the same fundamental liability trigger: a negligent act, error or omission in the course of delivering professional services that causes a third party a financial loss.

E8 — Miscellaneous PI (Direct)

Direct placement of professional indemnity for miscellaneous professions not covered by other PI Lloyd's codes. Principal classes: management consultants, insurance intermediaries, real estate agents/surveyors, HR/recruitment consultants, training providers, and other professional service firms.

E9 — Miscellaneous PI (Binder/XOL)

Binder and excess-of-loss treaty arrangements for miscellaneous PI. Includes delegated underwriting authority agreements with MGA coverholder partners writing miscellaneous PI portfolios, plus reinsurance of primary miscellaneous PI carriers.

ED — Design Professionals

Professional indemnity for design professions: architects (non-construction-contractor), interior designers, graphic/brand designers, industrial/product designers, urban planners, landscape architects, and creative agencies. Covers design errors, IP infringement allegations, and specification failures.

EE — Miscellaneous E&O

Errors and omissions liability for service providers adjacent to the US E&O market: employee benefits consultants, customs brokers, freight forwarders (professional liability element), travel agents, media/advertising agencies, and other service industries where "errors and omissions" is the market convention over "professional indemnity".

Claims-Made Caveat: All PI E&O miscellaneous policies respond to claims first made during the policy period (or extended reporting period), irrespective of when the alleged negligent act occurred — provided the act post-dates the retroactive date. Underwriters must scrutinise retroactive date continuity; any gap in cover creates an uninsured period that typically leads to disputes at the claims stage.

Standard Coverage Features

Core Insuring Agreement

Pays civil liability arising from a negligent act, error or omission in the performance of professional services. Includes legal defence costs (typically within limit on Lloyd's forms; some London market wordings provide costs in addition).

Extensions (Standard)

Dishonesty of employees (sub-limited); intellectual property infringement defence (sub-limited); libel and slander arising from professional services; data protection liability (cyber overlap); court attendance costs; run-off tail protection.

Standard Exclusions

Bodily injury / property damage (to GL); known circumstances at inception; insolvency of the insured; fraudulent or dishonest acts by principals; war / terrorism; contractual liability beyond civil liability; prior known circumstances without disclosure.

Retroactive Date

The single most commercially critical term. Full prior acts (no retroactive date) preferred by insureds. Underwriters typically grant full prior acts for clean risks at renewal; inception retroactive dates applied for unknown/gap histories. Any breach in continuity creates an uninsured window.
