Landmark Cases & Market Events

Hedley Byrne & Co v Heller [1964]

House of Lords establishes that a duty of care for negligent misstatement exists where a "special relationship" of reasonable reliance exists between advisor and advisee.

Insurance significance: Creates the legal foundation for PI liability for all advice-giving professions. Without this case, most E8/ED/EE PI claims would be legally unrecoverable.

Murphy v Brentwood DC [1990]

House of Lords narrows Anns: pure economic loss from building defects is generally irrecoverable except against the professional who gave negligent design advice causing physical damage to "other property".

Insurance significance: Restates the boundary between construction PI (E6/E7) and design PI (ED). Confirms design professional exposure for advice-related losses while limiting property damage extension.

UK Pensions Mis-Selling Review (1994–2000)

FSA-mandated review of pension transfer advice given 1988–1994. £11.8bn total redress. Insurance broker and IFA PI carriers absorb significant claims from advisers unable to fund compensation.

Insurance significance: The defining event for E8 insurance broker/IFA PI sub-class. Established that mass-advice retail businesses carry systemic aggregation risk; drove mandatory PI requirements for FCA-regulated advisers.

Arthur Andersen / Enron (2001–2002)

Andersen's collapse following Enron triggers advisory PI scrutiny globally. Management consultants and advisory firms providing governance, valuation, or restructuring opinions face notification waves.

Insurance significance: Demonstrated that management consulting PI can have catastrophic-level systemic exposure when a large advisory firm is implicated in a major corporate failure. Drove E8 management consultant pricing increases 2002–2006.

FCA Business Interruption Test Case (2020–2021)

FCA brings test case against eight insurers on BI coverage triggers. Supreme Court broadly rules in favour of policyholders on disease and prevention of access clauses.

Insurance significance: Generated a wave of insurance broker PI notifications from clients whose BI policies did not respond as expected — many alleged their broker failed to explain disease clause limitations. One of the largest single PI events for E8 insurance broker sub-class since pensions mis-selling.

Building Safety Act 2022 (Post-Grenfell)

Building Safety Act 2022 introduces Building Safety Regulator (BSR), higher-risk building (HRB) regime, and extends limitation periods for PI claims under Defective Premises Act 1972 to 30 years retrospectively.

Insurance significance: Architects, interior designers, and design professionals who worked on HRBs face significantly expanded retroactive PI exposure. The 30-year retrospective limitation extension is an unprecedented expansion of design professional PI tail risk.

Data Gaps & Limitations

Lloyd's E8/E9/ED/EE GWP Sub-Split

Lloyd's does not publish GWP at the individual E-code sub-class level. Annual Report PI figures are aggregate; no public split between E8, E9, ED, EE codes, or profession sub-classes within those codes.

Loss Ratio by Profession Sub-Class

No publicly available loss ratio data by profession within the E8/EE miscellaneous class. Market intelligence suggests wide variation (insurance broker PI 60–80% LR; management consulting 50–65% LR; travel/events 55–75% LR) but no authoritative published data.

ESG Advisory PI Claims Experience

ESG advisory is too new a sub-class to have established claims experience data. The first generation of greenwashing-related PI notifications (2022–2024) has not yet fully resolved. Loss quantum and frequency remain highly uncertain.

AI-Assisted Professional Services PI Exposure

No established market data on PI claims arising from AI-assisted professional advice. Current wording landscape is unclear; market working groups are reviewing. This is a significant data gap that will likely take 3–5 years to develop into usable claims experience.

MGA/E9 Binder Portfolio Performance Data

E9 binder/coverholder portfolio performance is not publicly disclosed. Lloyd's Franchise Performance Directorate review data is confidential. Binder profitability varies significantly by coverholder quality; aggregate E9 performance is estimated only.
