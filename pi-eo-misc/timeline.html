1990
Regulatory

Murphy v Brentwood — Pure Economic Loss Rule Restated

House of Lords reverses Anns v Merton, significantly narrowing recovery for pure economic loss in building defect cases. Design professionals' exposure clarified: structural design errors causing economic loss (not just physical damage) remain recoverable, reinforcing demand for architects' PI. VER

1993
Insurance Broker PI

UK Pensions Mis-Selling Review Commences

FSA/SRO investigation into personal pension transfers advised by insurance brokers and IFAs 1988–1994. £11.8bn in compensation ultimately paid by providers and advisers. Insurance broker PI carriers face significant claims from advisers unable to fund redress personally. Market-defining event for E8 insurance intermediary sub-class. VER

1996
Management Consulting

Foxboro/ERP Implementation Claims Wave Begins

Early large-scale ERP implementation failures (SAP, Baan, Oracle installations) generate management consulting PI claims. Clients allege consultants failed to advise on implementation complexity, inadequate project management, and cost overrun. The "IT advice" exposure within the E8 miscellaneous class begins to be separately priced. EST

1999
Design (ED)

Interior Designer IP / Copyright Claim — High-Value Residential Project

Landmark (illustrative) interior design PI claim involving intellectual property infringement: designer's original drawings used by a subsequent contractor without permission, and client alleged the designer's specification was not fit for purpose. PI policies tested on the IP infringement extension and fitness-for-purpose exclusion boundary. ILLUS

2001
Management Consulting

Arthur Andersen / Enron Collapse — Consultant PI Contagion

Andersen's collapse after Enron triggers PI liability scrutiny across management consulting and advisory professions. Strategy consultants who advised on Enron-related structures, or who provided governance opinions, face notification waves. E8 management consultant underwriters tighten underwriting for advisory firms providing M&A, restructuring, or governance opinions. VER

2001
Travel / Events (EE)

ILM / Oneill Travel Agent Insolvency Wave Post-9/11

9/11 travel disruption generates travel agent E&O claims: clients allege travel agents failed to advise on change-of-mind cancellation options, inadequate travel insurance recommendations, and non-refundable booking failures. EE travel agent carriers reassess terrorism and force-majeure event exclusions. EST

2003
Recruitment / HR

Major Wrongful Placement Claim — Executive Recruitment

An executive search firm (illustrative, typical of class) faces a £2.5m PI claim after a CEO candidate it placed was found to have misrepresented qualifications; the firm failed to conduct adequate reference and credential checks. The claim tested the "negligent reference" and "failure to verify" coverage triggers in recruitment agency PI. ILLUS

2006
Regulatory

Insurance Mediation Directive — UK ICOB Rules

EU Insurance Mediation Directive implemented in UK via FSA ICOB rules. All FCA-authorised insurance intermediaries required to hold PI at regulatory minimum levels. Significantly expands mandatory demand for E8 insurance broker PI. Lloyd's and specialist market underwriters compete aggressively for broker PI volume. VER

2008
Insurance Broker PI

GFC — Insurance Broker PI Spike from Underinsurance Allegations

Global Financial Crisis triggers wave of broker PI claims: insureds allege brokers failed to advise on adequate D&O limits, credit risk insurance gaps, or business interruption coverage following market-wide losses. Some of the largest E8 insurance broker PI claims since pensions mis-selling emerge in 2009–2011. EST

2011
Media / PR

News of the World / PR Crisis Management Fallout

Phone-hacking scandal generates PR agency and crisis communications PI exposure: firms that advised media companies during the scandal face allegations their crisis management advice exacerbated reputational damage. Raises question of whether PI covers "failed crisis management" advice — typically yes, if advice was negligent. VER

2015
Regulatory

Insurance Act 2015 — Fair Presentation Replaces Uberrima Fides

Insurance Act 2015 comes into force (August 2016). "Fair presentation of risk" duty replaces full uberrima fides; proportionate remedies replace avoidance. Significant across all E8/E9/ED/EE classes: underwriters can no longer avoid for innocent non-disclosure; proportionate remedy framework introduces premium adjustments and partial coverage for misrepresentation. VER

2017
Recruitment / HR

IR35 Off-Payroll Extension — HR Consultant PI Notifications

HMRC extends IR35 to public sector contractors (2017), then private sector (2020). HR consultants and employment law advisers who provided IR35 status advice face claims from clients caught by unexpected tax liabilities. Claims range £50k–£2m depending on size of contractor workforce affected. VER

2018
Management Consulting

NHS IT Transformation — Management Consultant PI Claim

Large-scale NHS digital transformation programme (illustrative, reflecting class-wide experience) generates PI claim against a management consultancy for advice on programme design that allegedly led to a failed £400m NHS IT system deployment. Highlights the "failed outcome" vs "negligent advice" boundary debate central to miscellaneous PI. ILLUS

2018
Media / PR (EE)

GDPR Launch — Data Consultant and Marketing Agency PI Exposure

GDPR implementation (May 2018) creates immediate PI exposure for: data protection consultants who gave incorrect GDPR compliance advice; marketing agencies that advised on data collection strategies; HR systems consultants who implemented non-compliant employee data processes. EE and E8 underwriters update wording language around data protection liability. VER

2020
Travel / Events (EE)

COVID-19 — Travel Agent and Event Manager PI/E&O Claims Wave

COVID-19 generates the largest single-event claims experience in EE travel/events codes: travel agents face claims for failure to advise on FCDO travel warnings, inadequate booking refund processes, and negligent travel insurance recommendations. Event managers face claims for events booked without adequate cancellation provisions. Estimated industry-wide losses substantial; market introduces pandemic exclusions on new business. VER

2021
Insurance Broker PI

FCA BI Test Case Fallout — Broker PI Notifications

Following the FCA business interruption test case (2020–2021), insurance brokers face PI notifications from clients who had BI policies that did not respond as the insured expected. Broker PI claims allege: failure to advise on policy coverage gaps, inadequate explanation of "disease clauses" vs "prevention of access" coverage, and failure to place sufficient limits. VER

2022
Design (ED)

Product Designer IP / Specification Claim — Consumer Electronics

Industrial product designer faces dual PI/IP claim (illustrative): client alleges design specification failures led to a product recall and £8m loss, and a third party alleges the design infringed an existing registered design right. Tests the interaction between PI (negligent specification), IP infringement defence extension, and product recall — highlighting coverage coordination challenges in ED. ILLUS

2022
Management Consulting

ESG Advisory PI — First Wave of Greenwashing-Related Notifications

ESG and sustainability consultants face first wave of notifications from clients alleging ESG advice did not deliver promised outcomes: fund managers allege sustainability ratings advice was negligent; corporates allege sustainability reporting frameworks led to regulatory scrutiny. Market debates whether standard PI wordings cover "ESG advisory errors" — currently a coverage grey area. EST

2023
Regulatory

FCA Consumer Duty — Insurance Broker PI Recalibration

FCA Consumer Duty (July 2023) raises standard of care for retail-facing insurance brokers to "good outcomes" level. PI underwriters recalibrate E8 insurance broker PI exposure: higher standard of advice required means broader claims trigger; underwriters adjust rates and sub-limits for retail distribution businesses. VER

2024
Regulatory / AI

AI-Assisted Professional Advice — PI Wording Debate

Management consultants, HR advisers, and marketing agencies increasingly use AI tools (ChatGPT, Copilot, Perplexity) to generate client advice. PI market debates: is advice generated by an AI tool covered under standard PI wordings? Draft AI endorsements begin to circulate. Lloyd's market working group reviews E8/E9/ED/EE wordings to address AI-assisted professional services liability. EST
