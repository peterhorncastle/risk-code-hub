1993–2000UK Pensions Mis-Selling — Insurance Broker/IFA PI
Insurance Broker PICatastrophic
▼

Loss: £11.8bn total redress paid across all parties; insurer PI claims estimated £500m–£1bn+ absorbed by Lloyd's and specialist market VER

Trigger: Advisers mis-sold personal pension transfers to occupational pension members 1988–1994; FSA-mandated redress programme 1994–2000

Lesson: Advice-volume businesses with large client populations carry systemic/aggregation risk — a single regulatory mis-selling finding can trigger thousands of individual PI claims. Underwriters must assess volume and client type (retail vs wholesale) separately.

▼

Trigger: Advertising agency created a campaign that the ASA ruled was misleading; client faced £450k ASA-directed corrective advertising costs plus brand damage remediation; client alleged agency failed to carry out basic regulatory compliance checks before campaign launch

Trigger: Graphic design agency created a brand identity for a retail chain that was subsequently alleged to infringe the registered trade mark of an existing brand; client faced injunction, rebrand costs of £600k, and business disruption losses

Trigger: FCA Consumer Duty investigation found that a retail insurance broker's advice process did not meet the "good outcomes" standard for a category of customers; broker required to provide redress; PI carrier notified of likely claim quantum
