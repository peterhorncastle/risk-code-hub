IBNR & Development Considerations
FactorImpact on Reserves
Long construction periodsCAR policies running 3–7 years mean losses can be reported years after policy inception. IBNR loading for open projects essential.
NatCat tail developmentMajor NatCat events (earthquake, flood) generate claims reported over 12–36 months as damage is assessed and reinstatement costed. Reserve gross; develop net of reinsurance recovery.
Defect latency (LEG 3)LEG 3 defect claims may emerge during maintenance period (12–36 months post-completion). PI interface claims can develop over 6+ years under latent defect regimes (Building Safety Act 2022 — 30-year period for higher-risk buildings).
DSU reserve complexityDSU delay assessment requires independent project monitor certification. Claims can remain open 2–5 years while delay certification, subrogation, and recovery actions are pursued.
Subrogation recovery (CAR)Successful subrogation against defaulting subcontractors or defective material suppliers can materially reduce net loss. Reserve gross; flag subrogation prospects separately. PI interface critical.
Offshore tail durationOffshore wind and O&G EAR claims involve specialist salvage, reinstatement engineering, and technical investigation. Claims lifecycle 3–7 years not uncommon for major offshore losses.
