Russia 2022 IBNR and Reserve Note: The Russia 2022 confiscation claims remain subject to extensive litigation in multiple jurisdictions as of May 2026. No definitive insured loss figure has been agreed between Lloyd's syndicates, their reinsurers, and the claimant lessors. Underwriters carrying Russia 2022 exposure must maintain prudent IBNR reserves that account for: (a) the full claimed value ($10–13bn market estimate), (b) expected recoveries through subrogation/litigation (uncertain), (c) reinsurance recovery disputes (lead/follow disagreements), and (d) the multi-year litigation timeline. Actuarial guidance from Lloyd's Corporation on Russia 2022 reserving should be followed. [I4]
IBNR & Development Considerations
FactorImpact on Aviation War Reserves
Liability war — Montreal Convention claimsWrongful death claims under Montreal Convention develop over 3–7 years; multiple jurisdictions; punitive damages debates. MH17 liability claims still developing 10 years post-event.
Russia 2022 confiscation — litigation uncertaintyClaims quantum uncertain; Russian court refusal to recognise judgments; potential partial settlement vs full trial outcomes. IBNR must reflect full range of outcomes from 0% to 100% recovery.
Reinsurance recovery disputesLarge aviation war losses trigger disputes between lead and follow underwriters and their respective reinsurers. Recovery from reinsurers may be delayed by coverage disputes. Net reserve must account for uncertain RI recovery.
GNSS spoofing — emerging classNo actuarial history for navigation losses attributable to state-directed spoofing. Prudent IBNR loading for post-2022 policy years until coverage position and frequency data established.
Currency and agreed value fluctuationsAircraft values denominated in USD; significant currency movements (post-Russia sanctions USD/RUB, EUR/USD) affect reinsurance recoveries and net loss calculations for non-USD reporting syndicates.
