IBNR & Reserving Considerations
FactorImpact on Reserves
Employment tribunal backlogPost-COVID tribunal waiting times of 18–24 months mean employment LEI claims may be notified 1–2 years before the tribunal hearing — claims lifecycle extends reserving period significantly
Employment law reform lagNew legislation (Employment Rights Act amendments, flexible working rights) creates uncertain frequency impact — prudent to hold additional IBNR pending 12–24 months of claims development data
GDPR investigation durationICO regulatory investigations can run 12–36 months — BTE policies covering investigation costs face extended claims lifecycles; IBNR loading for open regulatory investigation costs
ATE adverse developmentCommercial ATE claims develop over litigation lifecycle (2–5 years for large commercial disputes). IBNR calculation must reflect full adverse costs exposure until final judgment/settlement, not just current incurred costs
Fixed Recoverable Costs — BTE impactFRC extension (2023) reduces average BTE claim cost for sub-£100k disputes; IBNR models should be updated to reflect lower average settlement costs in FRC-capped proceedings
Panel solicitor fee arbitrationDisputes between BTE insurers and panel solicitors over fee rates can generate unexpected cost overruns. Reserve for panel fee disputes at 5–10% of panel claims costs
