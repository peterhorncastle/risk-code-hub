Primary Sources

Legislation

1
Building Safety Act 2022 UK Parliament. The most significant reform to UK building safety law since the Building Act 1984. Establishes Higher Risk Building regime (>18m / 7+ storeys); Building Safety Regulator (BSR); Gateway 1/2/3 process; Principal Designer (BSA); Building Liability Orders; 30-year retrospective limitation for fire safety claims (s.135). VER legislation.gov.uk — Building Safety Act 2022
2
Defective Premises Act 1972 (as amended by BSA 2022) UK Parliament. Section 1: duty to build dwellings fit for habitation. Original 6-year limitation period extended by BSA 2022 s.135 to 30 years for new dwellings (s.1) and 15 years for existing (retrospective). VER legislation.gov.uk — Defective Premises Act 1972
3
Construction (Design and Management) Regulations 2015 (CDM 2015) UK Statutory Instrument 2015 No. 51. Creates Principal Designer (CDM) statutory role. F10 notification threshold. VER legislation.gov.uk — CDM 2015
4
Fire Safety Act 2021 UK Parliament. Extends Fire Safety Order 2005 to external walls, balconies, and flat entrance doors of multi-occupied residential buildings. VER legislation.gov.uk — Fire Safety Act 2021

Case Law

5
Hedley Byrne & Co Ltd v Heller & Partners Ltd [1964] AC 465 House of Lords. Establishes duty of care for negligent misstatement; voluntary assumption of responsibility; special relationship. Foundation of all construction professional PI. VER bailii.org — Hedley Byrne v Heller [1963]
6
Anns v Merton London Borough Council [1978] AC 728 House of Lords. Local authority duty of care for negligent foundation inspection; two-stage Lord Wilberforce test; dangerous defect actionable in tort even after property transfer. Overruled by Murphy v Brentwood (1991). VER Available via Westlaw UK; BAILII: [1977] UKHL 4
7
D&F Estates Ltd v Church Commissioners for England [1989] 1 AC 177 House of Lords. Contractor not liable in tort to third party for cost of repairing defective plaster; pure economic loss not recoverable. Precursor to Murphy v Brentwood. VER Available via Westlaw UK; BAILII: [1988] UKHL 4
8
Murphy v Brentwood District Council [1991] 1 AC 398 House of Lords. Overrules Anns v Merton. Pure economic loss (cost of repairing defective building) not recoverable in tort. Fundamentally narrows construction professional tort liability to third parties. VER Available via Westlaw UK; BAILII: [1990] UKHL 2
9
South Australia Asset Management Corp v York Montague Ltd [1997] AC 191 (SAAMCO) House of Lords. Valuer's liability capped at the difference between the negligent valuation and the true value — not the entire lender's loss. Benchmark for all UK valuation PI claims. VER Available via Westlaw UK

Regulatory & Industry Guidance

10
Hackitt Review — Independent Review of Building Regulations and Fire Safety (2018) Dame Judith Hackitt. Final Report May 2018. Finds systemic failures in UK building regulatory system. Recommends Gateway process, dutyholders, golden thread. Blueprint for Building Safety Act 2022. VER gov.uk — Hackitt Review Final Report
11
Grenfell Tower Inquiry — Phase 1 Report (2019) & Phase 2 Report (2024) Sir Martin Moore-Bick. Phase 1 (October 2019): findings on cause of fire and spread. Phase 2 (September 2024): systemic failures — manufacturers, contractors, regulators, government. Criminal referrals expected. VER grenfelltowerinquiry.org.uk
12
RIBA Plan of Work 2020 Royal Institute of British Architects. 8-stage project delivery framework (Stages 0–7). Industry-standard project management structure for architectural and engineering projects. VER architecture.com — RIBA Plan of Work 2020
13
ARB Code of Conduct (2017 as updated) Architects Registration Board. Mandatory standards for registered architects including professional indemnity insurance obligation. VER arb.org.uk — Architects' Code
14
RICS Professional Standards and Guidance — Insurance Requirements Royal Institution of Chartered Surveyors. Mandatory PI requirements for RICS regulated firms. VER rics.org — PI Insurance Requirements
15
ISO 19650-1 & 19650-2 — Information Management using BIM International Organisation for Standardisation. Parts 1 and 2 of the ISO 19650 series define information management concepts and requirements for the delivery phase of assets. VER iso.org — ISO 19650
16
JCT Design and Build Contract 2016 (JCT DB 2016) Joint Contracts Tribunal. Standard form D&B contract. Clause 2.17.1 limits contractor's design liability to reasonable skill and care — key underwriting document for E7 fitness-for-purpose risk assessment. VER jctltd.co.uk — JCT DB 2016
17
FIDIC Yellow Book (Design-Build) 2017 — Plant and Design-Build Fédération Internationale des Ingénieurs-Conseils. International standard contract for D&B; widely used in Middle East, Asia-Pacific, and international infrastructure. Clause 4.1 design obligation. VER fidic.org — Yellow Book 2017
18
NSW Design and Building Practitioners Act 2020 (Australia) NSW Parliament. Mandatory PI requirements for registered designers and builders in NSW residential sector. Post-Opal Tower Tower reform; triggered major market change in Australian construction PI. VER legislation.nsw.gov.au — DBPA 2020

Known Data Gaps

Data Limitations — PI E&O Construction Hub

  • GAP Lloyd's E6/E7 GWP breakdown: Lloyd's does not publish GWP at the E6/E7 sub-class level. Estimates derived from industry association reports and market commentary.
  • GAP BSA 2022 retrospective liability quantum: The total insured liability created by BSA 2022's 30-year retrospective limitation period for fire safety claims is not yet quantifiable. No actuarial model has been published; estimates vary widely from £500m to £5bn+ for the industry. This is the most significant current data gap in UK construction PI.
  • GAP RAAC aggregate exposure: The total PI exposure to the construction industry from RAAC-related claims is not yet known. The number of affected buildings, the scope of each engineering firm's RAAC assessment history, and the potential for class action litigation are all unquantified as of 2024.
  • GAP E6/E7 combined ratio by sub-class: No published combined ratio data exists at the E6/E7 sub-class level. The rates-analysis module uses directional estimates consistent with market commentary; these should not be used as authoritative actuarial data.
  • GAP AI design liability — no settled law or actuarial data: The liability of construction professionals for errors in AI-generated design outputs is untested in law as of 2024. No loss data, no case law, no settled underwriting practice exists. This is a forward-looking risk with substantial uncertainty.
