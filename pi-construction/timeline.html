1964CASE

Hedley Byrne & Co Ltd v Heller & Partners Ltd [1964] AC 465

House of Lords establishes liability for negligent misstatement where a special relationship exists (voluntary assumption of responsibility + reliance). VER Foundational case for all construction professional PI — creates the duty of care for architects, engineers, surveyors giving professional advice to clients.

1968LOSS

Ronan Point Collapse

Ronan Point, a 22-storey tower block in Newham, London, suffered a progressive partial collapse following a gas explosion on the 18th floor. VER Structural design relied on large panel system (LPS) construction with inadequate connections. Major driver of structural engineering PI awareness and large panel system regulatory restrictions.

1972REG

Defective Premises Act 1972

Section 1 imposes a duty on anyone who does work in connection with the provision of a dwelling to ensure it is fit for habitation. VER Original 6-year limitation from completion. Extended to 30 years by Building Safety Act 2022 (s.135) for new dwellings, and 15 years for existing dwellings — retroactive effect creates historic tail risk for construction professionals.

1978CASE

Anns v Merton London Borough Council [1978] AC 728

House of Lords (Lord Wilberforce) establishes local authority liability for negligent foundation inspection; duty of care owed to building occupiers even after property transfer. VER "Anns era" expands construction PI exposure dramatically — professionals face liability to subsequent owners for latent structural defects. Major driver of PI premium growth 1978–1991.

1989CASE

D&F Estates Ltd v Church Commissioners [1989] 1 AC 177

House of Lords: contractor not liable in tort to third party for cost of repairing defective plaster. VER Pure economic loss not recoverable in tort for defective structures — precursor to Murphy v Brentwood. Narrows construction professional tort exposure for pure building defect costs.

1991CASE

Murphy v Brentwood District Council [1991] 1 AC 398

House of Lords overrules Anns v Merton. VER Pure economic loss (cost of repairing defective building) not recoverable in tort. Construction PI scope substantially narrowed — professionals' tort liability to third parties significantly reduced. Latent defects insurance (NHBC Buildmark) expands to fill gap. Market adjusts pricing downward; run-off reserving challenged.

1995MKT

NHBC Buildmark 10-Year Structural Warranty Standard

NHBC Buildmark 10-year warranty becomes near-universal for new residential construction, providing structural insurance to homebuyers independent of professional PI. EST Separates latent defects insurance (LDI) from PI market — construction PI focuses on professional advice/design liability rather than pure structural defect risk.

2003REG

RICS Homebuyer Survey Reform & Valuer PI Requirements

RICS strengthens professional standards and PI requirements for valuers and building surveyors following increased frequency of homebuyer survey claims. EST Surveyor PI becomes a significant premium line in Lloyd's E6 book; SAAMCO cap (South Australia Asset Management Corp v York Montague [1997]) limits valuation claim quantum but does not eliminate frequency.

2011REG

UK Government BIM Mandate — Level 2 by 2016

UK Government Construction Strategy 2011 mandates fully collaborative BIM Level 2 on all centrally procured government projects by 2016. VER Creates new professional liability exposure: BIM coordination failures, clash detection errors, COBie data handover defects. ISO 19650 series subsequently developed as international BIM standard.

2015REG

CDM 2015 — Principal Designer Role Created

Construction (Design and Management) Regulations 2015 replace CDM 2007. VER New statutory role: Principal Designer — responsible for pre-construction health and safety. Creates new PI exposure for architects and engineers appointed as Principal Designer; distinguishable from BSA 2022 Principal Designer role introduced later.

2017LOSS

Grenfell Tower Fire — 72 Deaths

14 June 2017. Fire in Grenfell Tower, Kensington. 72 deaths. Cladding system — Arconic Reynobond PE ACM panels and Celotex RS5000 insulation — failed to resist fire spread. VER Studio E Architects (design consultant), Rydon (D&B principal contractor), Harley Facades (cladding subcontractor). The most significant single event in UK construction PI history. Products and PI liability; long-term Inquiry; criminal investigations ongoing as of 2024.

2018BSA

Hackitt Review — Independent Review of Building Regulations and Fire Safety

Dame Judith Hackitt. VER Final report May 2018. Finds "systemic failure" in UK building regulatory system — inadequate oversight, fragmented responsibility, lack of competence. Recommends Gateway process, dutyholders, "golden thread" of building information. Blueprint for Building Safety Act 2022.

2019MKT

Cladding Exclusions Become Near-Universal in Architect PI

Following Grenfell and the scale of potential legacy liability, Lloyd's and London company market underwriters introduce cladding and external wall fire safety exclusions into architect, fire engineer, and high-rise specialist PI policies. VER (Market-wide practice). Premiums increase 25–40% for affected professions. EWS1 form introduced — creates new PI exposure for assessing fire engineers.

2021REG

Fire Safety Act 2021

Extends Fire Safety Order 2005 to include external walls, balconies, and flat entrance doors of multi-occupied residential buildings. VER Increases responsible person obligations; creates new compliance audit requirements; further drives EWS1 assessment demand.

2022BSA

Building Safety Act 2022 Enacted

The most significant reform to UK building safety law since the Building Act 1984. VER Key provisions: Higher Risk Buildings (HRBs) defined (>18m / 7+ storeys residential); mandatory Gateway 1/2/3 process; Building Safety Regulator (BSR) established within HSE; 30-year retrospective limitation for fire safety claims under s.135; Building Liability Orders enabling parent company liability; Principal Designer (BSA) new competence regime. Creates the longest actuarial tail in UK construction PI history.

2022BSA

Defective Premises Act 1972 Limitation Extended

Building Safety Act 2022 s.135 extends Defective Premises Act 1972 limitation from 6 years to 30 years for new dwellings and 15 years for existing dwellings — retroactively. VER Historic construction PI policies potentially on risk for claims from work completed decades ago — fundamental tail extension for E6 residential architects and structural engineers.

2023REG

Building Safety Regulator (BSR) Operational

BSR operational within HSE from April 2023. VER Gateway 2 (before construction) and Gateway 3 (before occupation) now mandatory for all new HRBs. Competence requirements for Principal Designer (BSA) and Principal Contractor (BSA). Existing HRBs must register with BSR by October 2023. New enforcement powers including stop notices and prosecution.

2023MKT

Construction PI Market: Partial Stabilisation for Non-Cladding Risks

After 4 years of severe rate increases and capacity withdrawal (2019–2022), the Lloyd's construction PI market shows partial stabilisation for non-residential architects and structural engineers without significant HRB exposure. EST Cladding-exposed practices and HRB specialists continue to face very difficult market conditions. BSA 2022 tail uncertainty prevents full normalisation.

2024BSA

Grenfell Tower Inquiry Phase 2 Report

Phase 2 Report published September 2024. VER Findings include systemic failures by manufacturers, contractors, regulators, and government. Studio E Architects and Rydon criticised. Potential for criminal prosecutions; further civil liability claims expected to follow. Government response and further regulatory change anticipated. Ongoing PI tail development.

2024REG

ISO 19650 BIM Standard — Full Implementation & AI Design Risk Emerging

ISO 19650 information management standard widely adopted across UK and major international markets. EST AI-assisted design tools (generative design, AI structural optimisation) begin entering professional practice — creates new PI exposure for professionals relying on AI outputs without adequate checking. Lloyd's market discussions on AI design liability underway as of 2024.
