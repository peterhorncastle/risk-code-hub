Module 04 — Underwriting

PI E&O Financial Institutions — Underwriting

Rating methodology, risk factor cards, two worked pricing examples, standard sub-limits framework, and pre-bind underwriting checklist for IFA and institutional FI PI risks.

FI PI is rated on a technical premium basis derived from the insured's revenue, advice product mix, regulatory history, limit of indemnity, and policy structure. All rating factors are applied as multipliers to a base exposure premium derived from client assets under advice (AUA) or gross revenue. EST

FI PI Technical Premium Formula
Technical Premium = (Base Rate × Revenue / AUA) × Product Risk Factor × Regulatory Factor × Limit Factor × Retroactive Date Factor × Deductible Credit

Base rate: 0.20%–0.60% of annual gross revenue for retail IFAs; 0.05%–0.20% for institutional advisers (AUA basis). EST
Product risk factor: 1.0 (vanilla funds/ISAs) → 3.5× (DB transfers, structured products, alternative investments).
Regulatory factor: 1.0 (clean history) → 2.5× (prior FCA investigation, FOS awards, regulatory censure).
Limit factor: Stepped — higher limits attract marginal rate reduction reflecting reduced severity probability at upper layers.
Retroactive date factor: Full retro = 1.0; restricted retro (date within last 3 yrs) = 0.75–0.85.

Primary Rating Factors

Revenue / AUA

Primary exposure base. Retail IFAs rated on gross income (fee + trail); DFMs and institutional managers rated on AUA. Revenue growth >20% year-on-year raises proportional exposure flag.

Neutral baseline — sets exposure unit

Defined Benefit Transfer Volume

DB transfer advice is the single highest-frequency source of FI PI claims in the IFA market. Any firm with >15% of advice revenue from DB transfers requires a dedicated sub-limit and surcharge.

+30%–100% loading

Structured Product / Alternative Investment

CPDO, structured notes, mini-bonds, P2P, UCIS, EIS/SEIS in retail context. Complexity and illiquidity of these products correlates with higher claim frequency and severity.

+25%–75% loading

Regulatory History

Prior FCA enforcement action, FOS awards >£10k, regulatory capital concerns, or withdrawal of regulated permissions in prior 5 years. Even settled matters indicate elevated process risk.

+50%–150% loading or decline

Appointed Representative Network

Principal firms responsible for AR conduct. AR network size, onboarding rigour, and monitoring procedures are critical. Large AR networks with limited oversight are a major accumulation risk.

+25%–75% loading; cap on AR count

Claims-Free History

Verified 5-year claims-free record (FOS + civil) is the most powerful positive rating indicator. Independent firm that has grown steadily with clean claims history represents the target risk profile.

−10%–20% credit

MiFID II / Consumer Duty Compliance Evidence

Documented suitability framework, client profiling records, product governance procedures, Consumer Duty outcome monitoring — all reduce the probability of a successful negligence claim.

−5%–15% credit

Retroactive Date

Claims-made policies — retroactive date controls the tail. Full retroactive date increases exposure significantly for firms with legacy books. Common approach: inception date retro for new insureds; maintained retro for renewals.

+15%–25% for full retro vs inception date

ESG / Sustainable Finance

Firms marketing ESG or sustainable mandates face greenwashing claims exposure. Require documentation of ESG methodology, SDR/SFDR compliance procedures, and fund labelling review process.

+10%–30% loading (ESG AUM >20%)

Cryptocurrency / Digital Assets

Any firm advising on or facilitating digital asset investments. FCA registration as crypto asset business required. Absent registration, exposure to unregulated activity exclusion disputes.

Sub-limit or exclusion standard

PI Cover Continuity

Unbroken prior cover without gaps is a key positive indicator. Gaps in cover create retroactive date risk and suggest periods of potential uninsured claims. Require prior insurer loss history for 5 years.

−5% credit (continuous 5+ years)

Limit of Indemnity Adequacy

FCA minimum PI requirements (£1m any-one-claim) are often inadequate for larger IFAs. Underwriters should assess whether limit is proportionate to AUA and DB transfer advice volumes. Insufficient limits may indicate adverse selection (firm not disclosing full exposure).

Referral if limit appears low relative to exposure

Worked Pricing Examples

Example A — Mid-Size Retail IFA Practice

Pemberton Financial Advisers Ltd — 12 advisers, £85m AUA, mixed advice book

Profile: 12 employed advisers, £85m client AUA, £1.2m gross revenue. Advice mix: 60% vanilla funds/ISAs, 25% DB transfer advice (£18m pa volume), 10% structured products, 5% protection. 5-year claims-free record. No FCA regulatory actions. Consumer Duty procedures documented. Seeking £2m any-one-claim / £4m aggregate. Full retroactive date from inception (2016). ILLUS

Limit factor (£2m/£4m — standard multiplier 2.8×)×2.8 applied to base
Sub-total before adjustments£17,640
Claims-free credit (−15%)−£2,646
Consumer Duty compliance credit (−10%)−£1,764

Conditions: DB transfer advice sub-limit £1m any-one-claim / £3m aggregate. Structured product sub-limit £500k. £5,000 deductible per claim. MiFID II suitability records subject to inspection. ILLUS

Pre-Bind Underwriting Checklist

Minimum information required before binding FI PI coverage. EST — additional questions required for higher-risk profiles.

  • R
    FCA Registration & Regulatory Status Confirm FRN, regulated activities, any current regulatory investigations, past enforcement actions, FCA capital adequacy requirements met.
  • R
    5-Year Claims History (FOS + civil) Full claims schedule including FOS decisions, settled civil claims, open circumstances, circumstances notified but not yet claims. Require prior insurer confirmation.
  • R
    Revenue Breakdown by Advice Category Percentage of gross revenue / AUM by product type: vanilla funds, DB transfers, structured products, alternatives, protection, mortgages, ESG, crypto. Minimum 3-year trend.
  • R
    DB Transfer Volume & Documented Process Where DB transfer volume >£5m pa, require copy of TVAS process documentation, FCA's DB transfer advice review findings if available, and specialist qualifications confirmation (AF7 or equivalent).
  • R
    Client Profile — Retail vs Professional vs Eligible Counterparty Proportion of retail, professional, and institutional clients. Elderly / vulnerable client policy. Any 'mass market' distribution of higher-risk products.
  • R
    Appointed Representative Network For principal firms: number of ARs, onboarding procedure, compliance monitoring framework, prior AR-related claims or regulatory actions. Maximum AR count thresholds apply.
  • P
    Consumer Duty Compliance Assessment Evidence of Consumer Duty implementation: outcome monitoring framework, product review cadence, vulnerable client identification procedures, complaints handling procedures.
  • P
    MiFID II Suitability Documentation Sample Sample anonymised suitability report to evidence advice documentation standard. Quality of documentation is the primary defence in PI claims.
  • P
    ESG Methodology Documentation Where insured manages ESG mandates or markets ESG products: copy of ESG screening methodology, SDR/SFDR categorisation procedures, fund labelling review process.
  • P
    Professional Indemnity Cover Continuity Prior cover schedule for 5 years. Any gaps in coverage, retroactive date changes, or change of insurer. Require explanation for any gaps.
  • I
    Technology / AI Tool Usage Whether AI tools used in advice preparation, research generation, or suitability assessments. Evidence of human review and accountability framework if AI tools used.
  • I
    Key Person Dependencies For smaller firms: dependency on founding adviser, succession planning, professional qualifications of all regulated individuals. Key person departure = material change requiring notification.
Referral Triggers — Automatic Referral to Senior Underwriter
Any of the following require senior referral before binding: (1) DB transfer volume >£25m pa, (2) active FCA investigation, (3) >3 FOS awards in prior 3 years, (4) AR network >50 representatives, (5) crypto advice without FCA crypto registration, (6) structured product distribution to retail clients >30% of revenue, (7) gaps in prior PI cover >30 days, (8) limit of indemnity >£10m any-one-claim.
