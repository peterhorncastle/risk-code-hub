Lloyd's Risk Codes

CodeClassScopeLloyd's Market Role
F2 PI E&O — Financial Institutions (Direct) Professional liability for negligent acts, errors and omissions in the provision of financial services. Covers civil liability to third parties arising from professional services. Defence costs first-dollar basis. FCA-regulated firms, SEC-registered advisers, FINRA broker-dealers, fund managers, corporate finance advisers, research analysts, custody banks, fund administrators, credit rating agencies, payment service providers. Lloyd's is a significant market for mid-to-large FI PI risks and complex bespoke programmes. Leading syndicates: Beazley (largest), Hiscox, Brit, AIG Syndicate, Axis. Primary and excess layers. FI PI is one of the most active claims classes in Lloyd's specialty portfolio. VER
F3 PI E&O — Financial Institutions (Facultative RI) Facultative reinsurance of FI PI/E&O risks. Used where primary insurers require capacity relief on large programmes, high-limit placements, or concentration risk in specific FI sectors (e.g. US investment banking). Lloyd's syndicates participate as reinsurers behind domestic carriers. Lloyd's plays a key excess-of-loss and facultative RI role for major US and European FI PI programmes exceeding primary insurer appetite. Particularly active on hedge fund, private equity and investment banking PI towers. EST

The PI vs. Crime vs. D&O Triangle

FI professional liability sits at the intersection of three distinct insurance lines. Understanding the boundaries is fundamental to FI underwriting. VER

PI E&O (F2/F3) — This Hub

  • Covers negligent professional services — errors, omissions, bad advice
  • No dishonesty element required — negligence alone is sufficient
  • First-party defence costs + third-party civil liability
  • Regulatory investigation defence (not fines or penalties)
  • Retroactive date covers past advice still in force
  • Run-off cover essential when firm ceases trading

Crime / Fidelity BB — Separate Hub

  • Covers intentional dishonesty — manifest intent + financial benefit required
  • Employee theft, fraud, BEC, computer crime, securities forgery
  • Discovery basis — not claims-made
  • No negligence element — intentional acts only
  • Boundary: rogue trader who is also negligent — BB responds; negligent trader without dishonesty — PI responds

D&O — Separate Hub

  • Covers management decisions by directors and officers acting in that capacity
  • Wrongful acts in managing the company — not providing services to clients
  • Securities claims, derivative suits, M&A disputes
  • Boundary: director who also provides advice to a client — that advice claim goes to PI, not D&O
  • Many FI programmes bundle PI + D&O under a combined FI policy
⚠️ Combined FI Policy vs. Standalone PI Large financial institutions increasingly purchase a single combined FI policy that bundles PI/E&O, D&O, Employment Practices Liability and sometimes Crime under one tower with sublimits per coverage section. Lloyd's syndicates are active in both the combined FI policy market and standalone PI placements. Combined policies require careful drafting of priority of payment, deductible allocation and the interaction of sublimits to avoid coverage gaps at the seams between sections. VER

Coverage Taxonomy

🏦 Investment Management & Advice

  • Unsuitable investment recommendations (wrong risk profile, wrong product)
  • Negligent portfolio construction or management
  • Breach of mandate (investing outside agreed parameters)
  • Model error — quantitative strategy failure
  • Performance misrepresentation
  • Failure to hedge or diversify as promised

📊 Stockbroking & Corporate Finance

  • Execution error — wrong security, wrong price, wrong quantity
  • Best execution failures
  • Research liability — negligent analyst reports relied on by investors
  • Conflict of interest in research (analyst / banking Chinese wall failures)
  • M&A advice — negligent due diligence or valuation
  • Capital raising — negligent prospectus or suitability assessment

🏠 Retail Financial Advice (IFA)

  • Pensions transfer advice — defined benefit to defined contribution
  • Structured product mis-selling (with-profits bonds, CPDO, mini-bonds)
  • SIPP self-invested pension — inappropriate underlying investments
  • Mortgage advice — unsuitable product selection
  • Consumer Duty (FCA 2023) — higher suitability and outcome standards
  • FCA enforcement action leading to civil claims

🔒 Custody, Administration & Infrastructure

  • Custodian liability — incorrect settlement, failed trade processing
  • Fund administrator error — incorrect NAV calculation, redemption processing
  • Prime broker — margin call errors, securities lending dispute
  • Transfer agent — incorrect shareholder register
  • Trustee — breach of trust in administering pension scheme

🌱 Emerging Exposures

  • ESG mis-labelling — greenwashing claims against fund managers and advisers
  • Crypto / digital asset advice — suitability of high-risk assets for retail clients
  • Robo-adviser liability — algorithm-driven portfolio advice failures
  • AI investment model error — unexplained model decisions causing loss
  • APP fraud reimbursement — payment services FI liability (PSR 2024)
  • Climate-related financial risk advice — negligent TCFD disclosures

⚖️ Regulatory Defence Costs

  • FCA investigation defence costs (Section 166 reviews, enforcement)
  • SEC / FINRA investigation defence costs
  • Note: regulatory fines and penalties are not insurable (public policy)
  • Compensation to customers ordered by FOS / FCA — typically covered
  • FSCS levy not insurable; civil liability arising from same facts covered
  • Criminal defence costs sometimes included with sub-limit

Key Policy Features

FeatureStandard PositionUnderwriting Note
Policy Basis Claims-made: claim must be made during policy period; retroactive date excludes known circumstances Retroactive date is critical — do not shorten without understanding prior exposure; run-off tail for ceased firms typically 6 years minimum VER
Defence Costs Usually first-dollar (no deductible on defence costs) or low deductible; erode the limit FI defence costs can be substantial — regulatory investigations run 2-4 years; negotiate defence costs outside the limit for large FIs EST
Civil Liability Trigger Negligent act, error or omission in the performance of professional services Services must be defined carefully — blanket "financial services" can overreach; exclude personal trading, own investments VER
Regulatory Coverage Defence costs for FCA/SEC investigations; FOS/arbitration awards; compensation schemes Fines, penalties, disgorgement orders not insurable; civil restitution to clients IS typically insurable; confirm jurisdiction VER
Run-Off Extended reporting period (ERP) on expiry or cancellation; typically 6 years automatic FI run-off is a significant market segment — M&A target FIs, fund wind-downs, IFA retirement. Run-off premium typically 100–200% of expiring annual premium EST
Aggregate Deductible Per-claim or aggregate deductible (often both); large FIs use SIR or captive layer Per-claim deductibles encourage early notification; aggregate caps insured's total annual retention; negotiate which applies to defence costs VER
