IBNR & Development Considerations
FactorImpact on Reserves
Cast abandonment — long-tail litigationCast illness claims where production is abandoned may be disputed (did the production have to abandon, or could reshoots have continued?); claims lifecycle 1–3 years
E&O — IP dispute durationE&O claims for copyright infringement and defamation typically take 2–5 years to resolve; IBNR loading essential; discovery period 1 year post-delivery standard
Rust-type liability — criminal proceedingsProduction liability claims linked to criminal proceedings develop very slowly; criminal appeals, civil settlements and OSHA proceedings overlap; reserve to ultimate
Digital asset loss — recoverability uncertaintyDigital footage loss claims depend on recoverability assessment; if partially recoverable, reshoot costs reduced; IBNR must allow for best/worst case recovery scenarios
COVID exclusion confirmationPandemic exclusion wordings must be confirmed unambiguous; any residual coverage dispute (e.g., "was this COVID or a concurrent insured peril?") creates tail reserve uncertainty
AI/likeness emerging claimsNo actuarial history for AI-related production E&O claims; prudent underwriters apply additional IBNR for 2023+ policy years pending first claims data
