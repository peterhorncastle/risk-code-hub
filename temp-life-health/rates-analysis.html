IBNR & Reserve Development Considerations
FactorImpact on Reserves
Long-COVID claim developmentClaims notified 2021–24 are still developing; severity uncertain; functional assessment outcomes variable. Significant IBNR loading required for IP books with pre-COVID inception policies
Mental health IP claim durationAverage mental health IP claim duration extending post-COVID — was 2–3 years, now 3–4+ years. Reserves must reflect longer payment periods; recovery rates declining for complex mental health cases
Group life CAT XL treaty reinstatementCOVID group life losses may have eroded CAT XL cover; reinstatement premium and reduced CAT protection affect net position for subsequent years
Genetic testing moratorium expiry (2025)Post-2025 framework uncertain — adverse selection risk if moratorium lapses without replacement. IBNR for lives with undisclosed Huntington's or BRCA test results may require uplift
Substandard life mortality developmentEPM ratings on impaired lives require experience monitoring — actual mortality vs expected for diabetic, cardiac and cancer-survivor cohorts. Post-COVID mortality changes affect EPM calibration
Reinsurance credit riskLife RI treaties with large retrocessionaires require credit review; Solvency II requires gross and net of RI reserves to be stated; RI counterparty default risk is low but must be monitored for long-duration books
