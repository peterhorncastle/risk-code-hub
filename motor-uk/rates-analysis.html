Market Cycle Narrative

2010–2012: Hard Market — PI Claims Crisis

Whiplash claims industrialisation, CFA expansion and rising bodily injury costs drove a hard market cycle. Average personal lines premium rose 30–40% from 2010–2012. EST LASPO reforms then partially deflated rates from 2013.

2013–2016: Soft Drift — Reform Optimism

LASPO impact, referral fee bans and early signs of falling whiplash volumes encouraged competition. Rates softened; combined ratios improved to near-breakeven 100–105% for best operators. EST

2017: Ogden Shock — Abrupt Market Hardening

PIDR cut to –0.75% in March 2017 forced immediate reserve strengthening industry-wide. Average personal lines premium rose ~15–20% within 12 months. EST Lloyd's motor portfolio required significant IBNR uplift. VER

2018–2020: Ogden Reversal & COVID Dislocation

Ogden rate corrected to +0.25% (2019), reducing reserve pressure. COVID-19 lockdowns slashed claims frequency in 2020 — some insurers issued premium rebates. Short-term profitability improved dramatically. VER

2021–2022: FCA Reform & Inflation Emergence

FCA pricing rules restructured renewal pricing — end of new-business discounting. Simultaneously, post-COVID claims severity began rising sharply: parts shortages, EV total losses, labour cost inflation. VER

2023–2024: Claims Inflation Peak — Premium Catch-Up

Average UK comprehensive premium rose 29% in 2023 (ABI) VER — the steepest annual increase on record. Rates began outpacing loss inflation by H2 2024 as pricing discipline held and frequency moderated. VER

ROE Scenarios — £200m UK Motor Portfolio

Three return-on-equity scenarios on a hypothetical £200m GWP Lloyd's UK motor portfolio (specialist mix: 35% fleet M5, 30% specialist private M2, 20% commercial M3, 10% motor trade M6, 5% motorcycle M4). Assumptions: 35% expense ratio; 60% combined ratio target on underwriting; investment income at 3.5% on float. ILLUS

Bull Scenario

+18%

Loss ratio 58%; combined 93%; investment income £7m; profit £36m on £200m equity base. Favourable: claims inflation moderating, telematics driving frequency reduction, OIC portal limiting BI severity. ILLUS

Base Scenario

+9%

Loss ratio 68%; combined 103%; investment income £7m; profit £18m. Near-breakeven underwriting; investment float generates acceptable return. EV losses and BI severity offset by rate increases. ILLUS

Bear Scenario

−7%

Loss ratio 82%; combined 117%; large bodily injury claims, Ogden-type reserve shock or EV battery fire cluster event. Investment income partially offsets. Loss £14m. Demonstrates motor volatility vs other Lloyd's classes. ILLUS
