Confidence Badge Framework

All factual claims in this hub carry inline confidence badges. The four tiers reflect data quality and source verifiability at the time of writing (April 2026).

VER — Verified

From primary legislation, official regulatory publications, named industry reports or ABI/Lloyd's official data. Can be independently confirmed.

EST — Estimate

Derived from industry sources, broker data, or reasonable inference from published ranges. Directionally reliable but not pinpoint-accurate.

ILLUS — Illustrative

Worked examples, hypothetical scenarios, indicative rate structures. Realistic and internally consistent, but not actual market data.

GAP — Data Gap

Data point known to exist but not publicly available at hub publication date. Marked where absence affects the analysis.

Knowledge Currency Note

This hub was built in April 2026. UK motor insurance is subject to rapid regulatory change (FCA, Ministry of Justice, DVLA). The following areas are most likely to have changed since publication: Automated Vehicles Act 2024 implementation guidance; OIC portal thresholds and tariff rates; EV-specific policy wording market practice; and the Personal Injury Discount Rate (which is reviewed periodically and may have changed for Scotland or Northern Ireland).

Known Data Gaps

  • GAP Lloyd's GWP by M code (M2–M6): Lloyd's does not publish GWP broken down to individual risk code level. The ~£800m total estimate is derived from industry analyst reports and is not Lloyd's official data. Actual split between M2, M3, M4, M5 and M6 is unknown publicly.
  • GAP UK motor claims inflation by component (2024 actuals): ABI publishes aggregate premium data; component-level breakdown (parts vs labour vs EV write-offs vs BI) at industry level is not publicly available. The decomposition chart in Rates & ROE is an illustrative estimate consistent with public commentary from major insurers.
  • GAP OIC portal fraud rate: MIB does not publicly disclose the proportion of OIC claims suspected as fraudulent or the rate of claim withdrawal after investigation. Industry commentary suggests fraudsters shifted patterns; published data on this is limited.
  • GAP EV total-loss threshold by manufacturer: Individual manufacturers' battery health assessment criteria (determining write-off vs repair) are commercially confidential and vary by brand. The statements in this hub represent general market practice rather than specific OEM protocols.
  • GAP Lloyd's motor combined ratio (market-level): Lloyd's publishes overall market combined ratio but not by risk code class. The illustrative combined ratios in this hub are not Lloyd's official published data.
