Surplus Treaty — Proportional Rater

Two calculators: the first computes the surplus cession for a single risk from its sum insured, the retention and the number of lines; the second prices the treaty result across the ceded portfolio. Inputs update live — indicative for training.

Overview History Timeline Loss Database Underwriting Rates & ROE Risk Mitigation Global Programme References Rater
How it prices Ceded SI = min(SI − retention, lines × retention), floored at 0. Cession ratio = ceded SI ÷ SI, and premium and losses cede pro-rata. The treaty panel then applies ceding commission, brokerage and profit commission to the ceded premium exactly as a quota share.
