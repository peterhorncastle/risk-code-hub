Surplus Treaty — Market & Loss Database

Like all proportional cover, a surplus treaty is stressed by loss-ratio deterioration and by catastrophe accumulation across many risks at once. These are the episodes that moved surplus terms and retentions.

Reading the database A surplus treaty's chief hidden danger is catastrophe accumulation — one event touching many ceded risks simultaneously. Without an event limit that turns a proportional property treaty into unpriced cat cover, which is why the Contract & Wordings module treats the event limit as essential on any accumulation-exposed book.
