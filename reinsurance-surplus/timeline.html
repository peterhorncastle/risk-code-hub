Modelling Catastrophe models push property cat protection toward XoL; surplus retreats to non-cat, heterogeneous risk sharing. VER

KRW hurricanes
Market Event limits and cat clauses tighten on surplus treaties exposed to accumulation across many risks. VER

Market Contingent BI on commercial-property surplus books drives an aggregation and wording review. VER
