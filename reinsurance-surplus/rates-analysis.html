Surplus Treaty — Rates & ROE

Like quota share, surplus has no rate-on-line — its cycle runs through the ceding commission and the retention. Higher retentions and lower commissions widen reinsurer margins; the reverse compresses them.

The proportional cycle runs through commission and retention: in soft markets reinsurers pay more ceding commission and accept lower retentions to win premium; in hard markets they cut commission, push retentions up and add event limits. The charts reconstruct a typical path for training. EST

The Surplus Cycle in Words

Soft market

Commissions rise, retentions ease

Abundant capacity lets cedants negotiate higher ceding commissions and lower retentions — cheap capacity, thin reinsurer margins.

Hard market (2023)

Commissions cut, retentions up

After losses, reinsurers cut ceding commission, push the retention line up and add event limits — restoring proportional margins.

Sliding scale

The aligned middle

A sliding-scale commission moves with the actual loss ratio, dampening the cycle for both parties automatically.

Reinsurer Return — Scenarios
