Legislation & Regulations

1
EU General Product Safety Regulation (EU) 2023/988 (GPSR 2024) Official Journal of the European Union, L 135, 23 May 2023. Effective 13 December 2024. Replaces GPSD 2001/95/EC. Applies to all non-food consumer products placed on the EU market including online marketplaces. VER eur-lex.europa.eu — Regulation (EU) 2023/988
2
EU Product Liability Directive 85/374/EEC (original) Council Directive 85/374/EEC of 25 July 1985 on the approximation of the laws, Member States concerning liability for defective products. First harmonised strict liability regime for defective products in the EU. VER eur-lex.europa.eu — Directive 85/374/EEC
3
EU Revised Product Liability Directive (2024) Directive of the European Parliament and of the Council on liability for defective products (recast), 2024. Extends strict liability to software, AI systems, and circular economy products; narrows development risk defence; shifts disclosure burden. Member state implementation within 2 years. VER eur-lex.europa.eu — Directive 2024/2853
4
Consumer Protection Act 1987 (UK) UK Parliament. Implements EU Product Liability Directive 85/374/EEC into English law. Strict liability for defective products causing death, personal injury, or property damage exceeding £275. Development risk defence retained post-Brexit. VER legislation.gov.uk — Consumer Protection Act 1987
5
REACH Regulation (EC) No 1907/2006 Regulation of the European Parliament and of the Council concerning the Registration, Evaluation, Authorisation and Restriction of Chemicals. Cornerstone EU chemical safety legislation. VER eur-lex.europa.eu — REACH Regulation
6
EU Medical Device Regulation (EU) 2017/745 (MDR) Regulation of the European Parliament and of the Council on medical devices. Fully applicable from May 2021 (with transitional provisions). Replaces MDD 93/42/EEC. CE marking via Notified Body required for Classes IIa/IIb/III. VER eur-lex.europa.eu — MDR 2017/745
7
EU Representative Actions Directive 2020/1828 Directive of the European Parliament and of the Council on representative actions for the protection of the collective interests of consumers. Enables consumer organisations to bring collective redress actions across EU member states. Implementation by member states required by 2023. VER eur-lex.europa.eu — Directive 2020/1828
8
Construction (Design and Management) Regulations 2015 (CDM 2015) UK Statutory Instrument 2015 No. 51. Health and safety regulation for construction projects. Establishes Principal Designer and Principal Contractor roles; requires F10 notification for notifiable projects. VER legislation.gov.uk — CDM 2015
9
Occupiers' Liability Act 1957 & 1984 (UK) UK Parliament. 1957 Act: common duty of care to lawful visitors. 1984 Act: duty of care in respect of trespassers. Foundation of premises public liability law in England and Wales. VER legislation.gov.uk — Occupiers' Liability Act 1957

Case Law

10
Donoghue v Stevenson [1932] AC 562 (House of Lords) The foundational negligence case establishing the modern neighbour principle and duty of care — including manufacturer liability to ultimate consumer regardless of privity of contract. Lord Atkin's judgment created the legal basis for products liability insurance. VER bailii.org — Donoghue v Stevenson [1932]
11
Rylands v Fletcher (1868) LR 3 HL 330 House of Lords. Established strict liability for the escape of dangerous things from land. Foundational precedent for non-fault liability theory; underpins strict liability doctrine that informed later product liability legislation. VER Available via Westlaw UK; BAILII: [1868] UKHL 1
12
Volkswagen AG v Various Claimants (UK Dieselgate Litigation) UK civil litigation arising from the Dieselgate emissions fraud (2015–). Multiple proceedings in England and Wales courts; settled 2022. Distinct from US class action ($25bn). Demonstrates European civil liability route for corporate fraud affecting consumers. VER See: Which? Ltd v Volkswagen AG & Others; Merchant Cash Advance Ltd v Volkswagen Group United Kingdom Ltd
13
Grenfell Tower Inquiry (2017 onwards) Phase 1 (October 2019) and Phase 2 (2022–2024) reports. 72 deaths; cladding system failed fire safety standards. Products liability claims against Arconic (ACM cladding manufacturer), Celotex (insulation), Kingspan (insulation). Long-term liability development ongoing. VER grenfelltowerinquiry.org.uk

Known Data Gaps

Data Limitations — GL Non-USA Hub

The following are known limitations in the data underpinning this hub. Users should be aware of these gaps when applying hub content to real underwriting decisions.

  • GAP Lloyd's published breakdown of N/NC/NR/NS GWP by code: Lloyd's does not publish detailed GWP statistics at the sub-class level (N, NC, NR, NS separately). The £3bn Lloyd's non-US GL estimate is derived from aggregate liability class data and market commentary, not direct Lloyd's disclosure.
  • GAP Non-US GL combined ratio by sub-class: Lloyd's publishes combined ratios at the "Liability" class level, not at the N/NC/NR/NS code level. The combined ratio estimates in the Rates & ROE module are directional estimates derived from broker market analyses and academic sources.
  • GAP Quantified impact of EU Representative Actions Directive on GL claims frequency: The Directive was implemented in 2023 by most member states. As of early 2025, insufficient claims development has occurred to quantify the frequency and severity impact. This remains a forward-looking risk rather than an empirically quantifiable exposure.
  • GAP Australia social inflation loss cost data: Australian GL claims cost inflation data from APRA is available in aggregate but is not publicly broken down by liability class or by social inflation component specifically. The Australia social inflation score is derived from market commentary and Swiss Re qualitative analysis.
  • GAP Revised EU PLD (2024) — Quantified premium impact: The revised EU Product Liability Directive (2024) extends strict liability to software and AI systems. No actuarial loss cost data exists for these newly covered product categories. Premium impact on NR technology products lines is currently unquantifiable and represents a significant forward-looking uncertainty.
