CASE
1932

Donoghue v Stevenson — Duty of Care Established

House of Lords creates modern negligence tort — manufacturer owes duty of care directly to ultimate consumer regardless of privity of contract. VER Foundation of all subsequent products and public liability law in common law jurisdictions worldwide. Triggers expansion of non-marine GL underwriting at Lloyd's.

REG
1957 & 1984

Occupiers' Liability Acts 1957 & 1984 (UK)

Occupiers' Liability Act 1957 codifies duty owed to lawful visitors; 1984 Act extends duty to trespassers. VER Creates the statutory foundation for public/premises liability insurance in England and Wales. Establishes the underwriting requirement for any business operating premises open to third parties.

LOS
1961–1973

Thalidomide — Mass Casualty Pharmaceutical Liability

Thalidomide sedative causes ~10,000 birth defects worldwide; UK distributor Distillers reaches £28m settlement (1973). VER Reveals inadequacy of existing product liability law for pharmaceutical mass-tort events. Triggers calls for strict liability reform across Europe. Influences 1985 EU PLD framework.

REG
1969 / 1972

Employers' Liability (Compulsory Insurance) Act 1969

Compulsory EL insurance required for UK employers (effective 1972). VER Separates EL from general GL market — creates two distinct product lines. Lloyd's GL underwriters begin distinguishing clearly between public liability and employer liability risks, laying groundwork for modern code segmentation.

LOS
1976

Seveso Disaster — Industrial Liability and Environmental Contamination

ICMESA chemical plant explosion near Seveso, Italy releases dioxin cloud, contaminating ~18km². VER Major public and environmental liability event. Triggers EU Seveso Directive I (1982), the precursor to modern industrial accident regulation across Europe. Establishes principle that industrial operators bear strict liability for environmental contamination of third-party land.

LOS
1984

Bhopal — The Largest Industrial Liability Event Outside the USA

Union Carbide India gas leak kills ~15,000 (est.), injures hundreds of thousands. EST Settlement (1989): $470m — widely condemned as wholly inadequate. VER Demonstrates the liability protection gap in developing-world industrial disasters. Raises questions about multinational parent liability for subsidiary operations. Influences international casualty policy wording development for parent/subsidiary liability.

REG
1985

EU Product Liability Directive 85/374/EEC — Strict Liability Across Europe

Council Directive 85/374/EEC imposes strict liability on producers of defective products for personal injury and property damage throughout the European Community. VER No need to prove fault — only defect, damage and causation. Development risk defence permitted by Member States. Products aggregate limits become standard in EU products liability policies. Lloyd's NR book expands substantially to serve European manufacturer demand.

REG
1987

UK Consumer Protection Act 1987

Implements EU PLD into English law — strict liability for defective products. VER UK manufacturers, importers and own-brand retailers all potentially liable as "producers." Triggers mass purchase of products liability policies and review of limits adequacy across UK manufacturing sector. Claims-made vs occurrence debate intensifies for long-latency product claims.

PRD
1996

BSE/vCJD — Food Products Liability Crisis

UK government confirms link between bovine spongiform encephalopathy (BSE) and variant Creutzfeldt-Jakob disease (vCJD) in humans. VER EU beef export ban imposed; major food products liability event. Liability claims against beef suppliers, abattoirs and retailers develop over subsequent decade. Reveals long-latency risk in food products liability: exposure may be incurred years before disease manifests.

MKT
2001

9/11 & Lloyd's Reserve Strengthening — GL Market Hardens

September 11 attacks cause ~$35bn insured loss. VER Broader Lloyd's market reprices; non-US GL participates in across-board hardening. Simultaneously, Equitas (Lloyd's run-off vehicle) crystallises asbestos-related liabilities from 1960s-80s Lloyd's syndicates. VER Reinforces long-tail reserving discipline. Non-US GL rates rise 15–30% in 2002–2003. EST

REG
2006

EU REACH Regulation — Chemical Products Liability

Regulation EC/1907/2006 (REACH) — Registration, Evaluation, Authorisation and Restriction of Chemicals — shifts burden of proof to manufacturers/importers to demonstrate chemical safety. VER Non-compliance creates products liability exposure for chemical manufacturers and downstream users. EU SVHC (substances of very high concern) authorisation list creates ongoing underwriting trigger for chemical sector NR policies.

PRD
2010–2014

Takata Airbag Recall — Global Products Liability (Non-US Dimension)

Defective ammonium nitrate inflators in Takata airbags cause fatal shrapnel injuries. ~100 million vehicles recalled globally — largest automotive safety recall in history. VER US class action settled; non-US dimension: Asian-market vehicles, European recalls, manufacturer insolvency (2017). Non-US GL policies (NR) respond for non-US bodily injury claims. Lloyd's non-US casualty book affected. EST

PRD
2011–2013

PIP Breast Implant Scandal — Landmark European Products Liability

Poly Implant Prothèse (PIP), France, used industrial silicone (not certified medical grade) in breast implants supplied to ~400,000 women globally. VER French regulator recalled implants December 2011; PIP declared bankrupt. Liability fell to surgeons, hospitals and distributors — manufacturer had no recovery. Lloyd's NR policies for medical device distributors and cosmetic surgery providers respond. Ongoing French civil litigation through 2020s.

LOS
2015

Volkswagen Dieselgate — Pan-European Products & D&O Liability

Defeat device software in ~11 million VW, Audi, Porsche, SEAT, Škoda diesel vehicles manipulates emissions tests in EU and elsewhere. VER European regulatory fines (€1.5bn+ in Germany alone); civil litigation in UK, Germany, France, Australia. Distinct from US $25bn class settlement. European GL and D&O claims develop. German class action (Musterfeststellungsklage) novel mechanism — 400,000 consumers registered. VER

LOS
2017

Grenfell Tower — Products & Public Liability, Cladding

Grenfell Tower fire, London — 72 deaths; rapid fire spread attributed to ACM cladding panels (Arconic) and polyisocyanurate insulation. VER Products liability claims against Arconic (cladding manufacturer), Studio E Architects, the Kensington and Chelsea TMO, Rydon (contractor) and sub-contractors. Public inquiry ongoing. Insured losses distributed across products, public liability, D&O and professional indemnity policies. Long-tail claims still developing as of 2024.

REG
2019–2023

EU Representative Actions Directive — Collective Redress Arrives in Europe

Directive 2020/1828 on Representative Actions enables qualified consumer organisations to bring collective redress actions across EU on behalf of consumers. VER Member states implement by 2023. Potential to dramatically aggregate non-US products liability claims — analogous (in concept, not yet in scale) to US class actions. Significant underwriting concern for products, pharmaceutical and food sector NR policies.

PRD
2020–2021

COVID-19 — Liability Claims and Policy Response

COVID-19 generates GL claims across multiple dimensions: occupier's liability (duty of care re COVID transmission at premises); food products liability (contamination allegations); personal protective equipment (PPE defect claims — defective masks, gloves). VER Communicable disease exclusions — Lloyd's market issues guidance on application; LMA5400 cyber/communicable disease model clause widely adopted. EST

MKT
2020–2023

Social Inflation Reaches Non-US Markets

Concept of "social inflation" — rising claim costs driven by litigation funding, increasing jury awards and changing public attitudes — previously considered mainly a US phenomenon, begins manifesting in non-US jurisdictions. VER Australia: third-party litigation funding growth; class actions accelerating. UK: litigation funding enabling group actions. Key Lloyd's market response: tighter aggregate controls, claims-made conversion for long-tail risks, social inflation loading in GL rates. EST

REG
2024

EU General Product Safety Regulation (GPSR) — Digital-Age Product Safety

GPSR (Regulation EU 2023/988) effective 13 December 2024 — replaces GPSD 2001. VER Extends obligations to online marketplace operators (Amazon, Alibaba treated as distributors for safety purposes); mandatory incident reporting; digital product passports for traceability; new enforcement powers including RAPEX-successor system. Fundamental change in non-EU (incl. UK post-Brexit) product safety compliance requirements with direct liability implications.

REG
2024

Revised EU Product Liability Directive — AI and Software Liability

Revised EU PLD (replacing Directive 85/374/EEC) agreed 2024 — extends strict liability to software, AI systems, digital services and circular economy (refurbished) products. VER Development risk defence narrowed; disclosure obligations (court can order producer to disclose evidence). Online marketplace operators in scope as producers. Significant expansion of products liability exposure for technology companies and AI developers in EU market. Long-term underwriting implications for NR policies.
