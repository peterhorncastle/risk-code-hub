Every factual claim in this hub carries one of four inline confidence badges. This page defines them, audits the key claims, lists the underlying sources, and discloses the known data gaps. VER

Confidence Badge Legend

VER Verified

Sourced from a primary public record — legislation, regulator publication, court filing, or company financial disclosure. High confidence.

EST Estimated

Market/broker consensus or triangulation from secondary sources where primary data is not public. Directional.

ILLUS Illustrative

Constructed worked example or scenario built to demonstrate method, not a reported figure.

GAP Data Gap

Publicly unavailable or undisclosed; flagged so the reader knows the limit of the evidence.

Primary & Regulatory Sources

L1
Magnuson–Moss Warranty Act (15 U.S.C. §§2301–2312) VER
US Congress, 1975. Foundational US warranty disclosure law. uscode.house.gov.
L2
Supply of Extended Warranties on Domestic Electrical Appliances Order 2005 (SI 2005/37) VER
UK SI, 2005. 45-day cooling-off, annual cancellation, price transparency. legislation.gov.uk.
L3
EU Directive 1999/44/EC — Sale of Consumer Goods & Associated Guarantees VER
EU, 1999. 2-year statutory guarantee. Superseded by Directive (EU) 2019/771. eur-lex.europa.eu.
L4
Directive (EU) 2024/1799 — Right to Repair VER
EU, 2024. 5–10 year repair-service obligation; spare parts to independent repairers. eur-lex.europa.eu.
L5
FCA PS23/2 — Consumer Duty: Final Rules and Guidance VER
FCA, 2022. Fair-value obligation, effective 31 July 2023. fca.org.uk.
L7
ASIC Report 492 — Add-on Insurance in Australia VER
ASIC, 2016. Found loss ratios of 9–19% on some add-on products; precursor to deferred sales model. asic.gov.au.
I2
Allstate — SquareTrade Acquisition, January 2017 VER
Allstate, 2017. $1.4bn acquisition. investor.allstate.com.
I3
Grand View Research — Extended Warranty Market Size Report 2024 EST
2024. Market sizing; varies by provider — treat as directional. grandviewresearch.com.
I4
Microsoft 10-K (2007) — Xbox 360 Warranty Charge VER
Microsoft, 2007. $1.05bn RROD warranty charge. SEC EDGAR.
I5
Apple Annual Reports — AppleCare Revenue EST
Apple, 2020–2024. Not separately disclosed; analyst estimate $5–7bn within Services segment.
I6
Lopez v. LG Electronics USA — US Class Settlement, 2022 VER
US DNJ. $198m settlement, linear-compressor failures 2014–2019. Public court filing.
I7
In re Apple Device Performance Litigation — N.D. Cal. 2020 VER
US N.D. Cal. $500m battery-slowdown settlement (preliminary approval). Public docket.
I1
Warranty Week — Industry Newsletter & Data Repository EST
warrantyweek.com. Primary trade source for warranty accruals and loss-ratio commentary.
I10
EV Battery Warranty Underwriting — LMA Working Paper, 2023 EST
Lloyd's Market Association, 2023. Emerging-risk paper on EV battery cover, triggers and accumulation. lmalloyds.com (member access).

Known Data Gaps

Segment-level premium volumes GAP

Public market totals differ by methodology; electronics/white-goods/motor/home splits are not consistently disclosed.

Private programme terms GAP

Ceding commissions, attachment points and TPA economics are confidential; benchmarks here are indicative.

EV battery long-run loss development GAP

Mass-market EV fleet is under ~10 years old; loss development beyond Year 5 is essentially unknown.

AppleCare exact revenue GAP

Not separately disclosed by Apple; the $5–7bn figure is an analyst estimate.

Precise fraud rates GAP

AD fraud shares (5–15% US, 3–8% UK) are market estimates that vary by geography and data-sharing.

Educational use This hub is for training and market-intelligence purposes. Figures marked EST/ILLUS are indicative; regulatory summaries are simplified — always consult the primary legislation and current sources before relying on any figure in a live placement.
