Known Data Gaps

Segment-level premium volumes GAP

Public market totals differ by methodology; electronics/white-goods/motor/home splits are not consistently disclosed.

Private programme terms GAP

Ceding commissions, attachment points and TPA economics are confidential; benchmarks here are indicative.

EV battery long-run loss development GAP

Mass-market EV fleet is under ~10 years old; loss development beyond Year 5 is essentially unknown.

AppleCare exact revenue GAP

Not separately disclosed by Apple; the $5–7bn figure is an analyst estimate.

Precise fraud rates GAP

AD fraud shares (5–15% US, 3–8% UK) are market estimates that vary by geography and data-sharing.

Manufacturer-insolvency cover for home batteries GAP

No insurance product has been identified that pays out when a home battery manufacturer fails; warranties are enforceable only against the manufacturer (GivEnergy, April 2026) and IBGs exclude manufacturer failure.
