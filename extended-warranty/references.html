Regulatory & Legislative Sources

[L1]
Magnuson–Moss Warranty Act (15 U.S.C. §§ 2301–2312) LEGAL
US Congress, 1975. The foundational US warranty disclosure law. Full text: uscode.house.gov. Referenced in History [1], Timeline [1], Underwriting [—], Global [3].
[L2]
Supply of Extended Warranties on Domestic Electrical Appliances Order 2005 (SI 2005/37) LEGAL
UK Statutory Instrument, 2005. Implements Competition Commission recommendations — 45-day cooling-off, annual cancellation, price transparency. legislation.gov.uk. Referenced in History [10], Timeline [—], Global [2].
[L3]
EU Directive 1999/44/EC — Sale of Consumer Goods and Associated Guarantees LEGAL
European Parliament and Council, 1999. Establishes 2-year statutory guarantee across EU member states. eur-lex.europa.eu. Superseded by Directive (EU) 2019/771. Referenced in History [6], Timeline [6], Overview [11].
[L4]
Directive (EU) 2024/1799 — Right to Repair LEGAL
European Parliament and Council, 2024. Requires manufacturers to offer repair services for covered categories for 5–10 years; spare parts and repair tools available to independent repairers. eur-lex.europa.eu. Referenced in Timeline [18], Global [4].
[L5]
FCA PS23/2 — Consumer Duty: Final Rules and Guidance LEGAL
Financial Conduct Authority, July 2022. Policy Statement implementing the Consumer Duty, effective 31 July 2023. fca.org.uk/policy-statements. Referenced in History [17], Timeline [17], Global [2].
[L6]
Financial Services and Markets Act 2000 (FSMA) LEGAL
UK Parliament, 2000. Primary legislation regulating financial services and insurance in the UK. legislation.gov.uk. Referenced in Overview [9], History [—].
[L7]
ASIC Report 492 — Add-on Insurance in Australia LEGAL
Australian Securities and Investments Commission, September 2016. Found loss ratios of 9–19% on some add-on products. Precursor to deferred sales model. asic.gov.au. Referenced in Overview [12], Timeline [15].
[L8]
ASIC Regulatory Guide 275 — Deferred Sales Model for Add-on Insurance LEGAL
Australian Securities and Investments Commission, 2021. Implements 4-day deferral requirement for add-on insurance products. asic.gov.au/rg275. Referenced in History [12], Timeline [15], Global [5].
[L9]
UK Competition Commission — Extended Warranties on Domestic Electrical Appliances: Final Report, 2003 LEGAL
UK Competition Commission, December 2003. Found adverse effects on competition; recommended regulatory remedies. nationalarchives.gov.uk. Referenced in History [10], Timeline [7].

Industry & Market Sources

[I1]
Warranty Week — Industry Newsletter & Data Repository INDUSTRY
Eric Arnum (ed.), warrantyweek.com. The primary trade publication covering extended warranty industry data, manufacturer warranty accruals (from 10-K filings), and market trends. Referenced throughout — Overview [—], Underwriting [1], [2].
[I2]
Allstate Corporation — SquareTrade Acquisition Press Release & Investor Presentation, January 2017 INDUSTRY
Allstate Corporation, 2017. $1.4bn acquisition of SquareTrade announced. investor.allstate.com. Referenced in Timeline [12], History [14].
[I3]
Grand View Research — Extended Warranty Market Size Report 2024 ESTIMATE
Grand View Research, 2024. Market sizing estimates for global extended warranty industry. grandviewresearch.com. Referenced in Overview [—] (market size figures). Note: Market sizing figures vary significantly between research providers; treat as directional estimate.
[I4]
Microsoft Corporation 10-K (2007) — Xbox 360 Warranty Charge VERIFIED
Microsoft Corporation, Annual Report 2007. Discloses $1.05bn warranty charge for Xbox 360 extended warranty extension covering RROD failures. SEC EDGAR filing. Referenced in Timeline [8], Database [—].
[I5]
Apple Corporation Annual Reports (AppleCare Revenue) ESTIMATE
Apple Inc., Annual Reports 2020–2024. AppleCare revenue reported within Services segment; not separately disclosed. Analyst estimates of $5–7bn based on Services segment breakdown. Referenced in History [13], Timeline [10].
[I6]
LG Electronics — US Class Action Settlement (Lopez v. LG Electronics USA), 2022 VERIFIED
US District Court, District of New Jersey. LG agrees to $198m settlement covering linear compressor failures in LG refrigerators 2014–2019. Court filing publicly accessible. Referenced in Timeline [11], Database [—].
[I7]
Apple iPhone Battery Settlement — In re Apple Inc. Device Performance Litigation, N.D. Cal. 2020 VERIFIED
US District Court, Northern District of California. Apple settles battery slowdown class action for $500m (2020 preliminary approval). Court docket publicly accessible. Referenced in Database [—].
[I8]
EIOPA — Report on Thematic Review on Product Oversight and Governance (POG), 2022 LEGAL
European Insurance and Occupational Pensions Authority, 2022. Reviews product governance for insurance products including extended warranties and add-on products. eiopa.europa.eu. Referenced in Global [4].
[I9]
FTC — Extended Warranty and Service Contract Enforcement Actions (2023) LEGAL
US Federal Trade Commission, 2023. Actions against vehicle warranty robocall operators. ftc.gov/enforcement. Referenced in Database [—].
[I10]
EV Battery Warranty Underwriting — Lloyd's Market Association (LMA) Working Paper, 2023 INDUSTRY
Lloyd's Market Association, 2023. Working paper on emerging risks in extended warranty including EV battery cover — classification, trigger definition, and accumulation management. lmalloyds.com (member access). Referenced in Underwriting [9], Risk Mitigation [8].
