Agricultural Crop & Forestry Insurance

Lloyd's specialty agricultural insurance covering multi-peril crop insurance (MPCI), named-peril crop covers, weather index products, and standing timber/forestry for commercial growers, agribusinesses, and forest estate owners worldwide.

Agricultural and forestry insurance is among the oldest and most complex specialty lines in the Lloyd's market. The class operates at the intersection of weather risk, biological risk, geopolitical risk (food security), and government subsidy programmes — creating a unique underwriting environment where the private and public sectors are intimately intertwined. Lloyd's codes HA (crop/agricultural) and AG (forestry/silviculture) together cover the full spectrum from wheat fields in Kansas to timber estates in Scandinavia. VER

Risk Code Structure

HA

Agricultural Crop Insurance

Covers growing crops and agricultural produce against yield loss, revenue loss, and named perils. Includes multi-peril crop insurance (MPCI), named-peril policies (hail, frost, flood), weather index insurance, revenue protection, and crop-specific products (livestock, aquaculture, greenhouse). Primary markets: North America, Latin America, Europe, Australia, India, China.

AG

Forestry & Silviculture Insurance

Covers standing timber, plantation forestry, and commercial forest estates against fire, windstorm, flood, pest/disease, and accidental physical damage. Includes timber merchants' stock, timber in transit, and environmental liability for forest operations. Primary markets: Scandinavia, Canada, Australia, New Zealand, Chile, USA Pacific Northwest.

Coverage Taxonomy — HA Crop Insurance

Multi-Peril Crop Insurance (MPCI)

The broadest form of crop coverage — protects against yield loss from all insured perils including drought, excess moisture, frost, wind, flood, fire, insects, and disease. Government-subsidised in most major markets (US USDA RMA, EU CAP, India PMFBY). Written on area or individual farm yield basis. Requires satellite/drone yield verification at scale. VER

Named Peril — Hail

The most commonly purchased stand-alone crop cover worldwide. Hail causes immediate, objectively verifiable physical crop damage — making it a preferred underwriting risk. Sold both bundled with MPCI and standalone. European private market hail coverage (Central and Eastern Europe particularly active) is largely private-sector without government subsidy. VER

Revenue Protection

Combines yield risk and price risk — pays when farm revenue (yield × price) falls below a guaranteed level. The dominant MPCI product type in the US market (Revenue Protection and Revenue Protection with Harvest Price Exclusion). Settles using futures prices from CBOT/CME. Created a tight link between crop insurance and commodity derivatives markets. VER

Weather Index Insurance

Pays based on an objective weather parameter (rainfall, temperature, humidity index) rather than actual yield — eliminating moral hazard and basis risk at scale. Designed for smallholder farmers in emerging markets where individual yield assessment is not economically viable. Major programmes in India, Ethiopia, Kenya, Bangladesh, and Indonesia. Backed by IFC, World Bank, and Lloyd's market capacity. VER

Livestock & Aquaculture

Mortality insurance for commercial livestock (cattle, poultry, pigs) and farmed fish (salmon, trout, shrimp). Named-peril covers for disease (foot-and-mouth, ASF, avian influenza), accident, theft, and catastrophic mortality. Aquaculture coverage includes sea cage damage, escape events, and pollution/algal bloom mortality. Norway salmon farming is the world's largest aquaculture insurance market. EST

Greenhouse & Controlled Environment

Property and business interruption coverage for glasshouse and controlled-environment agriculture (CEA) including heated tomato/pepper/cucumber operations and vertical farming. Property damage from structural collapse (snow load, windstorm), technical failure, and fire. BI coverage for loss of growing crops in facility. Premiums typically higher per £ insured value than open-field crop due to concentration and capital intensity. EST

Coverage Taxonomy — AG Forestry Insurance

Standing Timber — Fire

The primary forestry peril globally. Covers commercial plantation and natural forest stands against fire damage, including wildfires spreading from adjoining land. Settlement based on standing timber value at time of loss (pre-merchantable timber, merchantable timber, or site rehabilitation cost). California, Australia, Portugal, and Canada represent the highest-exposure markets. VER

Standing Timber — Windstorm

Windstorm is the second major forestry peril. European windstorms (Lothar 1999, Kyrill 2007, Friederike 2018) have caused billions in standing timber losses. Softwood plantation forests in exposed coastal areas particularly vulnerable. Coverage typically written on a declared value basis with loss adjustment requiring post-storm cruise surveys. VER

Pest & Disease

Bark beetle (Ips typographus, Dendroctonus ponderosae) is the principal forest pest exposure — drought-stressed trees are highly susceptible and outbreaks can devastate millions of hectares. Fungal diseases (Hymenoscyphus fraxineus causing ash dieback, Phytophthora ramorum causing sudden oak death) represent an emerging catastrophe risk. Coverage is complex and sub-limited in most policies. EST

Timber Merchants & Stock

Marine-linked timber stock coverage for sawn timber, logs, and timber products in yards, on water, and in transit. Historically related to marine cargo classes. Covers physical damage to harvested timber from fire, flood, theft, and storm. Often combined with property coverage for sawmill and processing facility. VER

Lloyd's Market Participants

Lead Syndicates

Specialist agricultural syndicates at Lloyd's include Hamilton (formerly Ironshore), Convex, Chaucer, and Markel International. Most agricultural risk is led by syndicates with dedicated agri underwriting teams and access to specialist loss adjusters (crop surveyors, agronomists, cruising foresters). EST

MGAs / Coverholder Market

A significant proportion of agricultural volume is placed via specialist MGAs operating under Lloyd's binding authorities — particularly for US crop hail, European hail, and Australian specialty crop. MGAs provide local market access and agronomic expertise that Lloyd's syndicates rely on for underwriting accuracy. EST

Reinsurance Role

Lloyd's functions primarily as a reinsurer for government-backed MPCI programmes in the US, India, China, and Canada — providing excess-of-loss and quota share capacity to domestic carriers who front the risk. This means Lloyd's aggregate exposure to catastrophic crop loss events is substantial even when direct market share is modest. EST

Brokers & Specialists

Aon (specialist agricultural division), Gallagher (formerly Weald Risk), Willis Towers Watson (food & agriculture practice), and dedicated agri-brokers (CGB Gallagher, Farmweb) place agricultural risks into the Lloyd's market. Technical quality of presentation — including crop type, soil classification, irrigation status, historical yields — is fundamental to obtaining competitive terms. EST
