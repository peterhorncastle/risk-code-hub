Stop-Loss — Aggregate Loss-Ratio Rater

Price an aggregate stop-loss on the whole-year loss ratio. Enter the layer as loss-ratio points, the expected loss ratio and its volatility, and the loadings; the rater models the loss ratio as a normal distribution and returns the expected recovery, rate-on-line and the probability of attaching. Inputs update live — indicative for training.

How it prices The whole-account loss ratio is modelled as a normal distribution with the expected loss ratio as its mean and the volatility as its standard deviation. The expected recovery into the layer [attachment, exhaustion] uses the stop-loss formula E[(L−a)⁺] − E[(L−b)⁺], net of any cedant co-participation, times subject premium. That expected cost is loaded for risk and grossed up for expenses and brokerage. The normal model is a transparent teaching approximation — real stop-loss pricing uses the empirical or a skewed aggregate distribution.
