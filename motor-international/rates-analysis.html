IBNR & Development Considerations
FactorImpact on Reserves
Long-tail BI claims — injury severity developmentThird-party BI claims in some jurisdictions (South Africa RAF, certain EM markets) can take 3–7 years to reach final settlement — IBNR loading essential for BI-heavy territory exposure
NatCat aggregate tail — total loss settlementsAfter major hailstorm or flood events, the shortage of replacement vehicles and supply chain delays extends total loss claim settlement timelines — IBNR must reflect claims lifecycle, not just damage assessment date
EV battery fire repair uncertaintyNo established actuarial data on EV battery fire repair cost distributions; prudent underwriters apply +15–25% severity uplift IBNR for EV-inclusive portfolios pending 5+ years of development data
Currency and inflation developmentInternational motor claims in EM currencies with high inflation (Turkey, Argentina) can develop materially in GBP/USD terms after initial reserve setting — consider currency hedging or reserve in local currency with reviewed FX conversion
Fraud reserve adjustmentIn high-fraud territories (South Africa, parts of MENA), applying a "fraud reserve credit" to IBNR requires evidence of systematic SIU activity and recovery — prudent to reserve gross until fraud is confirmed and denied
