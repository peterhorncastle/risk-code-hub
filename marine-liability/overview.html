What Is Marine Liability?

Marine Liability is the class of insurance covering third-party legal liabilities arising from the ownership, operation, or management of ships and marine assets. It occupies a unique position in the Lloyd's market: the lion's share of global marine liability protection is provided by the thirteen International Group of P&I Clubs (IG Clubs) — non-profit mutual insurers that collectively cover approximately 90% of the world's ocean-going tonnage. Lloyd's (codes G, GC, GX) therefore operates in the spaces around, above, and between the mutual P&I system: writing risks the clubs cannot or will not cover, providing excess layers above IG pool limits, and offering commercial alternatives for operators who prefer fixed premium over mutual call structures. EST

The three key coverage categories within marine liability are: collision liability (Running Down Clause / RDC — typically written as a hull extension but reinsured or placed standalone through Lloyd's); Protection & Indemnity (P&I — crew, cargo, pollution, wreck removal, third-party BI/PD arising from ship operation); and specialist marine liability (ship repairer's liability, port and terminal operators liability, marine contractor liability, freight forwarder liability, yacht P&I, and small craft liability). VER

G — Marine Liability (Direct)

Direct marine liability placements at Lloyd's. Covers P&I-type risks (fixed premium alternative to club entry), collision/RDC standalone, ship repairer's liability (SRL), port and terminal operators liability (PTOL), marine contractor liability, freight forwarder liability, yacht P&I, and small craft third-party. Occurrence basis; IMO/MARPOL-compliant pollution cover available. Written on bespoke Lloyd's wording or LMAA/JL forms.

GC — Marine Liability (Coverholder / Binder)

Marine liability binder and coverholder arrangements. Typically covers small/medium fleets, inland waterway operators, recreational craft associations, yacht clubs, port authorities, and freight forwarder schemes. Binding authority issued to specialist marine MGAs (e.g. Navigators, Markel International, Ascot). Often includes automated rating for smaller risks below threshold. GC is the volume/distribution channel code.

GX — Marine Liability (Excess / Specialist)

Excess marine liability layers above P&I club retention or IG Pool limit ($3.1bn per vessel), and specialist structures. Includes: excess of loss above IG Pool (large tanker owners, cruise lines), clash reinsurance for P&I clubs, finite risk marine liability, specialist cruise passenger liability, offshore supply vessel (OSV) liability, and floating production unit (FPSO) liability where operational crew/pollution liability is involved.

Coverage Taxonomy

Marine liability covers an unusually wide range of third-party legal obligations. The following taxonomy maps the main product lines within G/GC/GX. VER

P&I — Core Club Risks (Fixed Premium)

  • Crew injury, illness, death (including repatriation)
  • Cargo damage / shortage / misdelivery
  • Oil pollution (MARPOL, OPA 90, CLC/Fund)
  • Collision with other vessels (¾ RDC usually) 
  • Property damage to fixed/floating objects (FFO)
  • Wreck removal (Nairobi Convention)
  • Stowaways / refugees / quarantine
  • Contractual liabilities (towage, salvage)

Collision Liability / RDC (Standalone)

  • Running Down Clause (RDC) — ¾ or 4/4 collision
  • Damage to other vessels and their cargo
  • Fixed/floating objects (BIMCO FFO clause)
  • Pollution arising from collision
  • Wreck removal post-collision
  • Often bundled with hull policy (IHC/ITC)
  • Lloyd's writes excess ¼ RDC above hull limit

Ship Repairer's Liability (SRL)

  • Damage to vessels in care/custody/control
  • Third-party bodily injury in yard
  • Pollution from yard operations
  • Consequential loss (loss of earnings/hire)
  • Tools and equipment property damage
  • Contractual liability for work performed
  • Lloyd's leading market for UK/European SRL

Port & Terminal Operators Liability (PTOL)

  • Cargo in care/custody/control
  • Third-party bodily injury (stevedores, visitors)
  • Environmental / pollution liability
  • Damage to vessels berthing/departing
  • Crane/equipment third-party damage
  • Harbour master and pilotage liability
  • Contractual liability for port operations

Freight Forwarder / MTO Liability

  • FIATA Standard T&Cs / BIFA T&Cs cargo liability
  • Errors and omissions in freight booking
  • Multi-modal transport operator (MTO) liability
  • Customs and declaration errors
  • Loss/damage during consolidation
  • CMR (road) / Warsaw (air) statutory limits
  • Errors in dangerous goods documentation

Specialist & Emerging Marine Liability

  • Cruise passenger liability (Athens Convention)
  • Yacht / small craft P&I and third-party
  • Offshore supply vessel (OSV) liability
  • Wind farm support vessel (SOV/CTV) liability
  • Autonomous vessel liability (MASS — emerging)
  • Wreck removal specialist (Nairobi excess)
  • Marine cyber liability (NMA 2914 / LMA 5403)
🚢 P&I Clubs vs. Lloyd's: The IG P&I Clubs are mutual organisations where shipowners share liabilities through a pool. Individual club retention is approximately $10m; claims from $10m to $100m are shared between clubs; above $100m, excess reinsurance is purchased. Lloyd's participates as a reinsurer of the IG Pool's excess layers and as a direct insurer for fixed-premium P&I (operators who prefer certainty of cost over mutual calls), specialist craft not eligible for club entry, and SRL/PTOL/freight forwarder risks that clubs do not cover.
