Known Data Gaps

Sub-class premium volumes GAP

Lloyd's does not consistently disclose premium at this sub-class level; market-size figures are estimates.

Private placement terms GAP

Programme structures, ceding commissions and rates by cedant are confidential; benchmarks here are indicative.

Loss-ratio & margin benchmarks GAP

Loss ratios and margins vary by year, territory and cedant and are rarely published; figures marked EST are directional.

Floating offshore wind mooring loss data GAP

Directly relevant operational and loss data for FOWT mooring systems are scarce (LMA JNRC, 2024).

Offshore floating solar claims experience GAP

Historical data on damage frequency and claims experience is inadequate (WTW, 2024); the technology is still at prototype and small-scale phase.

2026 softening by sub-class GAP

The published summary of the WTW 2026 review does not split premium movements between R1 and R3 or between offshore and onshore.
