Multinational Group A&H Programme — Coverage Layers

A multinational employer's group A&H programme typically operates on a master/local policy architecture. The master policy (often placed in the UK, Bermuda or Cayman) provides international coverage and sets benefit standards; local policies comply with country-specific compulsory social security and insurance requirements.

Compulsory A&H Coverage Requirements by Territory EST

Local compulsory requirements vary substantially. Multinational programmes must confirm local compliance in each operating territory. Always verify with local counsel — requirements change. EST

UK — Statutory Sick Pay (SSP)

SSP: £116.75/week (2024/25) for up to 28 weeks. Employer-funded; commercial insurance supplements above SSP. No compulsory worker PA insurance per se, but employers' liability insurance is compulsory for bodily injury claims.

Germany — Social Health / Accident

Statutory employer accident insurance (DGUV) is compulsory for all employers — covers work-related accidents and occupational disease. Statutory health insurance (GKV) mandatory for employees below income threshold. Commercial supplements the compulsory base.

France — Prévoyance

Collective branch agreements (CCN) often mandate minimum disability and death coverage — the "prévoyance" obligation. Most sectors have minimum standards exceeding bare state benefits. Employer must provide compliant group cover or face surcharge.

Australia — Workers' Compensation

Each state has a workers' compensation scheme providing work-related PA cover. Employer must maintain state-specific workers' comp insurance. Commercial PA supplements non-work injuries and adds income protection benefits above statutory minimums.

USA — Workers' Comp + ACA

Workers' compensation (work injury) is compulsory in most states. ACA mandates minimum employer health coverage for employers with 50+ FTE employees. Defence Base Act (DBA) compulsory for US contractors working overseas. Complex multi-state compliance.

Singapore — Work Injury Compensation

Work Injury Compensation Act (WICA) requires employers to maintain work injury insurance. MOM (Ministry of Manpower) sets minimum benefit levels. Commercial group PA provides supplementary coverage above WICA statutory limits for all employees.

UAE / Middle East

UAE mandates basic health insurance for employees in Dubai (HAAD) and Abu Dhabi (DHA). Qatar, Saudi Arabia have similar employer health obligations. Commercial group PA supplements and adds disability/death benefits. No UAE state workers' compensation equivalent.

Nigeria / Sub-Saharan Africa

Variable local compulsory requirements; enforcement limited. Employers' Compensation Act 2010 (Nigeria) requires registration with NSITF. Commercial group PA is the primary meaningful protection. Local admitted policies preferred by regulators; non-admitted basis where necessary.

Russia / Belarus

Generally excluded from international group A&H programmes post-2022 sanctions. State social security provides basic disability support. Commercial solutions extremely limited.

Reinsurance Structure for Group A&H

Quota Share Reinsurance

The most common structure for group PMI and group PA books. The reinsurer takes a fixed percentage of every risk in the portfolio in exchange for the same percentage of premium, less a ceding commission. Provides capital relief and limits volatility. Typically 25–50% QS retention for syndicates. EST

Excess of Loss (Catastrophe XL)

Protects against accumulation losses from a single event affecting multiple employees — notably an air crash, industrial accident, or terrorism event killing or injuring a large number of employees from the same scheme. Attachment point set above the maximum feasible individual group claim. Catastrophe XL for group A&H typically attaches at £500,000–£5m per occurrence depending on portfolio size. ILLUS

Aggregate Stop-Loss

Protects against a year when total claims exceed an aggregate amount regardless of individual event size. Relevant for group PMI and income protection books with systemic claims patterns (e.g. pandemic, widespread mental health crisis). Aggregate XL typically attaches when loss ratio exceeds 110–130% of expected. ILLUS

Programme Architecture Notes

  • ›Lead Lloyd's syndicate: Takes 35% of master policy; sets terms and conditions for all following markets
  • ›Claims cooperation clause: All participating markets must cooperate in claims investigation and agree on settlement strategy before any payment on claims above £250,000
  • ›Assistance company: International SOS (or equivalent) on 24/7 standby for medical emergencies, repatriation and security evacuations for all internationally mobile staff
  • ›Annual programme review: HR and broker review annually — headcount changes, new territories entered, benefit benchmarking against sector peers
