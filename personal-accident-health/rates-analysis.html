Market Cycle — Key Eras

2010–2015 — Soft Market

Sustained softening. Low interest rates compressed investment returns. A&H capacity abundant; group PMI rates competitive. Travel insurance consolidated via aggregators driving down premium.

2016–2019 — Stable / Mildly Hardening

Post-PPI regulation improves conduct. Sports disability losses in rugby prompt selective hardening. Mental health frequency begins rising. Group IP duration risk re-emerges. Modest rate increases in disability lines.

2020–2021 — COVID Shock

Travel (KT) collapses. Pandemic exclusions deployed en masse. Long-COVID disability uncertainty. Lloyd's A&H losses elevated. Group life mortality increases in heavily affected regions. Capacity reduces in travel reinsurance.

2022–2023 — Hard Cycle Begins

Mental health claims +40% vs. pre-pandemic (EST). Long-COVID disability duration uncertainty. Medical inflation 8–10% p.a. (UK). Group PMI and IP re-rated. Travel rebuilds but pandemic exclusions remain. War PA active (Ukraine, Middle East).

2024 — Sustained Hard Market

Group IP pricing +10–25% in worst-affected segments (EST). PMI double-digit rate increases driven by NHS waiting list driven demand and medical inflation. Travel consolidating post-COVID losses. FCA Consumer Duty compliance costs impacting margins.

Base Scenario
+6–12%
Mental health claims remain elevated but manageable; medical inflation 8% p.a.; long-COVID reserves hold; group IP re-rated to adequate levels; Consumer Duty compliance cost absorbed
Bear Scenario
−10–20%
New pandemic event; long-COVID duration far exceeds reserving assumptions; mental health claims frequency doubles; medical inflation 12%+; regulatory intervention on premium increases
