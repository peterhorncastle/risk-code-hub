PA&H Rating Formula — Core Structure

Annual Premium = Base Morbidity Rate
             × Sum Insured Factor
             × Occupation Class Loading
             × Age/Gender Factor
             × Benefit/Indemnity Period Factor
             × Deferred Period Discount
             × Sports/Leisure Loading
             × Claims Experience Factor
             × Geographic Territory Factor

Base rates derived from morbidity tables (CMI data, insurer experience). All rates illustrative. ILLUS

Key Rating Factors by Sub-Class

Personal Accident (KA) — Key Factors

  • Occupation class (Class 1–5)
  • Age and gender (morbidity tables)
  • Benefit type: AD&D vs. disability income vs. both
  • Deferred period (1, 4, 13, 26, 52 weeks)
  • Benefit period (2yr, 5yr, to retirement)
  • Sum insured / benefit amount
  • Sports/leisure endorsements
  • Pre-existing condition status (IMU or moratorium)

Medical Expenses / PMI (KC/KG) — Key Factors

  • Age at entry (primary driver of medical claims cost)
  • Geographic scope (UK-only vs. worldwide)
  • Hospital list access (full list vs. guided)
  • Mental health coverage extent
  • Pre-existing condition basis (MORI/IMU/continued)
  • Aggregate excess / co-payment level
  • Out-patient treatment limits
  • Medical inflation trend loading

Travel Insurance (KT) — Key Factors

  • Trip type: single trip vs. annual multi-trip
  • Destination region (Europe / worldwide / USA+Canada)
  • Age (major rating factor; significant loading 65+)
  • Duration of trip
  • Pre-existing medical conditions
  • Winter sports extension
  • Cancellation sum insured
  • Excess amount / co-payment

Sports Disability (KS) — Key Factors

  • Sport type and contact level (rugby front row vs. cricket batsman)
  • Playing level: professional / semi-professional / amateur
  • Player position (prop / flanker / backs in rugby)
  • Contract value and earnings protected
  • Existing injuries / medical history
  • Sum insured (career earnings estimate)
  • Waiting/elimination period (first 26 or 52 weeks)
  • Club/team vs. individual policy

Group A&H (KG) — Key Factors

  • Group size (free cover limit increases with size)
  • Industry / SIC code of employer
  • Geographic spread of employees
  • Benefit structure (level/multiples of salary)
  • Mental health claims history
  • Age profile of workforce
  • Absence management / rehabilitation services
  • Group claims experience (3–5 year basis)

Maritime PA & K&R (KM) — Key Factors

  • Crew nationality mix (mortality/morbidity tables vary)
  • Vessel type and trade route
  • Operating territory (JWC HRA, piracy risk areas)
  • Crew certification and vessel flag
  • MLC 2006 compliance status
  • K&R: territory, ransom limit, insured's profile
  • Prior kidnap incidents (claims experience)
  • Medical evacuation logistics costs

Worked Example A — Group Personal Accident Scheme (KG) ILLUS

Risk: Manufacturing employer — 500 employees (Leeds, UK)
IndustryLight manufacturing / assembly (SIC 2811)
Employees500; age profile 30–55 (average 41)
BenefitAD&D: 3× annual salary; TTD: 52-week benefit; PTD: lump sum 5× salary
Average salary£34,000
Deferred period13 weeks (Employer SSP covers first 13 weeks)
Occupation classMix: 30% Class 1 (admin/management), 70% Class 3 (production floor)
Prior claims experience3 years: 2 minor injury claims; nil AD&D claims

Base PA rate (Class 3 average)~0.30% of covered benefit ILLUS
Occupation loading applied×1.4 (Class 3 weighting) ILLUS
Age factor×1.2 (average age 41) ILLUS
Experience discount×0.85 (favourable 3-year loss record) ILLUS
Total covered benefit500 × £34,000 × 5 (PTD multiple) = £85,000,000
Indicative annual premium~£85m × 0.30% × 1.4 × 1.2 × 0.85 ≈ ~£363,000 p.a. ILLUS

Illustrative only. Actual premium depends on syndicate appetite, benefit structure detail, reinsurance arrangements and market conditions at time of placement.
