Module 00 — Overview

Credit & Contract Frustration

Trade credit, whole turnover, single-risk credit, excess-of-loss credit, and contract frustration insurance — protecting exporters and lenders against buyer default and politically triggered non-payment across the global trade finance ecosystem.

Overview History Timeline Loss Database Underwriting Rates & ROE Risk Mitigation Global Programme References Rater Programme Builder

Credit insurance protects sellers, exporters, and lenders against the failure of buyers or borrowers to pay what they owe. It is one of the oldest forms of commercial insurance — originally designed to allow merchants to extend open-account credit to distant buyers they could not personally evaluate. Modern credit insurance underpins hundreds of billions of dollars of global trade annually, enabling sellers to offer competitive payment terms while protecting their balance sheets against bad debt losses. VER

CR

Credit Insurance

Single-risk and excess-of-loss credit insurance. Covers buyer insolvency and protracted default on short- and medium-term trade credit, project finance, and structured trade. Primary private market credit code at Lloyd's.

CF

Contract Frustration

Covers frustrated export or supply contracts where non-performance is caused by a political or government action — import/export prohibition, contract cancellation by a sovereign/state buyer, or government-imposed force majeure. Bridges credit insurance and political risk.

WT

Whole Turnover Credit

Whole turnover (WT) policies cover the seller's entire debtor book — all approved buyers — on a portfolio basis. This is the dominant product of the large specialist credit insurers (Allianz Trade, Atradius, Coface) and the most common SME-facing credit insurance form.

Product Taxonomy

Whole Turnover (WT)

Covers the policyholder's entire debtor portfolio on revolving basis. Annual policy with ongoing credit limit monitoring. Dominant product globally; offered by specialist credit insurers and Lloyd's syndicates via binding authorities. VER

Single-Risk Credit (CR)

Covers a specific, named buyer transaction — typically large-value medium-term export contracts (capital goods, infrastructure, commodities). Placed at Lloyd's or specialist markets on a per-transaction basis. VER

Excess of Loss Credit (CR XL)

Protects the insured's aggregate credit losses above a first-loss retention (excess point) in a given policy year — analogous to a stop-loss. Used by large exporters, banks, and fintech lenders with diversified debtor books. EST

Contract Frustration (CF)

Covers contract non-performance caused by government action: import/export prohibition, nationalisation of a buyer, government-imposed force majeure, contract cancellation by a state-owned entity. Waiting period typically 90 days. VER

Political Risk Credit (CR+PR)

Credit insurance extended to cover non-payment caused by a political peril — currency inconvertibility/transfer restriction (CI/TR), confiscation/expropriation, government-imposed moratorium. Bridge product between CR and PR codes. EST

Domestic Whole Turnover

WT policy covering sales to domestic buyers only. Premium rate lower than export; credit limit monitoring concentrated on domestic buyers. Important for companies selling goods on open account to retailers, distributors, and trade customers. VER

Export Whole Turnover

WT policy covering export sales — typically multi-country, with individual buyer credit limits monitored across the buyer's home market credit environment. ECGD (UKEF) and ECA cover competes here for government-supported export finance. VER

Bank Receivables Purchase / Supply Chain Finance

Credit insurance enabling banks to purchase trade receivables from sellers (receivables discounting / factoring) with insurance protecting the bank's credit risk. Growing intersection of credit insurance, fintech, and trade finance. EST

Structured Trade Credit (Medium-Term)

Covers medium-term (2–7 year) export credit for capital goods (machinery, power equipment, aircraft components). Often paired with ECA (UKEF/COFACE/HERMES) cover as a co-insurance or wrap structure. VER

ECA Wrap / Top-Up

Private market credit insurance providing coverage above or beyond what an ECA is willing to provide — higher risk buyers, non-qualifying markets, or coverage beyond ECA concentration limits. A niche but growing Lloyd's market product. EST
