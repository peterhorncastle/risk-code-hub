IBNR & Long-Tail Development Considerations
FactorImpact on Reserves
Obstetric cerebral palsy latencyCerebral palsy claims may not be brought until child reaches school age (5–7 years), meaning claims emerge 5–10 years post-birth. Obstetric IBNR loading of 80–120% of earned premium is typical for an active obstetric book.
COVID delayed-care claims tailClaims from delayed cancer diagnosis during COVID (2020–22) are crystallising 2023–2026 as harm becomes apparent and patients instruct solicitors. Prudent underwriters are loading 2020–2022 year IBNR significantly.
Montgomery consent claims emergencePost-2015 consent-based claims have a shorter latency (typically 2–4 years) but are rising in frequency. Annual consent claim development factor needs updating from pre-2015 actuarial data.
AI diagnostics uncertaintyNo actuarial history for AI-related diagnostic claims. Underwriters applying additional IBNR for risks with confirmed AI diagnostic tool deployment from 2022+.
Discount rate sensitivity (obstetrics)Large obstetric claims are capitalised at settlement using Ogden discount rates (currently −0.25% in England & Wales). Changes to the Ogden rate dramatically affect catastrophic claim values — a 1% shift in rate can change a £20m cerebral palsy claim by ±£3–4m.
