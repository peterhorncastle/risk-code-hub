Cat XoL is the most cyclical segment in reinsurance: rate-on-line rises after major losses destroy capital and falls when capital rebuilds — the pattern the Guy Carpenter Rate-on-Line indices have tracked since 1990. The Property-Cat Cycle in Four Phases

Hard · 2023

The generational reset

After Ian and years of loss creep, cat XoL rate-on-line jumped sharply, attachment points rose, and aggregate covers withdrew. Reinsurers reset the terms of trade.

Peak margin · 2024

Discipline holds, profits surge

Benign large-loss experience against reset attachments produced exceptional returns; capital rebuilt rapidly.

Softening · 2025–26

The weight of capital

Record capital and a 24%-larger cat-bond market softened January 2026 cat rate-on-line ~12% (retro −16.5%) — the largest decline since 2014, though attachments largely held.

Watch · forward

What turns it back

A single large cat, or an ILS pull-back, could reverse the softening quickly — the cycle's defining feature is how fast it turns.
