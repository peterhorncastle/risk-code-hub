Catastrophe XoL — Cat Layer Rater

Price a catastrophe layer from its modelled expected loss. Enter the layer, the cat-model AAL to it, and the loadings; the rater returns loss-on-line, rate-on-line, payback, premium and the margin over expected loss. Inputs update live — indicative for training.

Overview History Timeline Loss Database Underwriting Rates & ROE Risk Mitigation Global Programme References Rater
How it prices Expected loss to layer (the cat model's AAL to the layer) → loaded for risk → grossed up for expenses and brokerage = layer premium. Rate-on-line = premium ÷ limit; payback = 1 ÷ rate-on-line. The margin over expected loss is what the layer earns for capital and volatility.
