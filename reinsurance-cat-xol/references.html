Known Data Gaps

Exact broker index values GAP

Proprietary Rate-on-Line index levels sit with the brokers; the chart reconstructs the published shape, not exact values.

Private-treaty terms GAP

Attachment points, reinstatement terms and rate-on-line by cedant are confidential; benchmarks here are indicative.

Vendor-model loss costs GAP

Model AAL/PML by portfolio are licensed and confidential; the worked figures are illustrative.

Non-modelled & secondary-peril loss GAP

Demand surge, loss creep and some secondary perils are imperfectly captured by cat models.
